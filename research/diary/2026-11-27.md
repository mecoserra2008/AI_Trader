## 07:21 UTC [mistake] WS04: three design flaws found after the results (not changed, disclosed)
_ws04, mistake · H-WS04-5, H-WS04-7_

(1) Mincer-Zarnowitz in logs with a Wald test of (a,b)=(0,1) rejects for every spec because E[log RV] = log F + a Jensen term (a < 0 by construction); only the slopes b are informative.
