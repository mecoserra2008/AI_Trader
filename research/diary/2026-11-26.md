## 00:45 UTC [mistake] PART: two HMM inputs are price-level artefacts (relative spread, 5 bp near-touch band)
_participants, stationarity, mistake · H-PART3, H-PART4, H-PART5_

The flow-state HMM put 78% of HELD minutes in state 2 vs 25% on FIT. Cause: h_spread = ln spread in bp; for 1-tick markets the relative spread is tick / price, so it moved with the price level (BTC ~110k in 2025 vs ~62k in Nov 2026; median standardised h_spread +4.2 for BTC on HELD). h_cxl / h_refill use the 5 bp near-touch band, whose width in ticks also scales with price (BTC h_cxl +2.0). An ADF/KPSS + PSI check on the HMM inputs (house rule 1.3) would have flagged it; I did not run it before the fit. Fix for a follow-up (new id, not run): spread in ticks, the near-touch band in ticks, PSI check FIT vs HELD before fitting. The type-flow features (shares, signed shares) are scale-free and not affected; the H-PART3..6 verdicts do not hinge on the HMM block (its columns are a minority of P).
