## 22:54 UTC [observation] A6: rolling LLT MLE puts the slope variance at zero most months
_state_space, program-2026-11-10 · H-KF2_

In the first (unrecorded) run, 65% of the 1272 monthly 180-day LLT fits on 4h crypto log prices put q_hat = var(zeta)/var(eta) at the lower bound (exp(-16)); the 90th percentile is ~2e-5. This is the known pile-up of the MLE of a small state variance at zero (Shephard & Harvey 1990): at 4h, a 180-day window cannot distinguish a stochastic slope from a random walk.
