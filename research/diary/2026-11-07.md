## 17:09 UTC [mistake] Int8 overflow silently corrupted session labels
_calendar, bug_

Polars returns dt.hour() as Int8, so hour*60 wrapped (09:30 ET became minute 58). _weekend, methodology · H-W5_

## 22:53 UTC [mistake] H-VCL5 success criterion was unreachable
_methodology · H-VCL5_

I required median(realised/HAR forecast) > 1.1, but HAR's forecast is a lognormal MEAN (exp(mu + s2/2)); with right-skewed RV, the median ratio sits around 0.7 for ALL days. The test should compare compressed days against the all-days baseline (same metric), which the agent reported: -0.270 vs -0.280 mean log ratio, i.e. no difference. Conclusion stands (no compression effect), but the criterion was badly specified. Rule: ratio tests must be relative to the unconditional baseline of the same metric.
