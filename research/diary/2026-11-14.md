## 16:57 UTC [decision] OPX design frozen before evaluation: detector, contracts, pricing tiers, costs, grids, verdict rules
_options, compression, design · H-OPX1, H-OPX2, H-OPX3, H-OPX4_

Design frozen before any options result (agent OPX). Only data coverage has been looked at (Bybit BTCUSDT 1h from 2020-03-25; deribit_svi daily to 2026-10-09; DVOL hourly 2021-03-24..; option download in progress, 2020-01..2026-11, full history).

DATA. Deribit BTC option trades (history.deribit.com public API), aggregated per instrument-hour, block trades excluded (data/research/options/btc_YYYY-MM.parquet; src/ai_trader/research/options_download.py). Underlying = Bybit BTCUSDT 1h (lake), ETHUSDT for H-OPX3 robustness; Bybit funding (lake); smile summary deribit_svi; DVOL. Index S at an 08:00 fixing = index_price of the last option trade before 08:00 (hour 07:00 rows), fallback Bybit 08:00 open. Forward F = S exp(b T), b = iv_basis_ann_30 (last known), else 0.

DETECTOR (as registered). Evaluated at 08:01 UTC on day d: RV7 = sum of squared hourly log returns over the 168 bars ending 08:00; IV = iv_atm_30 of day d (known 08:01), or DVOL/100 (bar ending 08:00) where iv_atm_30 is missing (after 2026-10-09). Percentile = share of the trailing 365 daily values (d-364..d) <= today's, >= 300 values required. Trigger: RV pct <= 0.20 AND IV pct <= 1/3. Entry at day d+1 08:00. Declustered: no new entry within 14 days of the last one. Unconditional sample = every day from the first day the detector is defined to the last day with a full 14-day exit.

CONTRACTS. Expiry = the nearest expiry with >= 21 days to go among expiries traded in the 7 days before entry (point-in-time listing); strikes = strikes of that expiry traded in those 7 days. Straddle strike = listed strike nearest F. H-OPX2 legs: call with Black-76 delta nearest +0.25 and put with delta nearest -0.85, deltas from the model smile at entry.

PRICING (tiered, per leg per fixing). (1) real: the instrument's hourly row in 05:00-10:59 whose last trade is nearest 08:00; IV = its last trade iv (or implied from last_price x last_index if iv <= 0), repriced with Black-76 at the 08:00 F and T (IV transport, so the trade's index timing does not matter). (2) model: 3-point smile from ATM / RR25 / BF25 (sigma_25C = ATM + BF + RR/2, sigma_25P = ATM + BF - RR/2, pillar strikes by Black-76 delta inversion at the pillar vols), quadratic in log-moneyness k = ln K/F through the three pillars, linear extrapolation outside the 25-delta pillars with the quadratic's slope there, floor 0.5 ATM, cap 3 ATM. Tenor: ATM total variance linear in T between 7/30/90 days, RR and BF linear in T, flat outside. After 2026-10-09 ATM = DVOL/100 for every tenor, RR / BF = last known. Model error reported vs every real 08:00 quote with 7-120 days and |delta| 0.05-0.95 (IV points and % of price).

COSTS. Per option leg per transaction (entry, exit, each scale-out third) = max(half-spread, 0.0003 BTC + 0.0125% x 1 BTC notional) in BTC per option, x S in USD; half-spread = the row's median |price - mark| (real) or the trailing-90-day median of real quotes in the same |delta| bucket (<0.35, 0.35-0.65, >0.65) for model-priced legs. Settlement at expiry costs nothing. Perp / spot: 5 bps of notional per side; perp funding from Bybit BTCUSDT funding events in (entry, exit], long pays positive rates.
