Trailing exit (per symbol, per strategy, per k in {1.5, 2.5, 3.5}; no other k):
* sigma_t = sigma_risk_next at close t: the bias-corrected robust EWMA one-bar sigma forecast at the strategy's own bar size
  (1h or 4h), without the weekend shrink (the frame's stop/risk sigma); fallback sigma_next if null. Known at close t.
  Distance = k * sigma_t in log price, one-bar horizon (no sqrt(time-in-trade) scaling), re-read every bar.
* A trade starts at the close of the first bar where the (wrapped) signal is non-zero with a new sign; best_t = max close
  (long) / min close (short) over entry..t, closes only. At close t, if side * (log best_t - log close_t) > k * sigma_t
  (strictly), the wrapped signal is 0 at t: the position is closed at close_t (the same timing as any strategy exit),
  paying the usual cost.
* Re-entry: after a trailing exit of side d, the wrapped signal stays 0 while sign(raw signal) == d. The first bar where
  sign(raw) != d (raw flat or opposite) is the 'fresh signal change': from that bar on the wrapper follows the raw signal
  again (an opposite raw signal opens the opposite trade at that same close; a flat raw signal re-arms, and a later
  non-zero raw signal enters). Otherwise the wrapped signal equals the raw signal (the strategy's own exits still apply).
* Close-based (no intrabar stop orders): the rule uses only close_t and sigma_t.

Evaluation: daily pnl_d per symbol-date as in the panel; sleeve = strategy x universe (mean over symbols, T >= 3 symbols);
family book = equal-weight mean of the available trend sleeves (L and T) per date. Paired daily difference book_k - book_orig,
