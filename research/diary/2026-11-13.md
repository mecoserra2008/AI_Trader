## 00:31 UTC [decision] Execution pipeline (L): intent risk engine, PaperBook and atx exec-intents (paper/replay only)
_execution, risk, paper_

Built the Rust side of docs/EXECUTION_PIPELINE.md. at-risk::decide(intent, account, portfolio, rules, cfg) is a pure function. It sizes at fixed risk from entry/stop/equity with the fees.yaml round trip included, and checks every rule, reporting each violation by name: kill_switch, gate_blocked, daily_loss_limit, max_new_trades_per_day, max_open_positions, max_positions_per_cluster (family = cluster, R-PD-1), duplicate_symbol, max_gross_exposure, exceeds_max_leverage, stop_too_far, stop_inside_min_sigma / stop_beyond_max_sigma (|ln(entry/stop)|/sigma.per_bar, no weekday shrink, R-VOL-2), missing_sigma, net_er_below_min / net_er_missing (the engine checks the lower of its own recomputation and K net_er_r), below_min_qty, below_min_notional, invalid_intent, unknown_instrument, no_position_to_close, stale_intent and duplicate_intent. A gate reduce sizes at 0.5R. A flat intent closes only that strategy position and is never blocked.

at-exec PaperBook fills conservatively:
- market entries at the next open plus slippage (taker), cancelled if that open is already through the stop or target;
- limit entries only when the bar trades through them (maker), with a TTL;
- resting stops (R-EXEC-1) on the bar low/high, stop first when stop and target share a bar, gaps filled at the open;
- targets at the target price as taker;
- KILL cancels and flattens.

The new config/fees.yaml sets crypto maker 2 / taker 5.5 bp and TradFi maker 0 / taker 2.75 bp, with 2 bp slippage per market leg for both.

atx exec-intents runs replay or paper and refuses live mode and mainnet. Decision times are rounded up to the bar grid, so decisions are point in time. It writes data/exec/decisions/<run>.jsonl and data/exec/paper/<run>/ and journals the run.

Smoke test on the real lake with $100 equity: qty rounding leaves trades at about 0.5R, not 1R, and BTC/ETH fall below min_qty. Small accounts are lot-limited, which matters when reading paper R.
