# D7F: what the D7 verdict and stress fixes change (2026-11-28)

_Agent D7F, follow-up to decision D7 (user-approved 2026-11-27, "Lets go for the changes"). Research only. research/rules.yaml was not edited: the rule changes below are proposals for the orchestrator. New rows recorded: 12 (H-D7F-1..6, two segments each) and 33 (labelled STRESS re-run under the D7b harness). Earlier rows were not rewritten._

## In plain words

**Rules.** Of the 19 A/B rules that cite a holdout "supports" row that fails the new one-sided test, **2 should be downgraded (R-PX-8 and R-RB-3, B -> C)**, **4 should have their wording changed but keep their grade (R-EXEC-3, R-PX-3, R-STRESS-5, R-AVOID-1)**, and **13 stay as they are**. Most of the failing rows were the evidence *against* an avoid rule: vector candles, the weekend straddle, auction balance, squeeze breakouts, vol-surprise continuation and the trend-period classifiers had a holdout "win" that was only the right sign. Under the stricter test those wins become inconclusive, so the avoid rules get stronger, not weaker. Two of the 26 "failures" are mistakes in the A4 dry run's automatic reading, not real failures. H-TVTP1 passes (its "p = 0.5" came from reading the VAR lag order `"p": 1` as a p-value; the registered test gives min HAC t 2.89, one-sided p 0.002). H-WS07-1 also passes (its registered holdout check is the spec chosen in-sample, ghmm3, at t -2.31, p 0.010, not a Simes over all 13 specs). The two downgrades lost their only registered holdout support: regime episodes ageing out has a holdout one-sided p of 0.07, and the R9 -> R6 path shares were never tested against a registered null.

**Stress verdicts with the fixed harness (closed trades, bootstrap block max(5 d, p90 holding), funding in net R).** Three verdicts change:
- **zoo_ensemble goes from SURVIVES to FRAGILE.** It is +0.279 R per trade [95% CI -0.021, +0.697], one-sided P(mean <= 0) 0.037, and +0.079 R on 131 closed holdout trades. The ablation shows that funding moves the verdict: -0.037 R per trade for a long-biased trend book. Closing the open positions and the 32-day block only widen the CI.
- **geo_pivot_bo goes from SURVIVES to FRAGILE.** Its closed-trade holdout is -0.179 R on 71 trades (the old SURVIVES rested on 16 open positions marked at +45.7 R).
- **compression_breakout goes from FRAGILE to FAILS** (-0.919 R per closed trade). Its old positive mean came only from 17 positions still open at the data end.

That leaves **no unit that SURVIVES**. Every trend unit is FRAGILE, and the mean-reversion, calendar, shock-fade and setup-C units still FAIL. Three registered stress hypotheses change:
- H-STRESS1 goes from supports to inconclusive: only 4 of 9 trend units stay positive after fees x1.5 and +5 bp in both segments, because the geo_* closed-trade holdouts are negative.
- H-STRESS5 goes from inconclusive to contradicts: with every same-side position stopped together, the daily CVaR rises by 1.6-1.8x for compression_breakout, geo_pivot_bo and geo_flag_bo.
- H-STRESS10 goes from inconclusive to contradicts: both units that survive the in-sample screens (shock_regime, geo_pivot_bo) lose in the holdout.

The live-limit findings stand: the day stop is never hit, the worst day for tsmom is -7.20 USD and its P(3-month loss >= 50 USD) is 1.8%.

**"No effect" claims re-tested with an equivalence (non-superiority) test.** Six were registered (H-D7F-1..6) before anything was computed.
- The null is **established in the holdout** for two: no regime spec's direction term adds 0.005 nats or more to the next-day log score (H-WS07-5, IUT p 0.003), and crypto has no leverage asymmetry of 0.10 or more (H-LEV2, p 0.009).
- Some cells pass in **both segments**: floor pivots on universe L (H-BO1), and GARCH-family specs worse than the clock log-HAR at 1h and 4h (H-WS04-2).
- The null is **not established anywhere** for "alternative zoo weightings do not beat 1/N" (H-WS16-2). The CIs are two to four times wider than the 0.05 R margin, so this is absence of evidence.
- Market Cipher B's 4h dots are not shown to lose: 4h_mfon in-sample +0.04 R (t 0.3), 4h_mfoff holdout -0.16 R (t -0.8). The 1h dots lose clearly in both segments.

Three A rules should be restated on this basis. **R-AVOID-3** should split 1h (A) from 4h (B). **R-PX-10** stays A only for crypto: TradFi T_12h holdout is +0.074 sigma, SE 0.052. **R-VOL-7** is borderline: holdout and the log spec establish no leverage effect, but the in-sample level spec is too noisy (p 0.13).

## 5. Errors found in the A4 dry run's Part B extraction

## Caveats

- The equivalence re-tests are extra, labelled trials on estimates that were already recorded and visible when the margins were set. The margins come from stated external anchors, not from the numbers. While reading the rows I mentally estimated one outcome (H-WS16-2, zoo_iv) before registering; its margin is WS16's own pre-registered +0.05 R, so I did not choose it (diary).
- SEs are recovered from recorded t or CI values. H-WS07-5's means (5 decimals) and t (2 decimals) are rounded, so its SEs are approximate (to about 1%). H-WS04-2's 1d holdout EGARCH t of 74 looks degenerate, but it only strengthens the claim side.
- The stress re-run reuses the stored trade tables (as of 2026-11-25) and adds funding from the lake point-in-time at that date. Symbols without a funding table get 0 funding. The 2020-03 Deribit crisis window has no funding (the BTC-PERPETUAL table is not on Bybit). The perturbation (S5) and synthetic (S6) tables are the stored ones. S5 rows get funding, and the cliff rule is judged on closed trades.
- The H-STRESS0..10 re-run rows are recorded as "pooled" (H-STRESS10 as clean_holdout), like the originals. They are a re-run of the same frozen design under the approved D7b harness, not a new design.
- The A4 report's "26 fail" count includes the two extraction errors above. On re-reading, 24 genuinely fail the one-sided test.

## Method and reproduction

- Rules: each of the 19 rules' cited hypotheses were re-read: registration (statement, success rule) and the latest llm_in_sample / clean_holdout rows. The D7a judgement uses the registered statistic and level.
- Stress: `PYTHONIOENCODING=utf-8 uv run python -m ai_trader.research.d7f stress` (through the governor, heavy, 3000 MB). It calls `stress_report.analyse` with outputs redirected to data/research/d7f/stress, then runs the four-config ablation.
- Equivalence: `uv run python -m ai_trader.research.d7f tost [--record]` (src/ai_trader/research/d7f_tost.py; tests in tests/test_d7f.py). Registration: research/hypotheses_pending/D7F.yaml (appended under the registry lock before computing).
- Storage: data/research/d7f 1.4 MB (quota 200 MB). The stress re-run ran about 6 minutes in one heavy governor slot with no queue wait; the analyse step took 288 s.
