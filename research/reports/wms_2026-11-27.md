Agent WMS of program WMM2 (research/programs/2026-11-27_weekend_mm_news_program.md). Research only: public Bybit REST market data,
no orders, no private endpoints. Hypotheses H-WMS-1..13 were registered (research/hypotheses_pending/WMS.yaml, appended under the
registry lock at 22:06 UTC) and the design frozen in the diary ("WMS design frozen before results") before any index, outcome or fit
was computed. Code: src/ai_trader/research/wms_data.py (downloads), wms_select.py (indices and the frozen rule, tests in
tests/test_wms_select.py), wms_study.py (panel, BoS events, forward weekend, null simulations, bounce check), wms_analysis.py (tests),

## Verdicts in plain words

**Data.** I used 20,239 symbol-weekends: 286 FIT weekends (2021-01 to 2026-06, llm_in_sample) and 21 holdout weekends (2026-07-04 to
11-21, clean_holdout). They cover 572 Bybit USDT perps in the 5-50 M USD daily-turnover band, a median of 61 names per weekend.
188 of these perps are delisted today. Bybit's public kline REST still serves delisted perps, so most of the survivorship bias is gone
(see Caveats for what remains).

**1. A name's mean-reversion character persists, but mostly as microstructure (H-WMS-1 inconclusive, H-WMS-2 supports).**
- The registered composite S0 (15m-based VR, AR(1), DFA, ER, BoS imbalance) persists from the Friday window into the weekend in FIT:
  rank IC +0.093 (t 8.9, 251 weeks). In the holdout it does not: -0.033 (t -1.0).
- What persists strongly in both segments is the short-horizon, activity-type property:
  - 5m AR(1) / VR(2): FIT +0.156, holdout +0.100 (t 3.8).
  - Break-of-structure count: +0.25 / +0.28 (t 7.7).
  - These are tick size, bounce and how jumpy a name is, more than a regime.
- OU half-life, efficiency ratio and BoS imbalance do not persist at all (IC about 0).
- Typical alt in the band: 4h VR of about 0.86-0.89 (median log VR15(16) -0.116), i.e. these names mean-revert on average.

**2. The mean-reversion score picks slightly better weekends for a quoter; the effect is small (H-WMS-3 inconclusive, H-WMS-4 supports).**
- Registered S0 vs the weekend trend ratio (|sum r| / sqrt(RV), primary): IC FIT -0.022 (t -1.9, BH q 0.11, not significant), holdout
  -0.062 (t -3.3).
- S0 vs max excursion over forecast sigma: FIT -0.039 (q 0.009), holdout -0.100 (t -2.6). Round-trip ratio holdout +0.073 (t 3.3).
- Right direction everywhere, but the FIT primary misses its registered bar, so inconclusive.
- Single indices that pass FIT (BH) with the mean-reversion sign: vr5_12, vr5_48, vr15_16, ar1_15 (holdout ICs +0.02 to +0.07, same sign).
- The pre-registered fallback composite S1 = 7 FIT-passing indices (an extra trial chosen from 12):
  - IC with the trend ratio: FIT -0.041 (t -3.9), holdout -0.032 (t -1.3).
- **The rule in use (top third of S1, an extra labelled trial under H-WMS-5):**
  - Trend ratio vs the other two thirds: -0.033 (t -3.7) in FIT, -0.026 (t -1.3) in the holdout.
  - Round-trip ratio: +0.094 (t 3.5) / +0.128 (t 1.9).
  - The registered S0 rule: -0.011 (t -1.1) / -0.037 (t -1.9), inconclusive.
- Plain meaning:
  - A trend ratio of 0.68 on average becomes about 0.65 for the selected names (-5%). That is useful as a tilt, not a filter that removes trenders.
  - The holdout (21 weekends) cannot confirm it on its own: the MDE for an IC there is about 0.07.

**3. Weekend breaks of structure in these alts revert slightly; they do not start trends (H-WMS-6 contradicts).**
- After a 15m BoS (k = 3) on the weekend, the signed move is:
  - at 1 h: -0.026 sigma (t -3.4), -3 bp;
  - at 4 h: -0.026 sigma (t -3.4), -7 bp.
- Holdout at 4 h: -0.006. 217k FIT and 13k holdout events.
- BTC-hedged: also negative (-0.015 / -0.012 sigma).
- 1h BoS: nothing (|t| < 1.5).
- For a quoter: a weekend break is not a reason to pull quotes. Direction from it is worth less than a taker round trip (11 bp), which
  agrees with R-PX-9.
- BoS measures do not identify the weekend trenders either (AUC 0.51-0.52).

**4. No detectable weekend buyer bias in BTC (H-WMS-7 inconclusive).**
- Mean weekend log return: +0.10% in FIT (t 0.6, 52% of weekends up), +0.37% in the holdout (t 1.2).
- Weekend minus the same week's weekday rate: +0.03% (t 0.1).
- MDE about 0.5% per weekend: a bias smaller than that cannot be seen with 5 years of weekends.

**5. Alts bleed against BTC, but not more on weekends (H-WMS-8 inconclusive).**
- Equal-weight alt minus BTC log spread over the weekend:
  - mean -0.48% (t -2.0, just short of the registered -2), median -0.92% (t -4.3) in FIT;
  - holdout mean -0.95% (t -1.5), negative on 71% of weekends.
- The Tue-Thu 48 h window bleeds just as much (-0.53%): weekend minus weekday is +0.05% (t 0.1).
- So "altcoins suffer" is true, but as a constant drift, not as a weekend effect.

**6. A few names carry most of the weekend dispersion, and the big movers are skewed up (H-WMS-10 supports, H-WMS-9 inconclusive).**
- The top 5% of names by |demeaned spread| carry 58% (FIT) / 66% (holdout) of the cross-sectional variance (Gaussian: 28%).
- That concentration is not weekend-specific: Tue-Thu has 54% / 68%. Weekend minus weekday is +0.031 (t 2.7) in FIT, -0.019 in the holdout.
- Cross-sectional skew is +0.93 (t 6.8) in FIT and +0.12 in the holdout. Weekdays are similar (+0.88).
- The top movers go up 65% of the time in FIT (t 6.7 per weekend) and 56% in the holdout.
- This fits "most alts bleed, a few thrive". Survivorship inflates it only partly, since delisted names are included.

**7. The trenders are visible on Friday mostly as the already-volatile names (H-WMS-11 supports, but read the caveat).**
- The registered logistic ranks the top-5% movers with AUC 0.79 (leave-one-year-out) / 0.83 (holdout).
- But the Friday realised vol alone has AUC 0.78: big movers are the high-vol names.
- An extra, labelled vol-normalised label (top 5% of |spread| / forecast sigma) gives AUC 0.58 [0.55, 0.60] / 0.62 [0.55, 0.68]. It is
  driven by the 7-day spread vs BTC in sigma units (0.56 / 0.64) and vol level. Funding, OI change and BoS add little (0.48-0.53).
- For the quoter: drop the names that already moved most against BTC during the week, and size by sigma.

**8. A calm-weekend classifier helps in FIT, but the holdout cannot confirm it, and the full model is overconfident (H-WMS-12 inconclusive).**
- Target: BTC weekend RV below the median of the previous 52 weekends, with no 5-sigma 5m jump.
- Full 8-input logistic, leave-one-year-out:
  - Brier skill +0.16 vs climatology (DM t 2.6);
  - but calibration slope 0.35 (overconfident) and a log score worse than climatology.
- Persistence-only model (Friday 7-day RV vs its 52-weekend median): skill +0.18 (t 4.1), well calibrated. It is the better model.
- Holdout (21 weekends): skill -0.015.
- Holiday weekends (13 labelled in FIT, tiny): calm 77% vs 43% otherwise. All 4 past Thanksgiving weekends were calm (RV 0.22-0.91 x the median).
- The registered gate (full model, P >= 0.5) is kept as frozen. Read its probabilities as shrunk toward 0.45.

**9. Bid-ask bounce is much smaller than assumed (H-WMS-13 contradicts).**
- 1m last-trade AR(1) is -0.063 vs -0.057 on mids, a difference of -0.004 [-0.006, -0.001]. 49 alts, 74 symbol-days.
- The bias is real but negligible. Mean-reversion indices on 5m last-trade klines are not materially inflated.

**This weekend (2026-11-28/29; Friday-known at Fri 22:00 UTC): not a calm weekend, and the rule says do not quote.**
- BTC's 7-day realised variance is at the 97th percentile of the history of log(RV / 52-weekend median), at +2.24 in logs.
- DVOL is 52.0, at the 95th percentile of log(DVOL / 52-Friday median). BTC fell 1.7% in the last 24 h. The last day was calmer than
  the week (x2 -0.36).
- P(calm): 0.44 from the frozen full model, which only the Thanksgiving flag (+0.47 standardised) lifts from 0.10. The
  persistence-only model says 0.10.
- **The gate is closed: no name is eligible.**
- The top third by score (names that would be eligible if the gate were open), best first:
  - ASTER, GRAM, XPL, ICP, TRUMP, WLFI, LINK, USELESS, STRK, VVV;
  - then PENGU, BTW, H, ATOM, FARTCOIN, JUP, PONS, BNB, AVAX.
- The Thanksgiving prior (4 of 4 calm) pulls the other way, but the high-vol Friday dominates every model.
- So the user's call of a low-volatility weekend is not supported by the Friday-known data.
- If the user quotes anyway, use the eligible_name list and the unshrunk risk sigma (R-VOL-2).

**What to use / stop using.**
- Use the S1 score as a tilt toward quotable names, and the persistence-only vol state as the honest calm signal.
- Stop expecting weekend BoS to start trends, and stop expecting a weekend buyer bias.
- Treat alt bleed as constant drift, not a weekend effect.

## Dossiers: the Friday indices

Every index is computed on the Friday window FW (the 2016 5m bars opened in [Fri-7d 22:00, Fri 22:00) UTC; log last-trade closes;
gaps forward-filled after a >= 98% coverage check) and, for the persistence tests, on the weekend's 576 bars with the same code.
Sampling error comes from 1000 simulated 2016-bar windows (data/research/wms/nulls.json): (a) a random walk with Student-t(3.5) 5m
returns and sigma 25 bp (typical of a 5-50 M USD alt); (b) the same price seen through an 8 bp bid-ask bounce (Roll model); (c) an
OU component with a 2 h half-life plus a random-walk component (0.3 x).

The robust z* of the variance ratios has mean 0.00-0.02 and sd 1.00-1.05 under the simulated random walk, so the implementation is
calibrated (a unit test caught a missing sqrt(n) in z* before any data were touched).

**Variance ratio (Lo-MacKinlay 1988).** VR(q) = Var(q-bar return) / (q x Var(1-bar return)), with overlapping q-sums and the unbiased
variance estimators. VR(q) - 1 is about 2 x the weighted sum of the first q - 1 autocorrelations (weights 1 - j/q). Below 1 means
mean reversion over the q-bar horizon; above 1 means trending.
- Assumptions: increments with a constant mean inside the window. z* allows conditional heteroskedasticity (martingale-difference
  null), but not a level shift or a break inside the window.
- Bias: on 5m base returns an 8 bp bounce pulls VR down by 0.025 (q 2) to 0.058 (q 48), the size of a real effect. That is why the
  composite uses the 15m base (bias 0.018 at q 4, 0.026 at q 16).
- Sampling error is large: one 7-day window measures VR15(4) to +-0.07 and VR15(16) to +-0.17.

**AR(1)** of returns: OLS with Kendall's correction rho + (1 + 3 rho) / n (+0.0005 at 5m, +0.0015 at 15m).
- It carries the same information as VR(2) (VR(2) = 1 + rho).
- Bounce bias: -0.025 at 5m and -0.011 at 15m (simulation). Sampling sd: 0.022 at 5m, 0.038 at 15m.

**OU half-life** of the 15m log price around its window mean: Dickey-Fuller regression dp_t = a + b p_{t-1}, half-life
-ln 2 / ln(1 + b), capped at 1680 h, analysed in logs.
- It assumes a stationary OU inside 7 days.
- On a random walk the DF bias makes b negative, so a random walk gets a finite half-life: mean 120 h, sd 342 h.
- So it separates nothing slower than a few days from a random walk. The 2 h OU reads 4.0 +- 2.0 h because of its random-walk part.

**DFA-1 exponent** of 5m returns, scales 12-288 bars (1 h to 1 day; 12-96 on the weekend).
- alpha < 0.5 is anti-persistent, alpha > 0.5 persistent.
- It is robust to slow linear trends inside boxes, but not to intraday seasonality in volatility.
- Random walk: 0.499 +- 0.036. Bounce effect: -0.005.

**Kaufman efficiency ratio** on 1h closes: |p_end - p_start| / sum |dp| over 168 hours.
- Under a random walk it reads 0.081 +- 0.058, so a single 7-day ER is mostly noise (its sd is 70% of its mean).
- Its rank says more about the week's net move than about the name.

**Break of structure (15m bars, k = 3).**
- A swing high (low) at bar i: its high (low) is strictly above (below) the 3 bars on each side. It is confirmed at the close of bar
  i + 3 and usable from bar i + 4.
- A bullish (bearish) BoS: a close above (below) the most recent confirmed, unbroken swing high (low). A newer confirmation
  replaces the active level, and a break consumes it.
- bos_count = breaks in the window; bos_imb = |bull - bear| / (bull + bear). The 1h variant uses the same rule on 1h bars.
- Random walk: 54 +- 4 breaks per 7 days, imbalance 0.106 +- 0.078. A 2 h OU makes more breaks (57) with less imbalance (0.065).

**Bid-ask bounce at 1m (H-WMS-13).** Lake flow_3s carries both the last trade and the mid at each 3 s candle close, so both can be
compared on the same minutes. Sample: 74 complete symbol-days (49 linear alts in the 5-50 M band, November 2026, no trade_deficit
hours).
- The 1m AR(1) is -0.063 on last trades and -0.057 on mids. The difference is -0.004 [symbol bootstrap 95% -0.006, -0.001].
- At 5m: +0.044 vs +0.049, difference -0.004 [-0.006, -0.002].
- **H-WMS-13 contradicts:** the bounce effect is real but ten times smaller than the registered 0.02. Most of the 1m reversal is in
  the mid itself, and 5m returns of these alts are positively autocorrelated in this sample.
- On last-trade 5m klines, bounce therefore moves AR(1) and VR by well under the sampling error of one window. The 15m base of S0 is
  conservative, not necessary.

## Caveats

- **Survivorship, what remains.**
  - Delisted perps are in the sample (188 of 572 names), but only those with symbolType '' in today's listing. Delisted
    innovation-zone perps (96) are out, and innovation / main status is today's, not point-in-time.
  - Names Bybit no longer serves are missing. The remaining bias favours names that survived long enough to reach the band; it most
    likely inflates the upside of the "few thrive" tail.
- **No historical spread or depth.** The universe uses turnover only. The live select_universe.py filters (spread >= 4 bp,
  depth >= 50 k, |r1m| <= 3 x spread) apply forward only.
- **Bars are REST backfills:** the vintage served on 2026-11-27, last-trade prices. Missing 5m bars are forward-filled after a 98%
  coverage check (15 symbol-weekends dropped).
- **Outcomes are bar-level proxies, not replayed P&L.** They are path efficiency and excursion, which matter for inventory risk. They
  do not capture fill-level adverse selection, which dominated MMW (R-MMW-1). WMQ's replay is the test that counts.
- **Small holdout.** 21 weekends (median 57 names each); the IC MDE is about 0.07 and the rule-difference MDE about 0.06. FIT has 251
  weeks with >= 10 names (2021 has few names).
- **Weekly series.**
  - BTC weekend returns and the IC series are stationary (ADF p < 0.01, KPSS p >= 0.1) and not autocorrelated in levels (LB(4)
    p 0.47-0.68), but autocorrelated in squares (p 0.002-0.01). NW(4) handles that.
  - BTC weekend log RV is persistent (KPSS p 0.01), which is why the calm label uses a trailing 52-weekend median.
- **Extra trials.**
  - S1 was selected from 12 indices on FIT (pre-registered fallback, labelled).
  - The vol-normalised trender label and the alternative calm models (persistence-only, no holiday flag) were run after the results
    (data/research/wms/extra.json). They are reported, not used by the rule.
- **The holiday effect rests on 13 labelled weekends** (4 Thanksgiving, 4 Christmas, 5 New Year). Its +0.47 coefficient is what lifts
  this weekend's P(calm) from 0.10 to 0.44.

## Method

- **Data.**
  - Bybit public REST, category linear:
    - 1d klines of 617 candidates (397 trading, 220 delisted);
    - 5m klines (high, low, close, turnover; float32) for every symbol-week in the band, plus BTCUSDT 2020-12 .. now;
    - funding history and 1d OI over the same ranges.
  - Lake: BTC DVOL (alt_series deribit dvol_close, known_ts <= T_F) and the macro events table (data/research/news/events).
  - Download through the governor at about 6 requests/s per thread (2-12 threads), resumable, with backoff on 10006 / 10016 / 10018.
    Raw files 0 (written directly as compact parquet).
- **Point in time.**
  - Friday cutoff Fri 22:00 UTC. 5m bars closed <= T_F; daily turnover from complete days Fri-7 .. Thu.
  - Funding settled <= T_F. 1d OI with ts + 1 d <= T_F.
  - DVOL known <= T_F (the 20:00 hourly close), and its 52-Friday median from earlier Fridays.
  - The calm label's median uses only earlier weekends.
  - The forward weekend was fetched with end = T_F (data/research/wms/forward/).
- **Inference.**
  - Per-weekend Spearman ICs (>= 10 names), mean over weeks with NW(4) t. Panels with weekend fixed effects and weekend-clustered CR1 SEs.
  - BH within sub-families. Weekend block bootstrap (4-week blocks) for AUCs.
  - Diebold-Mariano with HAC and the HLN correction for Brier scores. Leave-one-year-out CV for the classifiers.
- **Weekend vol forecast for O1 / O3.** Panel log-HAR fitted on FIT: log RV_W = -1.17 + 0.488 log RV_d1 + 0.248 log RV_FW
  + 0.101 log RV_prevW (R2 0.59; QLIKE 0.320 vs 0.357 for the scaled 7-day RV), with a lognormal back-transform.
- **Runtime.** About 2 h of downloads (daily 5 min, 5m 52 min, funding / OI 37 min, in parallel), features 167 s, analysis 37-61 s,
  null simulations about 5 min.
- **Storage.** data/research/wms 0 -> 589 MB (bars5m 531 MB, daily 16 MB, features / panel / selection / events about 40 MB); quota 1 GB.
  data/raw/WMS empty.
