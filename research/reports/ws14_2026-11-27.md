## 1. Verdicts in plain words

**What the live 1h network computes.** live_nn.v1 and v2.1 run the same 2-seed polynomial-edge network; v2.1 only
changes the conformal layer. The network forecasts next-hour volatility *relative to the base variance s2*. In those
units:

- 78% of the variance of its log sigma is the hour-of-week clock. Hour-of-week dummies give R^2 0.785, and log sigma
  correlates 0.87 with lfac.
- The rest is a HAR-like pull toward the weekly and monthly levels (har_w, har_m), plus the last 6 h and 24 h of squared
  returns (lu_m6, lu_m24) and the last bar (lu_1).
- Its linear distillation on the live refit is log sigma ≈ −0.05 + 0.59 lfac + 0.12 har_w + 0.10 lu_m6 + 0.10 lu_m24
  + 0.06 har_m + 0.04 dow_sin + 0.02 lu_1 − 0.06 jump24 + …, with lsig2 at −0.002. So raw sigma scales one-for-one with
  the base vol, and the clock enters as variance ∝ fac^1.2, slightly more than a pure multiplicative profile.
- The latent units carry vol relative to the base, not the absolute level. A probe on the latent recovers lfac at R^2
  0.69 / 0.73 (selectivity +0.42 / +0.45 over random networks), but the absolute weekly RV level only at R^2 0.08 / −1.6.
- It does not encode funding (probe R^2 < 0, no better than the inputs).
- It has no input for scheduled releases.

That reading is sensible for a vol model. It is also what the brain atlas described, now checked out of sample with
valid controls.

**Is the 1h sigma a scaled HAR plus an hour-of-week term? No (H-WS14-1 contradicts).**

- The 6-coefficient HAR + clock surrogate reproduces the network's log sigma with out-of-sample R^2 0.707 [0.692, 0.719]
  in-sample, 0.790 [0.766, 0.809] in the holdout and 0.745 on the 623 live hours of 2026-11.
- In raw units the same surrogate scores 0.93 / 0.89, but only because network and surrogate share the base scale.
- Adding recent |returns| or using all 19 inputs linearly gets to 0.85-0.91.

**Could a simple surrogate replace it? Not yet shown; probably partly.**

- An additive-spline model (one smooth curve per input, a GAM) reproduces the network at R^2 0.968 / 0.971, so the
  network is essentially a GAM.
- But in the holdout the GAM keeps only 69% of the network's pinball gain over log-HAR-t: +0.00039 worse than the net,
  t 2.3. The linear 19-input surrogate keeps 40% (+0.00074, t 2.6). In-sample both are at least as good as the net.
- The lost part is the width, not the median (labelled extra, decided after seeing H-WS14-2/11). Surrogates reproduce
  q50 poorly (R^2 0.1-0.5), but that costs nothing: the network's sigma with the GAM's median and shape scores −0.00005
  (t −0.4) against the network in the holdout. The GAM's sigma with the network's median and shape loses +0.00049
  (t 2.8). The 3% of log-sigma variance the additive model misses is non-additive (interactions), and that is what pays
  out of sample. In-sample the GAM's median / shape even beat the network's (−0.00018, t −3.6).
- H-WS14-2 and H-WS14-11 are therefore inconclusive. The holdout (135 days) cannot resolve a margin of 0.0003: the
  minimum detectable effect is 0.0004-0.0007.
- Keep the network; do not swap in a linear formula. The GAM is a cheap shadow candidate, as a new registered trial.

**Does sigma rise monotonically with recent |returns|? Mostly, but with two wrong-signed kinks (H-WS14-4 contradicts).**

- Overall the response has the right shape. Sigma rises 2.2-2.3x as |r_{t-1}| goes from 0 to 8 sigma, close to the
  empirical ratio of realised rms (2.0x from the bottom bin to 3-4 sigma).
- Kink 1, below 0.25 sigma: lu_1 is log(max(r^2/s2, 1e-4)), so a near-zero bar is an extreme input. The network
  extrapolates and sigma dips about 1.4% (median).
- Kink 2, at the 4-sigma jump threshold: once the last bar crosses 4 sigma, jump24 / since_jump switch on and sigma
  *falls* in 10-16% of rows, by up to 5.9%. A 4.25-sigma bar should not predict less vol than a 3.9-sigma bar.
- 41-42% of rows have some drop larger than 2%. The drops are small in size but wrong in sign, and they sit exactly
  where stops get sized after a big bar.

**Price level: invariant for all four networks (H-WS14-5 supports).** Scaling OHLC by 1e-3 or 1e3 through the
production feature pipelines (with the xs_v4 liquidity mask on unscaled turnover) leaves only float rounding:

- Features change by at most 4e-10 in float64 and 6e-8 in float32 storage.
- PNN quantiles change by at most 2.8e-8 of the band width.
- xs_v3 vol / rel outputs change by at most 5e-8; xs_v4 outputs (frozen weights, 6 symbols, 720 h) are bitwise equal.

**Where it fails: scheduled releases, not volatility regimes (H-WS14-8 inconclusive by rule: release half confirmed,
regime half refuted).**

- At CPI / NFP / FOMC hours the raw 80% band covers 45% of outcomes (n 171, binomial p ≈ 0). The ACI band (46%) and
  log-HAR-t (46%) are no better. Realised rms is 2.8 against a network sigma of 0.95 in model units, about 3x too narrow.
- The hour after a release covers 62%.
- In every BTC v0 vol tier, coverage is 0.79-0.82, and the gain over log-HAR-t holds in high-vol cells (t −3.6
  in-sample, −2.4 holdout).

**What the skill rests on (H-WS14-7 inconclusive: clock first holds, the ordering below it does not).** Out-of-sample
resample ablation over 65 refits, pinball increase (t):

| group | llm_in_sample | clean_holdout |
|---|---|---|
| clock (lfac, dow) | +0.0051 (22) | +0.0111 (14) |
| daily HAR | +0.0018 (22) | +0.0028 (9) |
| recent returns | +0.0021 (18) | +0.0020 (5) |
| jump terms | tiny but t 2.9 / 2.3 | |
| base sigma, funding-hour flags | nil | nil |

**Attributions can be trusted (H-WS14-9 supports).**

- Integrated gradients are complete (error 3e-5).
- They change under full weight randomisation (median per-row Spearman 0.43; global ranking −0.01) and under label
  randomisation (0.48; global 0.27).
- They agree with the ablation ranking (Spearman 0.87).
- Top inputs: lfac, dow_cos, har_w, lu_1, lu_m6, har_m.
- Caveat: |IG| still correlates 0.61 with |input|, so read single-row maps loosely.

**Where the shock lives (activation patching, descriptive).** Over 671 matched calm/shock pairs (same hour of week):

- Two latent units per seed restore 105% of the log-sigma gap. Unit z0 of each seed restores 42-47% alone; the null
  patch restores less than 1%.
- On the input side, the recent-returns group carries all of it (120%); the clock and the base sigma carry about 0.

**24h network: not HAR + weekend clock (H-WS14-3 contradicts: R^2 0.72 in-sample, 0.89 holdout).** It does not beat
log-HAR-t either (R-PNN-2), so keep log-HAR-t at 24h.

**v2.1.** It does not change what the network computes:

- The issued 80% band is the network band times a median 1.11 (p10 0.90, p90 1.43) through ACI (alpha_t 0.07-0.26).
- The jump-cluster floor binds on 12% of lines, and live coverage is 0.80 over 116 non-replay lines.
- Nothing in v2.1 knows about releases, so the release failure applies to v2.1 as well.

**xs_v3 / xs_v4 (H-WS14-10 contradicts on its vol half; the rel half holds).**

- **Vol heads.** They are not a linear function of their inputs. The 1h log q50 surrogate on the 23 hourly-clock
  features scores R^2 0.62 (v3 2026Q4), 0.59 (v3 2025Q2) and 0.68 (v4); all 38 features only reach 0.66-0.72.
- **The rel ranking is vol drag.** The 24h relative-median ranking is reproduced by a one-parameter surrogate,
  −b·24·s2. Mean per-hour Spearman with the network is 0.86 / 0.87 (v3) and 0.80 (v4), with b = −0.46 / −0.56 (v3),
  i.e. the lognormal −sigma²/2 drag of median log returns, and −0.25 (v4).
- That surrogate earns almost all of the network's rank IC: 0.130 vs 0.148 (v3 holdout, difference t 1.15) and 0.110 vs
  0.113 (v4, t 0.14).
- The "skill" is the arithmetic of medians of log returns, not alpha. This is the mechanism behind R-XS-2 / R-XS-3.

**What to use, what to stop.**

1. Widen any 1h band or stop at scheduled release hours: the network and HAR-t are about 3x too narrow there. This is
   the single largest failure found; see rule candidate R-WS14-1.
2. After a bar beyond 4 sigma, do not rely on the network's sigma alone. Take the max of the network, HAR-t and the
   field (v2.1's floor already does this inside jump clusters). The fix belongs in a new network version: drop
   jump24 / since_jump, or make them monotone, as a registered trial.
3. Never read the xs rel ranking as a relative-value signal: it is −sigma²/2.
4. The 1h PNN stays; a linear formula is not a replacement. The GAM is worth a shadow test.
5. For WS12's candidates: their extra skill (if WS12 confirms one) comes from intraday high-low ranges over the past
   day. A cheaper test of that mechanism is a range-based RV input (a Parkinson HAR term) in the PNN or in log-HAR-t,
   registered as a new trial.

## 2. Dossiers

### A. PNN 1h sigma (live_nn.v1 / v2.1 next-hour band)

1. **Decision link.** It sets next-hour BTC bands, touch probabilities and stop context (R-PNN-1), and the v2.1 issued
   bands.
2. **Properties of the network output** (live refit, 8,711 h, 2025-11-14..2026-11-12):
   - Distribution: log sigma in model units has mean −0.22, sd 0.35, skew 0.28, excess kurtosis −0.12.
   - Stationarity: ADF p 2e-30, KPSS p ≥ 0.10, so stationary.
   - Dependence: ACF 0.89 at lag 1, 0.74 at lag 2, 0.30 at lag 24.
   - Clock: hour-of-week dummies give R^2 0.785, hour-of-day 0.24.
   - Live inputs: the lake rebuild equals the study cache (287 h) and the live v1 journal inputs (153 lines) exactly.
3. **Candidate specifications** (assumptions: additive or linear in standardised inputs; stationary mapping within a
   refit):
   - (i) HAR + clock: lsig2, lfac, har_d/w/m;
   - (ii) (i) + recent |returns|: lu_*;
   - (iii) linear on all 19 inputs;
   - (iv) additive cubic splines on all 19 (GAM; extra trial).
4. **Checks.** The checkpoints reproduce the stored walk-forward quantiles exactly (max error 1e-7). Surrogates miss most of
   q50 / shape (R^2 0.1-0.5), but the pinball loss sits in the non-additive part of the width (GAM decomposition,
   `gam_decomposition.json`).
5. **Out-of-sample comparison.** 65 monthly refits, each surrogate fitted on that refit's fit block, scored on its test
   month; pinball per row over 7 taus in model units; per-date paired differences, NW mean and SE.

   | surrogate | R^2 log sigma, in / hold [CI hold] | pinball vs PNN, in / hold (t) | share of PNN gain kept, in / hold |
   |---|---|---|---|
   | HAR + clock | 0.707 / 0.790 [0.766, 0.809] | +0.00088 (7.6) / +0.00120 (2.9) | −7% / 4% |
   | + recent \|r\| | 0.852 / 0.901 | +0.00008 (1.0) / +0.00089 (3.0) | 90% / 28% |
   | linear 19 | 0.876 / 0.913 [0.892, 0.928] | +0.00004 (0.6) / +0.00074 (2.6) | 95% / 40% |
   | GAM 19 (extra) | 0.968 / 0.971 [0.968, 0.975] | −0.00018 (−2.9) / +0.00039 (2.3) | 122% / 69% |

   The PNN − log-HAR-t gain is 0.00082 (t 7.3) in-sample and 0.00124 (t 3.0) in the holdout.
6. **How the conclusions differ.** In-sample every surrogate beyond HAR + clock looks as good as the network; in the
   holdout all of them lose ground. The decision ("is a formula enough?") flips with the segment, so it should not be
   made on in-sample fidelity.
7. **Power.** Holdout SE of the paired difference is 0.00017-0.00030, so the minimum detectable effect (80% power,
   one-sided 5%) is 0.0004-0.0007, larger than the 0.0003 margin. The non-inferiority verdicts are underpowered by
   design and need a longer forward window.

### B. PNN 24h sigma

1. **Decision link.** The 24h band (context; R-PNN-2 already prefers log-HAR-t).
2. **Properties.** log sigma has a strong weekly cycle (ACF at lag 7 0.88). ADF p 0.095 and KPSS p 0.01: the series is
   not stationary at the daily step because of the weekend clock.
3. **Specifications.** HAR + weekend clock (log_sigma, lrv_d/w/m/q, next_wk_hours, dow), and linear on all 14 inputs.
4. **Checks.** Reproduction error 2e-7.
5. **Out-of-sample R^2.** HAR + weekend 0.719 [0.682, 0.751] in-sample, 0.886 [0.857, 0.907] holdout; all-14 linear
   0.823 / 0.931. On pinball the HAR + weekend surrogate is better than the network in-sample (−0.0032, t −3.6),
   because the network itself loses to log-HAR-t there (0.0024 worse).
6. **How the conclusions differ.** Whichever spec is used, 24h stays a log-HAR-t job.
7. **Power.** 133 holdout days; SE 0.002 on pinball.

### C. xs_v3 / xs_v4 vol heads (1h / 4h / 24h median |r| per token)

- Pooled linear surrogates fitted on 5,000 fit-block hours, scored on the test quarter.
- 1h log q50 R^2: 0.48 / 0.62 / 0.68 for HAR + clock / 23 hourly / all 38 (v3 2026Q4); 0.47 / 0.59 / 0.66 (v3 2025Q2);
  0.55 / 0.68 / 0.72 (v4 2026Q4). 4h and 24h are similar (0.53-0.74).
- These heads are far more nonlinear than the BTC PNN. That is plausible, given 8 latents plus attention plus a
  27-output head shared across 23-45 tokens.
- Not done: a GAM fidelity run. A real "replace the vol head" test would need a GAM arm with pinball scoring.

### D. xs_v3 / xs_v4 24h relative-return ranking

| refit | Spearman net vs −b·24·s2 | b | IC net | IC surrogate | net − surrogate (t) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| v3 2025Q2 (llm_in_sample) | 0.874 | −0.56 | 0.057 | 0.035 | 0.021 (1.6) |

- Origins are 45-91 days: the IC differences cannot be resolved below about 0.03-0.04.

### E. WS12 deep models (GRU / TCN, seed-averaged chosen lr 1e-3)

1. **Decision link.** Candidates to replace the PNN as the 1h / 4h / 24h vol forecaster (WS12's benchmark).
2. **Data and protocol.** Refit R1 has 52,560 test rows (2025-07..2026-06, llm_in_sample); R2 has 21,168
   (2026-07..2026-11-24, clean holdout); 6 assets. Surrogates are fitted on 20,000 fit-block rows to each network's own
   logF.
3. **1h logF fidelity (H-WS14-12 contradicts).**
   - Linear [lsig_rel1y, har_d/w/m, lfacn, lu lags 1-6] gives R^2 GRU 0.733 [0.717, 0.743] / 0.716 [0.679, 0.745] and
     TCN 0.723 / 0.655 (R1 / R2).
   - The additive spline on the same inputs reaches only 0.71-0.77.
   - These nets are less additive than the PNN (0.97), and they use the window beyond these summaries.
   - The clock enters one-for-one (lfacn coefficient 0.96-1.02).
4. **What they read instead** (descriptive extra, declared before running; 32-step IG of the R2 seed-0 nets on 3,000 R2
   test rows; completeness error ≤ 1.5e-4):
   - The window carries 86-88% of the attribution and the static HAR / clock inputs 12-14%.
   - The top window input is the high-low range (lrange, Parkinson): 35% (GRU) and 26% (TCN) of window attribution,
     against 9-11% for random-initialised nets of the same architecture.
   - The lags spread over about a day: lags 1-6 carry 45% / 48% and lags 1-24 84% / 82%. A random GRU puts 95% in
     lags 1-6, a random TCN 76%.
   - So the deep nets have learned a range-based realised variance with a decaying kernel over roughly 24 h, which the
     PNN (close-to-close lu only) and the HAR surrogates cannot see. This is the mechanism behind their departure from
     HAR.
   - Caveat: the rank correlation of trained vs random lag profiles is 1.0, because both decay monotonically. The
     sanity check that matters is the size of the shares, not their order.
5. **24h logF (H-WS14-13 inconclusive).** On [har_w, lfac_n24, lsig_rel1y] the surrogate scores 0.833 / 0.757 (GRU)
   and 0.830 / 0.721 (TCN). The calendar coefficient (lfac_n24 1.65-1.80) dwarfs har_w (0.07-0.12): the 24h head is
   mostly the hour-of-week profile, as WS12 suspected.
6. **Student-t df.** It moves against the variance forecast: corr(df, logF) is −0.94 to −0.98 at 1h, so tails are
   fatter when vol is high. That is sensible, and it is what a fixed-df log-HAR-t cannot do.
7. **Power.** The bootstrap CIs (±0.02-0.04) sit far from the 0.8 / 0.9 thresholds. The 1h verdict is not a power
   problem.

## 4. Caveats and what went wrong

- **Surrogates imitate the network; they are not data-fitted models.** Fidelity R^2 is pooled across refits, so part of
  it is across-refit level shifts. Per-refit medians are reported in the JSON and are close to the pooled values.
- **Monotonicity is ceteris paribus.** s2 is held fixed and lu_m6 / lu_m24 / jump flags are updated; rows whose last
  bar was itself a jump (29) are excluded. The real pipeline would also move s2 slightly.
- **Mistake (logged): the block-shuffled control.** It used 168-row blocks, which keep the hour-of-week phase, so the
  "shuffled" lfac stayed decodable (R^2 0.16). Rerun with 101-h blocks and a full permutation, it gives R^2 ≈ 0. The
  H-WS14-6 verdict is unchanged.
- **Mistake (fixed): the invariance metric.** My JSON writer first rounded to 7 decimals, and the per-quantile relative
  measure is ill-conditioned at q50 ≈ 0 (it showed 2e-5). The band-width-normalised measure (2.8e-8) and the raw
  feature differences (4e-10) are the valid ones.
- **H-WS14-11 is a labelled extra trial.** It was registered after the running job had printed llm_in_sample GAM
  fidelity for refits 2021-07..2022-04, so its in-sample row is contaminated. The holdout was unseen at registration.
- **Activation patching was not in the frozen design text.** It was declared in the diary before running and is
  descriptive only.
- **IG check set.** It used one 2,000-row set spanning both segments. The label-permuted retrains stopped at epochs
  5 / 1, near the initial weights, which is what data randomisation should give.
- **Release calendar.** It lists scheduled times only (no surprise size); 171 hours over 5 years.
- **xs work is thin.** Three refits only (v3 2025Q2, v3 2026Q4, v4 2026Q4), surrogates fitted on 5,000 sampled fit hours.
- **Not done (credits):** v4 attention / probe work, a GAM arm for the xs vol heads, unit probes for xs, flow probes
  (flow_bars only start 2026-05, so there is no fit-block history), monotonicity / patching of the WS12 nets, and IG
  data randomisation for the WS12 nets (model randomisation only).
- **API session limit.** The session limit interrupted work from about 00:20 to 07:12 UTC; nothing was lost (the
  partial was committed at a25eedb).

## 5. Method, data, compute

- **Data.** The PNN study caches (`data/models/pnn/cache`, `wf_1h` / `wf_1d` checkpoints, `latent_1h.parquet`), plus
  live BTC 1h rows rebuilt from the lake point in time (`live.nn.hourly_frame`, as of 2026-11-26 23:05 UTC). The xs_v3
  panel cache, the xs_v4 panel and wf checkpoints, the frozen v4 weights, the macro events calendar, and the live_nn /
  live_nn_v2 journals (read only).
- **Probes.** Ridge with alpha by 5-fold blocked CV on 20,000 fit-block rows, scored on validation + test rows. Baseline:
  10 pairs of random-initialised PNNs. Control task: a random value per hour of week. Input ceiling: linear on the 19
  standardised inputs.
- **Ablations.** Resample (rows permuted within the test month, 5 draws), pinball increase, per-date mean, NW t.
- **Integrated gradients.** 64-step midpoint, baseline = training mean, target = log sigma of the 2-seed ensemble.
- **Compute.** Every job ran through the governor (heavy 1,400-2,500 MB, light 800-900 MB, 2 threads). Runtimes: core
  42 s, probe 116 s, xs 28 s, failure < 30 s. Governor waits for RAM were 5-25 min (free RAM sat at the 1.5 GB headroom).
- **Storage.** data/research/ws14 grew from 0 to about 0.3 MB (JSON only; no copies of weights were needed, since the
  checkpoints are loaded read-only).
