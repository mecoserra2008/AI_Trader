## Verdicts in plain words

Held = HOLD, 21 weekends 2026-07-04 .. 11-22, 12 illiquid alt perps, 504 symbol-days, all after the knowledge cutoff
(clean_holdout). USD per symbol-day at the venue minimum lot (~5 USD per order) unless stated; every number is net of the
2 bp maker fee and of adverse selection; primary cell = pro-rata queue, 150 ms latency (all four fill-model cells agree).

1. **No principled quoting scheme earns a positive held net.** The scheme chosen on SEL (H-WMQ-10: D_xstar, symmetric
   quotes at each symbol's CAL probe-optimal depth, 0.5-1.5 x the 1h sigma, 30-130 bp from the mid) made **+0.0002 USD
   per symbol-day [-0.015, +0.014]** with 2.5 fills per symbol-day: spread captured +0.021, adverse selection at 60 s
   -0.015, inventory -0.003, fees -0.002. Paired against B0 it is **+0.013 [-0.010, +0.035]** in the primary cell (+0.011
   to +0.014 in all four, every CI straddles 0). **Inconclusive** (H-WMQ-10, H-WMQ-6).
2. **The live width B0 lost on these 21 weekends: -0.0125 [-0.028, +0.002]** (FIFO-pessimistic 150 ms: CI below 0); it
   was +0.01 on the 4 weekends of H-MMW. "B0 is about zero" stands, with a slight negative lean on the longer sample.
3. **GLFT and Avellaneda-Stoikov, calibrated the textbook way, lose clearly (H-WMQ-3 contradicts).** The whole-curve
   exponential intensity fit is dominated by the touch (k = 12-17 per sigma), so the closed-form quotes sit ~0.1 sigma
   from the mid. The SEL choice A_as_s1_adj lost **-0.29 USD per symbol-day [-0.31, -0.26]** with 256 fills: spread
   +0.38, adverse selection at 1 s -0.46, fees -0.20, the touch result of H-MMW4 again. Refitting k on the legs where
   the quotes would sit (extra trial A2, k = 2-4) gives zero: -0.0000 [-0.016, +0.015] (H-WMQ-13 inconclusive).
4. **Fill intensity is not exponential in depth (H-WMQ-2 contradicts).** A power law beats it out of sample by 4,300
   log-likelihood units per held day (CI -5,500 to -3,400); measuring depth in sigma units beats bp by 15,000 per day
   (the sigma scaling is right); k is stable (within 30% of CAL for 11 of 12 names) but it is the slope of a mis-specified
   curve. The GLFT / AS closed forms should not be used with an exponential fit on these books.
5. **Where is the edge per fill? (probe, H-WMQ-1 inconclusive).** From the touch out to 1 sigma a fill loses after the
   fee at 60 s in every phase (held: -3.8 bp at the touch, -2.5 to -6 bp from 0.1 to 1 sigma). At the CAL-chosen x* the
   held edge is **-5.1 bp per fill at 60 s [-23, +5]** and +1.9 bp at 300 s (1,970 fills). Deep fills are sweeps that
   revert over minutes: held e300 is +7.7 bp at 0.75 sigma, +15 at 1 sigma, +53 at 1.5 sigma; but they are rare (0.05
   fills per hour per resting order at 1 sigma, 0.012 at 1.5 sigma), so at the minimum lot this is worth well under a
   cent per symbol-day. The deep quoters show it: D_m1.5 +0.0009 [-0.006, +0.007], D_m2 +0.0027 [-0.0004, +0.0067].
6. **Size skew does nothing; combined with GLFT it is worse.** Linear size skew minus flat size at 3 lots: -0.0008 per
   lot [-0.003, +0.002] (H-WMQ-4 inconclusive). GLFT price skew plus size skew minus GLFT alone: -0.14 per lot [-0.15,
   -0.12] (H-WMQ-5 contradicts): the bigger reducing orders are picked off at the touch.
7. **Inventory exits change nothing measurable (H-WMQ-7 inconclusive).** Joining the touch after 10 minutes of inventory
   minus the plain 1 sigma quoter: +0.005 [-0.007, +0.018]; the 60 min time limit and the 3 sigma stop do no better (the
   stop version is slightly negative, -0.004 [-0.009, -0.000]).
8. **The BTC pull passes the registered rule only on the losing near-touch quoter (H-WMQ-8 supports, read with care).**
   On A_glft_s0.5_p it removes losing fills (+0.36 [+0.31, +0.41]); on the 1 sigma quoter it is +0.0075 [-0.005,
   +0.022]. This is H-MMW2 again: a pull makes a losing quoter trade less, it does not make a quoter profitable.
9. **The trend pull is inconclusive (H-WMQ-9)**: -0.0025 [-0.007, +0.003] on the 1 sigma quoter, +0.032 on the
   near-touch one.
10. **WMS's selection (R-WMS eligible_name) does not rescue it (H-WMQ-14 inconclusive).** On eligible symbol-days
    D_xstar - B0 is +0.015 [-0.010, +0.046] and D_xstar's own net +0.011 [-0.001, +0.027]; eligible minus the rest is
    -0.0001 [-0.045, +0.045]; the S1 score as a moderator has slope +0.034 [-0.006, +0.083] on the net. With the calm gate
    also open (3 weekends, 38 symbol-days) the paired result is +0.036 [-0.026, +0.115], underpowered. 148 of the 504 held
    symbol-days have no WMS row (outside WMS's universe that weekend).
11. **The WMN news gate makes no measurable difference (H-WMQ-12 / 15 inconclusive).** On the compact subset (7 core names
    x 8 weekends, 112 symbol-days): pull + widen minus ungated +0.0026 [-0.009, +0.016]; pull only +0.0024 [0.000,
    +0.007], and only 2 of the 112 symbol-days change at all (both on 2026-11-08). This agrees with WMN: news adds no
    adverse selection beyond flow.
12. **Capacity at our size (H-WMQ-11 inconclusive).** The best scheme scales linearly: on the compact subset +0.012 per
    lot at 1, 4 and 10 lots; 4 lots (~20 USD per side) +0.048 [-0.031, +0.146] per symbol-day, 10 lots (~50 USD per side)
    +0.12 [-0.08, +0.36] with a worst symbol-day of -3.09 USD. A 12-name book on one clock (net 40 / gross 80 USD caps at
    the minimum lot) makes +0.064 USD per day [-0.16, +0.27], worst day -2.91 USD; up to 7 names hold inventory at once,
    gross peaks at 53 USD and 27% of fills are dropped by the caps. The same B0 book: -0.065 per day [-0.19, +0.04].

**What to use and what to stop.** Stop quoting near the touch on these names whatever the skew model (GLFT / AS with a
touch-dominated fit, size skew on near-touch quotes and BTC-pulled near-touch variants all lose 0.3-1 USD per symbol-day
at the minimum lot). Stop fitting lambda(delta) = A exp(-k delta) to the whole intensity curve; if a closed-form skew is
wanted, fit where the quotes sit, or use the power law. Deep symmetric quotes (1.5-2 sigma) are the only family that does
not lose, and they earn nothing measurable. **No variant passes, so no live pilot is supported.** If the user still wants
a forward shadow, the least-bad candidate is D_xstar or D_m2 as paper only at the minimum lot on the forward weekends
replayed by `wmq_study run fwd`: expect about 0 +/- 0.015 USD per symbol-day and a worst book day near -3 USD.

## Data

- **Names (12).** The H-MMW core 7 (ATOM, XLM, WIF, TRUMP, ICP, WLD, PUMPFUN; select_universe.py rule) plus 5 by a rule stated before any result: the same filters on the same 2026-11-21 snapshot with a widened band (spread >= 3 bp, depth within 25 bp >= 25 k USD per side), ranked by spread / median |1m return|, the top 5 not in the core: TRX, GALA, CRV, 1000BONK, GRASS. No innovation-zone, TradFi or SPY names. The snapshot (2026-11-21) is applied to earlier weekends: survivorship and look-ahead in the *choice of names*, not in any quote.
- **Weekends (Sat + Sun UTC).** CAL 2026-03-07 .. 04-12 (6 weekends, 144 symbol-days, probe only), SEL 2026-04-18 .. 06-28 (11 weekends, 264 symbol-days), HOLD 2026-07-04 .. 11-22 (21 weekends, 504 symbol-days, clean_holdout). Forward weekends from 2026-11-28 are left to `wmq_study run fwd` (the archive publishes a day or two later).
- **Sources.** quote-saver.bycsi.com ob200 books and public.bybit.com trades of each symbol-day, and BTCUSDT's tape of each day for the BTC pull; Bybit public 1m klines for the warm-up of days without an earlier replay (the same closes as tape bars; highs / lows can differ because a kline opens at the previous close); the public funding history; today's public instrument rules. Every raw file was processed one symbol-day at a time and deleted.
- **Grids.** A per-day audit: a finer historical grid (prices off today's grid) is inferred from the decimals and retried (XLM in March used 0.00001); a coarser one (every print on a multiple of k ticks) is replayed on k ticks.
- **Compact books** of the 7 core names on the last 8 HOLD weekends were kept (lossless binary + gzip, ~10 MB per symbol-day vs ~23 MB raw; bit-identical replays) for the conditional and capacity re-runs.

## Caveats and what went wrong

- **The registered calibration made family A degenerate.** The exponential fit over all probe legs gives k = 12-17 per
  sigma, so every GLFT / AS member quotes near the touch. This was seen in the CAL fits (no P&L) before any SEL or HOLD
  replay and handled with a labelled extra trial (A2, amendment 1 in design.json; H-WMQ-13), not by changing A.
- **H-WMQ-8 passes by the letter on a losing base.** Read it as "the pull cuts losses of a near-touch quoter", not as
  evidence for the pull (the 1 sigma base is +0.0075, not significant).
- **Selection overfitting.** D's choice has PBO 0.59 and deflated Sharpe 0.12; the purged-CV estimate of every family's
  selection procedure is negative (D -0.012, B -0.001, E -0.002, A -0.38 USD per symbol-day); the training-best pick
  changes across splits (D_m1.5 67, D_xstar 54, D_m0.5 24 of 165). PBO 0.00 for families with dominated losers (A, C,
  ALL) is not reassuring, for the same reason as in H-MMR.
- **Names.** The 2026-11-21 snapshot defines the names for every weekend: survivorship and look-ahead in the choice of
  names (not in any quote). 148 held symbol-days have no R-WMS row.
- **Day mark.** Each day starts flat and is marked to the mid at 24:00 UTC without an exit cost; the "closed" column
  charges a taker exit on the end inventory (D_xstar: -0.002). Funding is computed from the public history and is below
  0.0002 USD per symbol-day for every variant (small positions held briefly).
- **Fill model.** Virtual orders (no impact, no hidden Bybit priority); a skewed quote placed inside the spread is filled
  by prints at the touch (optimistic, only for the near-touch families, which lose anyway); probe orders are independent
  experiments, so one sweep can fill several legs.
- **Throughput.** HOLD days took 35-200 s each (one 1,505 s): every BTC print with z > 2 advances all 168 quoters
  because two variants use the BTC pull (time, not correctness). To finish I added a guarded "tail" worker and later
  replaced two workers by four helpers on disjoint symbols; a killed worker's day is redone from scratch. The first tail
  run stopped itself after one symbol-day because of a bug in its guard (its own finished day counted as the owner's
  progress); it was fixed and restarted, and no day was processed twice.
- **Builds.** The first atx test build hit the governor's 3000 MB cap at 4 jobs (rustc out of memory); one job at 3000 MB
  worked; two later test runs failed at 1300 / 1700 MB caps and passed at 2300 MB. Clippy needed four fixes (three
  behaviour-neutral rewrites and then_some for then(|| ..)).
- **H-WMQ-12 text.** The registration states the compact subset's dates as 09-26 .. 11-21; the frozen design and the
  test use the last 8 HOLD weekends 10-03 .. 11-22 (the diary recorded the error before any replay).
- **Power.** With 42 day-clusters the minimum detectable mean at 80% power is ~0.02 USD per symbol-day for the near-zero
  deep quoters (CI half-width ~0.014): far below anything that matters in money, so the nulls are informative as "too
  small to matter".

## Method

- **Engine.** `atx mm-replay` (crates/atx/src/mm_replay.rs; private release copy data/research/wmq/bin/atx-wmq.exe): the ob200 snapshot + deltas through at_match's replayer into a real order book; the public tape merged by matching-engine time; virtual orders (no impact on the book). Queue: join the back at activation; prints at our price consume from the front; prints through our price or a crossing book fill us; unexplained level decreases are cancels split pro-rata or FIFO-pessimistic. Orders and cancels take effect one latency after they are sent. Regression: B0 on XLMUSDT 2026-11-15 with the new binary equals the MMW stats in every field of all four cells; replays from the compact files are bit-identical to the JSON source.
- **Cells.** pro-rata / FIFO-pessimistic x 150 / 300 ms; primary = pro-rata 150 ms. Fees: maker 2.0 bp, taker 5.5 bp.
- **Day.** Each symbol-day starts flat at 00:00 UTC and is marked to the mid at 24:00 UTC. net = spread + adverse 60 s + inventory - fees; adverse 1 / 10 / 300 s reported; funding = -position x mark x rate at the funding times inside the day (Bybit public history, 4 h or 8 h by symbol); closed = net - |end inventory| x (taker 5.5 bp + half the snapshot spread).
- **Probe.** One minimum-lot order per side at each x = 0 (the touch), 0.1, 0.2, 0.35, 0.5, 0.75, 1, 1.5, 2, 3 x the quoter's 1h sigma (max of the 240 and 30 min robust sigma, floor 20 bp; loghar_cr exists only for BTC ETH SOL XRP DOGE XAU XAG, none of them in the universe), re-pegged by the live replace rule; a filled order is re-sent at the next 10 s tick. Intensity = fills per live second of one order-side; per-fill edge e_h = spread captured + markout at h - fee (bp of the price).
- **Quote families** (mode model; minimum lot unless stated; symmetric cap 25 USD x lots, one base order from flat always allowed; the live 1.5 USD per-symbol and 10 USD global loss stops x lots; the live shock pause; no flow / toxicity / level layers). D: symmetric depth m sigma. A: GLFT closed-form asymptotic quotes (Gueant, Lehalle & Fernandez-Tapia 2013) and Avellaneda-Stoikov (2008) with a 1 h horizon, gamma set by the skew at the cap s_cap with 5 base orders at the cap, plain or adjusted by the CAL adverse cost at 60 s + fee (the Cartea-Jaimungal-Penalva depth). A2: the same with A, k fitted on the deep legs (amendment 1, extra trial). B: size skew at 3 lots. C: GLFT + size skew at 3 lots. E: passive unwind (the reducing quote joins the touch after 10 min), time limit (taker close after 60 min), adverse-move stop (3 sigma). F: the H-MMW2 BTC pull. T: a 60 min trend pull. B0: the live mm_illiq20 rule.
- **Selection** (SEL only): every choice of 3 of the 11 SEL weekends as test (165 splits); split statistic = the worst cell's test mean per base lot; score = median over splits; PBO by CSCV (2000 random half splits of the 22 SEL days), deflated Sharpe (N = 41 quoting variants), purged-CV OOS estimate of the procedure. Frozen in data/research/wmq/selection.json before any HOLD analysis.
- **Tests** (HOLD): mean per symbol-day with a day-clustered percentile bootstrap (B = 10000, seed 20261127); paired differences on the same symbol-days. Pass = mean > 0 with the CI above 0 in all four cells (plus the SEL mean > 0 for the absolute tests).
- **Segments.** CAL and SEL are before research.knowledge_cutoff (llm_in_sample); HOLD is clean_holdout. All results are recorded with hypotheses.record.
