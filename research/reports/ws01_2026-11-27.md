## 1. What we now believe (plain words)

1. **A "supports" in the registry is weak evidence on its own.** Over all 596 result rows (one primary two-sided p per
   row, 217 rows with no extractable statistic kept at p = 1), Benjamini-Yekutieli at 10% keeps **47 of 123 effect-claim
   supports (38%)**, Benjamini-Hochberg at 10% keeps 66 (54%). H-WS01-1 and H-WS01-2 support.
2. **The clean holdout mostly confirms signs, not effects.** 24 of 57 holdout supports with a statistic have p > 0.10
   (H-WS01-3 supports, marginally) and only 17 of 66 survive BY10 within the holdout family. `verdicts.py` and most
   study scripts judge the holdout on sign only.
3. **Only 15 hypotheses replicate in the two-stage sense** (discovery survives BY10, then the holdout p passes BH10
   among the selected): H-W1, H-W7, H-V1b, H-VCL1, H-RL2, H-NS4, H-NN8, H-RFSV1, H-XS1, H-XS2, H-GEO3, H-EXE1, H-JD4,
   H-PART2, H-FLD1. **Every one is about variance, its timing, densities or execution; none is a directional return
   edge.** Use these; treat everything else as unconfirmed.
4. **"zoo_ensemble SURVIVES" should be withdrawn; no stress unit passes every screen.** On closed trades with a bootstrap
   block matched to the holding period (p90 32 days; the stress harness used 5), zoo_ensemble's mean +0.32 R has CI
   [-0.004, +0.70] (one-sided P(mean <= 0) 0.027). Its holdout +0.66 R comes from 20 positions still open at the data
   end (+87 R marked at the last close); closed holdout trades: +0.10 R on 131, CI [-0.73, +0.99], negative without the
   5 best trades. Same signals as H-TR1, whose continuous-position holdout contradicts (Sharpe -0.42). zoo_ensemble and
   trend_4h are the strongest FRAGILE-positive units, not validated edges. This matters because trend_4h, a live sleeve,
   is gated by zoo_ensemble's rules (`agent/decision.py` GATE_STRATEGY).
5. **The live sleeves cannot be confirmed by the holdout in any useful time.** Closed-trade MDE (80%) is about 1.1 R
   per trade for zoo_ensemble, 0.53 R for trend_4h and 0.2 R for shock_regime; confirming effects of +0.06 to +0.3 R
   needs 1,700-7,700 trades, 5+ years. So the sleeves need harm-first forward rules (registered: H-WS01-9 trend_4h,
   H-WS01-11 shock_regime), not more historical tests.
6. **Most absence-of-evidence A-grade rules are powered nulls; R-LEV-1 is not.** Weekend shrink (R-VOL-2), HAR add-ons
   (R-VOL-7), vector candles (R-VOL-5), pivots (R-PX-10) and session tilts (R-AVOID-4) have MDEs well below a
   decision-relevant effect; R-VOL-24 is actually a significant opposite effect. But **R-LEV-1 ("crypto has no leverage
   effect")** rests on a daily test whose MDE (0.32) exceeds the coefficients themselves, and the hourly re-test
   H-WS01-10 finds a leverage asymmetry in-sample: next-hour log RV responds 1.5x more to down hours than to up hours
   with the same RV (A = +0.053, day-clustered t 7.3, 814k token-hours); the holdout is null but underpowered
   (A +0.001, MDE 0.065). Suggest R-LEV-1 -> grade C, scoped to daily.
7. **Rules are graded on verdict labels, not on surviving evidence.** 8 of 17 A-grade rules rest on no row that
   survives BY10 in either direction (the powered-null ones are fine; R-LEV-1 is the exception above). R-PNN-1 (active
   B) prefers the quantile network over log-HAR-t citing H-PNN1, which contradicts.
8. **Power cannot be audited for most of the record**: only 5 in-sample / holdout pairs record n next to the statistic
   (H-WS01-4, -7, -8 inconclusive). BY-surviving discoveries replicate 79% vs 56% (H-WS01-5 inconclusive, Fisher p
   0.12); no clear threshold bunching at 1.96 (H-WS01-6 inconclusive, p 0.11; exploratory and seen after the data: a
   12-vs-4 bunch just above the house bar t = 2).

What to stop using: "zoo_ensemble SURVIVES" as validation; holdout "supports" labels without their p; R-LEV-1 as A-grade;
R-PNN-1 as active; H-FU2's holdout "supports" (t 6.45 on 14 overlapping observations). What to use: the 15 robust
results; the stress FAILS verdicts (unchanged under the re-check); the powered nulls behind R-VOL-2/5/7/24, R-PX-10,
R-AVOID-4.

## 7. Caveats

- One p per row by frozen rules; Simes combinations (112 rows) test "some cell", liberal for "all cells" claims;
  adjusted-only p (65 rows) are conservative; bootstrap p floors (1/(B+1)) cap some rows; several rows store a study-level
  p identical in both segments (H-NS4, H-PD4, H-NN3). BY is conservative under positive dependence; m counts every row,
  including pooled restatements.
- The null-claim regex mislabels one effect claim (H-VBOS2).
- Two-stage replication, the stress re-judgement and the MDE table are descriptive (not registered); the stress
  re-judgement re-reads data already seen and is a sensitivity, not new evidence.
- H-WS01-10's relative equivalence margin (25% of the mean coefficient) is narrow when both coefficients are small; the
  in-sample result is a significant asymmetry regardless of the margin. In-sample is dominated by the 2022 bear market.
- The family audit was done by read-only sub-audits within a time budget; "not checked" is stated where they did not
  read code. Their claims that feed the top of the ledger were verified by WS01; the rest are leads.

## 8. Method

Frozen before results (diary 2026-11-26 "WS01 design frozen before results"; REFINE diary 2026-11-27): snapshot = first
596 rows of `research/hypotheses_results.jsonl` (sha256 1be16ba2...); primary p per row in priority segment sub-dict /
`primary` sub-dict, raw p, t / z (t_nw first, Student-t df = n - 1 when a count exists), ratio or difference with CI
(log scale for ratios), adjusted-only p, Simes over cells, a manual map for 33 supports rows (statistic named by the
registered success rule), else p = 1. BH and BY step-up at 5% / 10% per family. Power: expected holdout z = z_in
sqrt(n_hold / n_in), one-sided 5%. Rules x evidence from `research/rules.yaml` evidence lists. Stress re-check: the
stress harness's own `r_net`, `daily` and stationary bootstrap on `data/research/stress/trades` (read only). TVTP
re-check on `data/research/tvtp/m1h_walkforward.npz`. H-WS01-10: pooled within-token OLS on 890k token-hours from
`data/research/rough_vol/rv_*.npz` (deseasonalised log RV `h_lu`), day-clustered sandwich SE.

Storage: data/research/ws01 about 2 MB (quota 100 MB). Compute: all jobs in the light pool (caps 400-1200 MB, peak
well under the cap, each under 2 minutes); one job waited on RAM (free RAM 1.4 GB < 1.5 GB headroom) and was restarted
with a smaller cap.
