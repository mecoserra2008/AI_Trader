## 1. Verdicts in plain words

1. **The registry's "supports" are a weak signal on their own.** Over all 596 recorded result rows (one primary p per
   row; the 217 rows with no extractable statistic kept p = 1), Benjamini-Yekutieli at 10% keeps **47 of the 123
   effect-claim "supports" rows (38%)**; Benjamini-Hochberg at 10% keeps 66 (54%). Among supports rows that carry a
   statistic at all: BY 46%, BH 64%. (H-WS01-1 supports, H-WS01-2 supports.)
2. **The holdout mostly confirms signs, not effects.** Of 57 clean_holdout effect-claim supports with a statistic,
   **24 (42%) have p > 0.10**, and 9 more have no statistic. This is by design (`src/ai_trader/research/verdicts.py`
   requires only the sign in the holdout; MIN_HOLDOUT = 8), and under a zero effect a sign agrees half the time.
   Within the holdout family BY10 keeps only 17 of 66 holdout supports. (H-WS01-3 supports, marginally: 0.42 vs 0.40.)
3. **Only 16 hypotheses are "robust" in the two-stage sense** (discovery survives BY10 in the discovery family AND the
   holdout p passes BH10 among the selected): H-W1, H-W7 (weekend vol / weekend VRP), H-V1b, H-VCL1, H-RL2, H-NS4,
   H-NN8, H-RFSV1 (volatility forecasting), H-XS1, H-XS2 (xs network), H-GEO3 (release minutes), H-EXE1, H-JD4
   (execution / brackets), H-PART2 (taker-type markouts), H-FLD1 (BTC field density), H-VC2 (swept zones revisited).
   **Every robust result is about volatility, timing of variance, densities or execution; none is a directional
   return edge.** This matches and sharpens the launch state of knowledge.
4. **The trend family's own registry evidence is weak.** H-TR1 (equal-weight zoo ensemble, net Sharpe): in-sample
   portfolio NW t = 2.38 (p 0.017, q_BY 0.47: does not survive), deflated Sharpe of the best variant 0.72, and the
   **clean holdout contradicts** (portfolio Sharpe -0.42, t -0.32, 8 of 23 symbols positive). It is still cited by
   active rules R-PX-3, R-PD-1, R-PD-2. The "zoo_ensemble SURVIVES" claim rests on the stress-harness trade layer
   (H-STRESS0 unit rows: mean R 0.35, CI [0.03, 0.71]; holdout mean R 0.66 on 151 trades), a different P&L definition;
   that discrepancy is the first red-team item (v2).
5. **Power cannot be audited for most of the record:** only 5 in-sample/holdout pairs record a sample count with the
   statistic (H-WS01-4, -7, -8 inconclusive). Median holdout power for those 5 is 0.57 (H-PD3 0.22, H-W5b 0.40).
   Recording n (and the estimate and SE) next to every statistic is the cheapest fix in this audit.
6. **Significance carries some information:** BY-surviving in-sample supports replicated in the holdout 19/24 (79%)
   vs 9/16 (56%) for non-surviving ones (Fisher one-sided p 0.12, H-WS01-5 inconclusive).
7. **No clear p-hacking signature at the conventional threshold:** 16 |z| just above 1.96 vs 9 just below (10% caliper,
   one-sided binomial p 0.11, H-WS01-6 inconclusive). Exploratory (not registered, seen after the data): the excess is
   concentrated at t in [2.00, 2.07), the registry's own "t > 2" bar.
8. **Rules are graded on verdict labels, not on FDR-surviving evidence.** Of the 17 A-grade active rules, 8 rest on no
   row that survives registry-wide BY10 in either direction (R-VOL-2, R-VOL-5, R-VOL-7, R-LEV-1, R-VOL-24, R-PX-10,
   R-PD-4, R-AVOID-4: built only on contradicts / inconclusive / null-claim rows whose power was never stated; R-KFD-1/2
   also have no supports but do rest on significant negative rows, which is real evidence for "avoid").
   Absence-of-evidence rules need an MDE next to them.

## 6. Method (frozen before results; diary 2026-11-26 "WS01 design frozen before results")

- Snapshot: first 596 rows of `research/hypotheses_results.jsonl` (sha256 1be16ba2...); later rows belong to running
  workstreams. Registered first: H-WS01-1..7, extra trial H-WS01-8 (registered after H-WS01-4 qualified only 2 pairs).
- Primary p per row, priority: segment sub-dict / `primary` sub-dict first; raw p (p_raw, p, ...); t / z (t_nw first;
  Student-t with df = n - 1 when a count key exists); ratio or difference with its CI (log scale for ratios); adjusted-
  only p (p_holm, holm_p; conservative); Simes over per-cell sub-dicts (multi-asset / multi-horizon rows; liberal for
  "all cells" claims); a manual map for 33 supports rows the generic rules miss (statistic named by the registered
  success rule: Simes for "at least one", max p (intersection-union) for "all of", binomial vs 1/2 for share claims);
  otherwise p = 1. Sources: t 80, p 76, adjusted-only 65, Simes 112, CI 13, manual 33, none 217.
- BH and BY step-up at q = 0.05 / 0.10 per family; two-stage replication = BY10 discovery then BH10 on the holdout p
  of the selected (Bogomolov-Heller style; descriptive, not registered).
- Null / bound claims by a frozen regex on title + statement (13 ids; one false positive H-VBOS2 noted).
- Caveats: Simes and adjusted-only p mix test semantics; bootstrap p floors (1/(B+1)) cap some rows (H-PD4, H-NN3);
  several rows store a study-level p identical in both segments (H-NS4, H-PD4, H-NN3), so their holdout is not an
  independent test; the BY dependence correction is conservative; m counts every row including pooled restatements.
