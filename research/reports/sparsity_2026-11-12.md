Code: `src/ai_trader/ml/sparse.py`, `src/ai_trader/research/sparsity_study.py` (design in its docstring), `src/ai_trader/research/sparsity_report.py`; tests: `tests/test_sparse.py`; artifacts: `data/models/ml/sparse/`. **The vol network is mostly redundant weight.** Pruning the H-NN1 MLP to 95.4% sparsity leaves 100 of 2192 weights, about 24 of 48 hidden units and 29 of 52 inputs. It forecasts as well as the dense ensemble or slightly better. The QLIKE ratio is 0.986 [0.964, 1.007] in-sample and 0.974 [0.943, 1.027] in the holdout. Against log-HAR the 95% network gets -9.3% (t -4.0) in-sample and -8.6% (t -1.6) in the holdout; the dense network got -8.0% / -6.2%. H-SP1 passes in-sample but is inconclusive in the holdout: 133 dates leave the CI upper bound at 1.027, above the 1.02 bar. Beyond about 97% the circuit starts to break in-sample: 1.026 at 98.4% (36 weights) and 1.059 at 99.0% (21 weights).

**What survives is the vol level and the calendar.** At 99% sparsity, across 56 refits x 3 seeds, the inputs that keep a path to the output are log_sigma 94%, lrv_w 93%, next_wk_hours 46%, lrv_m 44%, lrv_d 38%, lrv_q 36%, er_7d 36%, next_is_weekend 36%. At 98%: lrv_w 99%, log_sigma 98%, lrv_m 83%, next_wk_hours 82%, lrv_q 71%, lrv_d 67%, next_is_weekend 64%, v0_dir_score 60%. Jump, semivariance, range-estimator, funding, OI and raw-return inputs are pruned first. Stability selection on a 236-column library agrees, at the level of concepts rather than columns. No single column reaches probability 0.8 in either segment (0 / 0 selected), because the vol-level measures substitute for each other. So H-SP2 contradicts as registered. The post-hoc cluster view (|rho| >= 0.8) shows what is stable: the vol-level cluster (1.00 in-sample / 0.99 holdout); the weekend-calendar cluster (0.99 / 0.75); and nothing else in both segments. The minimal input set for next-day vol is one vol-level measure (robust sigma or the 7-day mean log RV, with the 30/90-day level as a slower anchor) plus the forecast day's weekend / TradFi-weekend hours. Every other tool output is optional for this target.

**The surviving neurons are readable, but redundant rather than monosemantic.** The analysed network (seed 0, 2026-07 refit, 95% level) has 24 surviving units (19 in layer 1, 5 in layer 2). Layer-1 units are mostly 1-4-input ramps. The five layer-2 units carry most attribution, and each is a mix of vol level and weekday. 17 / 24 units are fitted with holdout R^2 > 0.8 by an additive spline of 2 inputs (42% by 1 input), short of the 80% bar. Mean-ablating the top-3 units raises QLIKE x1.45 [1.30, 1.61] in-sample and x1.37 [0.95, 1.97] in the holdout. Ablating any single unit changes holdout QLIKE by -5% to +6%. The circuit is a few redundant copies of one function, 'vol level, damped on calm weekends', so H-SP3 contradicts. Top units in plain words: L2u10: fires on 100% of days, more when low 7-day mean log RV and the forecast day is Fri-Sun; when active it lowers the vol forecast; L2u3: fires on 100% of days, more when low 30-day mean log RV and the forecast day is Fri-Sun; when active it lowers the vol forecast; L2u4: fires on 100% of days, more when high 30-day mean log RV and low market return yesterday; when active it raises the vol forecast; L2u12: fires on 90% of days, more when high robust sigma (log) and the forecast day is Fri-Sun; when active it raises the vol forecast; L2u14: fires on 100% of days, more when high robust sigma (log) and high v0 trend score; when active it raises the vol forecast.

**Direction does not collapse to the base rate, but it carries nothing.** The pruned direction network keeps 33 units at 95%; its forecasts keep sd 0.031. Its in-sample Brier skill stays at -0.0007 [-0.0049, +0.0031] (dense -0.0024). The holdout is +0.0049 [-0.0000, +0.0089] on 133 dates, one of 23 rounds looked at. The inputs that survive at 99% are market-wide day-level variables (market log RV, downside share, market and BTC returns), the same date-noise signature as H-NN2. Read this as no signal, not as a lead.

**What this means for the decision layer.** For next-day vol, call the vol-level tool (robust sigma / log-HAR level) and the calendar (weekend and TradFi-weekend hours). The ML vol forecaster adds a modest nonlinear refinement of those two, which a ~100-weight network reproduces. Lagged, transformed, interacted or quantile versions of the other inputs add no stable information. Don't ask a tool for next-day direction.

## Methods

**Panel and protocol.** Identical to H-NN1 (`ai_trader.ml.features`, universe L, 52 point-in-time inputs, complete rows, decision at 00:00 UTC of D+1; monthly expanding walk-forward 2022-04 to 2026-11 with 24 h purge + 1 day embargo; last 15% of training dates as the purged validation block; fit-block standardisation; early stopping; validation-block bias constant). Network: vol MLP (32, 16), wd 0.01, 3 seeds; direction MLP (64, 32), wd 1e-4. The dense network is re-trained with `ml.sparse.train_masked` (no mask), which is bit-identical to `models.fit_mlp`.

**H-SP1 pruning.** Iterative magnitude pruning, layer-wise (20% of surviving hidden weights, 10% of the head per round; biases kept), rewinding the survivors to the end of epoch 1 (weights and batch order) and retraining with the mask re-applied after every step; 22 rounds per seed and refit, full walk-forward (56 refits, no subset). Sparsity is deterministic: 95.4% at round 14 (test level), 98.4% at 19, 99.04% at 22. Ratio = date-averaged QLIKE of the sparse ensemble / dense ensemble; 10-day moving-block bootstrap over dates (1000).

**H-SP2 stability selection.** Library of 236 columns: base 52, lag 49, log 35, product 45, quantile_dummy 48, weekday 7. Lags are exact calendar lags within the symbol; products are of the standardised H-NN3 top-10 inputs; quantile dummies are 1{x > q} with in-sample edges. Target next-day log RV. Per segment: 200 subsamples of half of the dates, randomised lasso (penalty 1/W, W ~ U[0.5, 1]), 40 lambdas, region where the mean union size <= q_max = sqrt(0.6 p) (E[V] <= 1), threshold 0.8.

**H-SP3 units.** Seed-0 member of the 2026-07 refit (trained on labels to 2026-06-30) at the H-SP1-selected level. Additive cubic splines (6 quantile knots; one-hot for inputs with <= 8 values) on the best input and the best additive pair (chosen by fitting R^2); holdout R^2. Attribution |a x dy/da| on in-sample rows; top-3 mean ablation.
