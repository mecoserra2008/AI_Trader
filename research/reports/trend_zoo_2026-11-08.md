# Trend model zoo: leaderboard, H-TR1, H-TR2 (2026-11-08)

Zoo: `src/ai_trader/strategies/trend_zoo.py` (21 fixed variants, not in the strategy REGISTRY). Study:
`src/ai_trader/research/trend_zoo_study.py`. Data: bybit, 23-symbol universe, `market_frame(funding=False, oi=False)`,
4h bars (primary) and 1d (secondary). Costs 7.5 bps per side, `evaluate.strategy_pnl` (sized at entry, risk units).
Signals are computed on each symbol's full history. All variants of a symbol share one evaluation window, which starts on
the first full UTC date after the slowest variant is warm (about 64 days after listing plus burn-in on 4h, 240 days on 1d),
so every variant is scored on the same days. Daily net P&L is the sum by UTC date. Sharpe = mean/std·√365 per segment,
then averaged over symbols. Segments: `llm_in_sample` < 2026-07-01 ≤ `clean_holdout` (130 days). All 23 symbols have
more than 30 days in both segments. The in-sample numbers and verdicts were recorded (hypotheses_results.jsonl, diary
2026-11-08) before the holdout view was computed. No parameter was changed after that. Cross-check: the three configured
strategies reproduce the pivot-geometry report's figures (ema_stack 0.24 / −0.43, donchian 0.27 / +0.02, tsmom 0.35 / −0.56).

**Panel for H-TR3/H-TR4:** `data/priors/trend_zoo_daily_4h.parquet` (symbol, date, variant, pnl; 22 variants including
`ensemble`, 902,814 rows, 2020-06-17 to 2026-11-07). The 1d panel is `trend_zoo_daily_1d.parquet`. Both are written
atomically, before any analysis runs.

## The zoo (fixed before testing)

| family | variants | signal |

The registry comment says "~24" variants. This list gives 21. "EMA stack × 2 scales" was read as the 1:4:16 day spacing
at two scales. Ensemble = the equal-weight mean of the 21 signals, traded as one signal (it is not an average of P&L).
Its P&L is in the panel as `ensemble`.

winner, it would become in-sample data (§3b, "a holdout used for selection is in-sample"), and there would be nothing
clean left to check the winner against. It is also too short to rank on. With 130 days, the standard error of one
symbol's annualised Sharpe is about √(365/130) ≈ 1.7. Even the 23-symbol equal-weight portfolio's holdout t-stats are

PSR(0), the undeflated probability that the true Sharpe is above 0, is 0.99. After selection across 22 trials, the

already tested in H-R1, H-X2, H-X2b, H-X3 and H-GT1/2. In-sample, then, the winner is "probably positive, not proven".

## H-TR2: walk-forward top-3 selection vs the ensemble (4h)

Each calendar month, per symbol (once 365 days of evaluation history exist), the 21 variants are ranked by trailing
365-day net Sharpe and the top 3 are held with equal weight (mean of their daily P&L). The ensemble is scored on the
same symbol-days. The paired test averages, by month, the difference of vol-normalised daily P&L (×√365 ≈ a Sharpe
difference) and takes a Newey–West t over months.

| segment | selection Sharpe | ensemble Sharpe | symbols where selection wins | paired monthly diff | t | months | rank IC trailing→next month | verdict |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---|
| in-sample | +0.07 | +0.23 | 3/23 | −0.19 | **−2.9** (NW) | 60 | +0.03 (t 1.5) | **contradicts** |
| holdout | −0.90 | −0.42 | 6/23 | −0.32 | −1.2 (plain) | 5 | −0.00 | **contradicts** |

H-TR2 is **contradicted in both segments**, in the direction the registry's rationale expected: picking past winners
costs about 0.2 Sharpe a year compared with holding everything, and in-sample this is significant (t −2.9). The
trailing ranking has almost no persistence (rank IC +0.03). Selection tends to pick fast variants after they have had a
lucky year (tsmom_3d is the 3rd most-picked model in-sample but ranks 21st overall), which also raises turnover. If a
trend book is run at all, it should be the ensemble or a fixed choice, not a rotating "best model". The ensemble Sharpe
here (0.23) is below its H-TR1 figure (0.33) because the walk-forward period starts a year later.

## Stability: does the in-sample ranking predict the holdout?

Barely. The rank correlation of in-sample vs holdout Sharpe across the 22 rows is +0.35 at 4h (p 0.11) and +0.21 at 1d.
The whole zoo shifted down by about 0.66 Sharpe (mean of the in-sample column +0.24, mean of the holdout column −0.43).
That is one common factor: Jul–Nov 2026 was a poor trend regime for crypto across all horizons. It does not mean some
variants broke and others didn't. The only rows with a positive holdout (donch_14 +0.04, cfg_donchian_breakout +0.02,
mac_14_60 +0.08) sit in the middle of the in-sample table, and their holdout t-stats are 0.1–0.4.

## Which trend model deserves a place in the demo book?

**None, as a standalone sleeve, and the risk engine's net-E[R] gate is right to keep blocking trend candidates.**

1. **The edge is thin even in-sample.** The best single-symbol Sharpe is 0.50 (tsmom_14d). Its deflated Sharpe at
   N = 22 is 0.72, so it does not clear deflation. The ensemble passes H-TR1 in-sample by 0.03.
2. **A rough translation into the gate's units shows why the sleeve sits near +0.02R.** For a trade held about h days
   with a stop near one horizon-σ, E[R] ≈ SR_ann · √(h/365). With in-sample figures that gives tsmom_14d ≈ 0.50·0.20 =
   0.10R, tsmom_30d ≈ 0.43·0.29 = 0.12R, and ensemble (h ≈ 2 weeks) ≈ 0.33·0.20 ≈ 0.07R. These are at or below the
   0.10R gate before any bracket and stop slippage, and in the holdout all three are negative. The demo sleeve trades
   1h candidates with a 3.2% stop and roughly 3:1 brackets, so its holding period is much shorter and it has even less
   room. The 4h-and-slower models clear costs only because they trade rarely. The fast variants (tsmom_1d/3d) show that
   the gross edge at short horizons is real (gross Sharpe +0.20/+0.46) but costs eat all of it.
3. **Do not chase the holdout winners.** Promoting donch_14 or mac_14_60 because they were the only positive holdout
   rows would select on the holdout, and their holdout t-stats are 0.1–0.4.
4. **What would earn a place:** a forward record. The ensemble is the right default (lowest drawdown, no selection
   risk, and H-TR2 shows selection hurts). Keep it **paper-only**, scored from the daily panel as the holdout grows. Treat
   it as the "trend" input to the regime work (H-TR3/H-TR4): if a regime gate can't lift its out-of-sample Sharpe well
   above about 0.5, trend stays out of the live book. Do not lower `min_net_expected_r` to let the current sleeve
   through.
