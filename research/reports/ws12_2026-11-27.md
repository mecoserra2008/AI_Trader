## Plain-words verdicts

**Small deep networks beat log-HAR-t at 1h, but the gain is the input, not the network.** A 6k-parameter GRU (and an
equivalent TCN) pooled over BTC, ETH, SOL, XRP, DOGE and BNB beats per-asset log-HAR-t on next-hour return quantiles by
0.8% of the pinball loss in-sample and 1.0% in the clean holdout (t -9.1 / -5.7; H-WS12-1 supports) and on next-hour
variance by 5-7% QLIKE (t -5.3 / -3.3; H-WS12-3 supports). Most of that comes from one input log-HAR-t did not have: the
high-low (Parkinson) range of the recent bars. A **linear** log-HAR-t with three range terms and turnover / funding / OI
(`harx`, an extra trial registered after the first results) gets 98% of the GRU's in-sample pinball gain and 78% of its
holdout gain. Against harx the GRU keeps nothing in-sample on pinball (t -0.2) and a small edge in the holdout (-0.2% of
the loss, t -2.4; QLIKE -2.5%, t -2.8): H-WS12-8 is inconclusive. A plain feature MLP with the same inputs equals the GRU
(t +0.7 / +0.6 on pinball; H-WS12-9 supports), so the 72-hour sequence encoder adds nothing over hand-made lags.

**At 4h the networks are a little better, at 24h only on variance.** 4h: pinball -0.4% (t -2.3 in-sample, -1.6 holdout),
log score t -3.5 / -3.3. 24h quantiles: no difference (t -0.2 / -1.4; H-WS12-2 inconclusive, MDE about 1.5-2% of the loss,
so a 0.5% gain like the 1h one would go undetected). 24h variance: the networks beat log-HAR-t (QLIKE -3.0% / -4.7%, t -4.8 /
-2.7) and harx too (-1.6% / -3.8%, t -3.5 / -2.2).

**The results do not depend on the seed (H-WS12-5 supports).** Every one of the 3 seeds beats log-HAR-t at 1h with t below
-4.6; the seed sd of the 1h pinball (0.00003 in-sample, 0.00019 holdout) is 12-60 times smaller than the gap. GRU and TCN are
indistinguishable on quantiles everywhere; on 1h QLIKE the TCN is better in-sample (by 0.011, t 3.4; the MCS keeps only
the TCN there) but not in the holdout (t 0.9), and the configuration choice shows no sign of selection overfitting (PBO 0.0
over the 6 seed-0 candidates; the inner-validation choice of lr did not matter: inner-validation differences <= 0.005).

**Calibration is fine for every model**, classical included: 80% / 90% central intervals within 1.1 pp of nominal at 1h
(within 2.4 pp at 4h and 24h), PIT KS distances 0.006-0.018 at 1h. The gains are sharpness, not calibration.

**Meta-labelling the 4h trend trades does not work (H-WS12-6 contradicts).** A shallow LightGBM, trained walk-forward on
the conditions at entry, ranks trades a little better than chance (AUC 0.54 in-sample, 0.58 holdout). But the trades it
would reject are still profitable in-sample (+0.14 R per trade on 2,651 trades), so filtering cuts the net R per candidate
from +0.19 to +0.10 R. In the clean holdout it looks the other way (rejected -0.21 R, t -1.1, 172 trades; per candidate
+0.18 -> +0.31 R), but that is one small window pointing against the in-sample result. Keep taking every trend_4h /
zoo_ensemble signal the existing gates pass; do not add an ML filter.

**What the tree looks at is stable (H-WS12-7 supports).** Top-5 TreeSHAP sets agree across 15 quarterly folds x 3 seeds
(mean Jaccard 0.53): the vol level relative to its 1-year mean (in the top 5 of every fit), side-signed funding (87%), the
BTC 72 h and 240 h trend in the trade's direction (80%, 60%) and the monthly HAR level (60%). A stable description of a
weak signal: it does not turn into net R.

`chronos-forecasting` was refused by this session's permission system; per that refusal it was not retried another way.

## What changed, what to use, what to stop using

- **Use** for next-hour bands and sigma_risk inputs: log-HAR-t **with the Parkinson range terms** (harx: range variance of
  the last bar and the mean over the last 6 and 24 bars, in units of the robust base). It is linear, per asset, fitted in
  seconds and captures nearly all of what the networks find at 1h. Rule candidate R-WS12-1 below.
- **Keep** the existing small networks (live PNN 1h, xs vol heads) where they already run, but treat their edge over a
  range-augmented HAR as small (0-0.2% pinball, 1-2.5% QLIKE); do not build bigger sequence models for this target.
- **Stop expecting** sequence architectures (GRU / TCN over 72 h of raw inputs) to beat a feature MLP or a linear model on
  the same inputs at these horizons; H-WS12-9 says they do not.
- **Daily variance** is the one place where the networks still add something beyond harx (24h QLIKE -1.6% / -3.8%,
  t -3.5 / -2.2); a classical candidate for the same job (HAR with range and the intraday clock at daily frequency, WS04's
  specs) should be tried before adopting a network there.
- **No ML filter for the 4h trend trades** (H-WS12-6 contradicts).

## Dossier 1: next-hour, next-4h and next-day return distribution (y_h, vol-standardised)

**Variable and decision link.** y_h = log return over the next h hours divided by sqrt(h s2_t), s2_t the robust_bc base
variance known at the bar open. Its quantiles set stop distances (the 4 sigma cap prior), band widths, probability of touch
and the EV gate; its scale is the risk unit.

- The base scale is about 10% too small (sd of y 1.08-1.24; XRP 24h 1.43) and the residual tails are very heavy (Student-t df 2.4-4.7,
  lower at 4h and 24h than at 1h). XRP's moments are dominated by a few extreme upward jumps (skew 11, excess kurtosis 806 at 1h).
- Returns are nearly white at 1h for BTC, ETH, SOL and BNB (Ljung-Box p 0.06-0.54); XRP and DOGE reject (p <= 0.005,
  small autocorrelations on 35k rows). The 4h series show small but significant
  autocorrelation in several assets, plausibly the intraday clock (4h origins sit at 6 different hours) rather than drift.
- Squares are autocorrelated at 1h in 5 of 6 assets (BNB p 0.056): vol clustering is not fully removed by the base;
  much less at 24h (SOL is the exception).
- log v_h (variance relative to the base) passes ADF everywhere. KPSS does not reject at 1h (ETH borderline, p 0.05) or
  at 24h for 4 of 6 assets (ETH p 0.041 and XRP 0.016 reject); it rejects at 4h for every asset (p 0.01), again the
  hour-of-day pattern of 4h origins. In base units the 24h ratio mean-reverts (acf1 about -0.1): the base already
  carries the persistence.

**Candidate specifications.**
- base-t: robust sigma x causal hour-of-week factor, Student-t residual (MLE). Assumes the base captures all
  predictable variance; one df for all states.
- log-HAR-t (the classical champion, R-VOL-20 / R-PNN-2): log variance linear in the base level, hour-of-week factor and
  daily / weekly / monthly RV relative to the base; QLIKE bias constant; Student-t residual. Assumes log-linear variance
  dynamics, a constant residual shape, parameters stable over the expanding window; per asset.
- emp: trailing empirical quantiles of y_h (720 h / 180 x 4h / 365 days). Assumes stationarity of the standardised
  distribution over the trailing window; no conditioning.
- mlp: feature MLP on the 19 H-NN8 hourly features + 6 static covariates, Student-t head with learned df and a QLIKE log-RV
  head per horizon. Nonlinear in hand-made lag features.
- gru / tcn: the same heads on a 72-hour window of 13 raw per-step inputs (returns, ranges, vol path, turnover, funding,
  OI, calendar, hour-of-week factor) + the same static covariates. Nonlinear, learns its own lags; pooled over assets.

- harx (extra trial): log-HAR-t plus the Parkinson range variance of bar t-1 and its 6 h / 24 h means (base units),
  turnover, |funding|, |OI change|. Same assumptions as log-HAR-t; adds the most efficient cheap realised measure (range).
- mlpx (extra trial): the feature MLP with the same 6 extra inputs.

**What each would imply if true.** If log-HAR-t were adequate, no conditioning variable would lower the scores further. If
the sequence models' extra capacity mattered, the GRU / TCN would beat a feature MLP on identical information.

**Assumption checks (fit data and test PIT).** The residual distribution is Student-t-like with df 3.1-4.2 at 1h for every
asset (MLE on standardised returns), so a Gaussian head would be mis-specified; all heads here are t. The networks' learned
df moves with the state: at 1h the median df is 3.9 (10-90%: 3.5-4.4) and it falls when the forecast variance rises
(correlation of df with log F -0.94 / -0.89): tails get heavier relative to the scale in high-vol hours, which a single-df
log-HAR-t cannot do; it is a plausible source of the networks' log-score advantage over harx (not tested separately). PIT KS distances of
0.006-0.018 at 1h for all t-based models (n 21k-53k: formally rejected, practically small); 80% / 90% coverage within
1.1 pp. Parameter stability: the two refits give the same ordering of model families (networks < log-HAR-t < base-t < emp)
at every horizon, and the in-sample year is
scored by models frozen at 2025-06-30 without refitting.

## Dossier 2: realised variance over the next 1h / 4h / 24h (v_h, QLIKE)

**Variable and decision link.** v_h = sum of squared hourly returns over the next h hours divided by h s2_t: the sigma_risk
input to stops and the EV gate. QLIKE (v / F + log F) is the loss because it is robust to the noise in the RV proxy
(Patton 2011).

**Properties.** log v_h is stationary by ADF in every asset and horizon; KPSS agrees at 1h and for 4 of 6 assets at 24h
and rejects at 4h (p 0.01, the hour-of-day pattern of 4h origins). Squared returns are strongly autocorrelated at 1h
(Ljung-Box p 0.000 in 5 assets, BNB 0.056); the robust base removes most but not all of the clustering.

**Specifications** are the RV heads of the same models (networks: a log F output per horizon trained on QLIKE; log-HAR(-X):
OLS on log max(v_h, 1e-6) with the QLIKE-optimal bias constant; base: the hour-of-week factor; emp: the trailing mean of
v_h). Assumptions: a log-linear conditional mean (HAR) or a smooth nonlinear one (networks); the RV proxy is unbiased for
the conditional variance (QLIKE ranks correctly under a noisy unbiased proxy).

## Dossier 3: outcome of a 4h trend trade (meta-labelling, WS13 core)

**Variable and decision link.** 1{net R > 0} and net R of a zoo_ensemble / trend_4h trade (stress trade streams, base
variant, base costs, closed trades only: 5,857). It would gate entries of the one surviving family.

**Properties.** Win rate 26% (zoo_ensemble) and 28% (trend_4h); per-trade sd 4.0 R and 1.8 R around means of +0.32 and
+0.09 R; heavy right tail (zoo_ensemble accepted-stream skew 8.4, kurtosis 111): the mean is carried by a few large winners, so a classifier of P(win) is only loosely tied to E[R]. Trades overlap in time across
symbols, so inference clusters by entry date.

**Specification.** LightGBM binary, depth 3, 200 trees, lr 0.05, min 40 rows per leaf, bagging / feature fraction 0.8, 3
seeds; quarterly expanding walk-forward from 2023Q1 with purging (training trades must have exited a day before the test
quarter). Rule fixed before results: take iff the 3-seed mean p_hat >= the training-fold base rate. Assumptions: the
conditions at entry (vol level, trend agreement, funding, OI, calendar, liquidity tier, stop distance) carry stable
information about the outcome; the relation is stable across quarters; costs as in stress.r_net.

## Overfitting diagnostics

- **Selection.** The only choices made on data were the lr per arch and refit (2 values, inner validation, seed 0) and
  D* (gru vs tcn by R1 inner-validation loss). Every test row was scored once. The extra trials (harx, mlpx) were registered
  after the first results and are labelled as such (2 extra trials).
- **PBO** (CSCV, 16 blocks of the 512 test dates, mean per-date loss, 6 seed-0 candidates = 3 archs x 2 lr): 0.00 for 1h
  pinball and for 1h QLIKE (12,870 splits; the in-sample winner is always a sequence model, gru or tcn, which also wins out of
  sample). With candidates this similar, PBO mainly says the ranking gru/tcn > mlp is stable across time blocks.
- **Learning curves.** Early stopping at epoch 9-21 (gru), 2-20 (tcn), 2-15 (mlp) of at most 40; best inner-validation loss
  2.411-2.422 (R1) and 2.440-2.459 (R2) for gru / tcn vs 2.453-2.482 for the mlp; the training loss at the best epoch is
  at or above the validation loss (dropout is on in training, and the training block covers older, noisier years). Each epoch sees 16,384 of 195k (R1) / 248k (R2) fit
  origins, so the networks see each origin only a few times: they are data-rich and
  capacity-poor, the opposite of the overfitting regime.
- **Seed variance.** 1h pinball sd over seeds: gru 0.00003 / 0.00019, tcn 0.0006 / 0.0003, mlp 0.0002 / 0.0002 (in-sample /
  holdout); one tcn seed in-sample is clearly worse (0.22776 vs 0.22674), which is why the 3-seed ensemble is the reported
  model. Seed sd is below a sixth of the gap to log-HAR-t for gru (H-WS12-5).
- **No trading rule was derived from the forecasts**, so there is no deflated Sharpe for the deep part. The meta-label
  filter's deflated Sharpe is reported in dossier 3 and is not informative there.

## Caveats

- **Scope cut.** The original WS12 plan (7 architectures, 3 heads, 5 seeds, several refits) was compacted by the
  orchestrator to 2-3 architectures and 3 seeds. Not run: LSTM, Transformer, N-BEATS, TFT-lite, DeepAR, quantile and
  mixture-density heads, a per-config hyperparameter search beyond lr. The head comparison is therefore open.
- **Two refits only.** R1 forecasts a full year (2025-07..2026-06) without refitting, for the networks and for log-HAR-t
  alike; a monthly refit could favour either side.
- **Classical comparator.** WS04 / WS03 published no point-in-time forecasts on these origins when this was evaluated, so
  the comparator is the in-house log-HAR-t / base-t / empirical set (the PNN study's champion). GARCH-family and HAR-J /
  HARQ forecasts were not included.
- **Universe.** Today's liquid survivors (survivorship bias); pooled training has no asset id, so asset-specific shapes are
  left to the Student-t scale and df.
- **RV proxy.** Hourly squared returns (no 5m / 15m history for alts): the 1h QLIKE target is a single squared return, a
  very noisy proxy; QLIKE remains consistent for the ranking but power is low.
- **CRPS** is the 99-quantile approximation (exact up to the 1% / 99% tails).
- **H-WS12-4 as registered confounds architecture and inputs**: the registered feature MLP lacked the range, turnover,
  funding and OI inputs that the sequence models see. Its "contradicts" verdict stands as recorded, but the extra trial
  (H-WS12-9, same inputs) shows the difference was the inputs. Read H-WS12-4 together with H-WS12-9.
- **Extra trials after results.** harx and mlpx were designed after seeing H-WS12-1..5 (registered before fitting, labelled
  as extra trials). They add 2 trials to the family; the conclusions that rest on them (R-WS12-1, R-WS12-2) need a forward
  check (review clauses in the rule candidates).
- **Foundation model not run** (permission refusal of the venv install; see the verdicts). This leaves WS11's question open.
- **Closed trades only** for meta-labelling, which already avoids the inflation WS01 found in the stress holdout from open
  positions marked at the data end.
- **Meta-labelling** uses the stress streams' base variant (base costs) and closed trades; open positions at the stress
  as_of (43) are excluded. The DSR is not a meaningful filter test here (see dossier 3).

## Method

- **Data.** Bybit 1h last-price perps BTC, ETH, SOL, XRP, DOGE, BNB from the lake (`research.frame.market_frame`, bars with
  known_ts <= 2026-11-26 00:00 UTC, burn-in applied), funding and 4h open interest as-of joined; `ml.hourly.symbol_hourly`
  grid (a return only when the previous bar exists). 58k (BTC) to 44k (SOL) hourly rows per asset; cache 48 MB.
  Flow aggregates (flow_bars) start 2026-05 and are not inputs (no multi-year history). 15m bars exist only for BTC/ETH from
  2024, so RV is built from hourly squared returns.
- **Point in time.** Decision at the open of bar t; every input uses bars t-1 and earlier; the hour-of-week factors for
  hours t..t+23 are taken from the profile known at t (`ws12_deep.factor_ahead`, unit-tested for causality); OI and
  funding as-of their known_ts. Training rows end with labels (t + 24 h) at least one day before the refit; the inner
  validation block is the last 90 days of training dates with a one-day purge before it.
- **Networks.** Pooled over the 6 assets, no asset id. GRU 6.2k parameters, TCN 26.9k, MLP 3.3k (incl. heads). Loss =
  mean over horizons of Student-t NLL + QLIKE. AdamW wd 1e-4, batch 256, 16,384 random fit origins per epoch, early
  stopping (patience 6) on up to 16,384 inner-validation origins, max 40 epochs, grad clip 1, dropout 0.1, 2 CPU threads.
  lr {1e-3, 3e-3} chosen on inner-validation loss with seed 0 per arch and refit; seeds 1 and 2 at the chosen lr.
- **Classical.** Per asset and refit on the same training rows: base-t, log-HAR-t (OLS on [1, lsig2, lfac_h, har_d, har_w,
  har_m] for log max(v_h, 1e-6), QLIKE bias constant, Student-t MLE on y_h / sqrt(F_h)), emp (trailing quantiles).
- **Scores.** Vol-standardised units at non-overlapping origins (1h all, 4h at 00/04/../20 UTC, 24h at 00 UTC); pinball over
  7 taus (0.05..0.95); CRPS approximated as 2 x mean pinball over 99 taus; log score (negative log density; ensemble = equal
  mixture of the 3 seeds); QLIKE = v / F + log F; seed ensemble quantiles averaged, F averaged. Coverage of the 80% / 90%
  central intervals; KS distance of the PIT from uniform.
- **Tests.** Per-UTC-date mean loss over assets; differences against log-HAR-t and between models; Newey-West t (automatic
  Bartlett lag) times the Harvey-Leybourne-Newbold factor, one-sided p from t(n-1); MDE = 2.8 x se. MCS (Hansen-Lunde-
  Nason, Tmax, 10-day circular blocks, B 1000, alpha 0.10) per horizon x metric x segment. Holm over H-WS12-1..3 per
  segment. PBO by CSCV (16 blocks, mean per-date performance) over the 6 seed-0 candidates.
- **Segments.** llm_in_sample = origins 2025-07-01..2026-06-30 (R1 models), clean_holdout = 2026-07-01..2026-11-25 (R2
  models, trained only on data before the knowledge cutoff).
- **Meta-labelling.** `ws12_meta.py` docstring; features from `ws12_deep.build_symbol` rows at the entry bar.
- **Extra trials (H-WS12-8/9).** `ws12_deep.extra_feats` (xr1, xr6, xr24 = log Parkinson range variance of bar t-1 and the
  mean over bars t-6..t-1 / t-24..t-1 in units of s2_t; xturn, xfund, xdoi of bar t-1), `cmd_classical_x` (harx) and the
  `mlpx` arch; scored on exactly the same rows by `ws12_eval.evaluate_extra`; MCS over the 6 models gru, tcn, mlp, mlpx,
  har, harx.
- **Runtime and resources.** Training: gru 10-11 min per refit (4 runs), tcn 14-25 min, mlp and mlpx about 1 min for both
  refits; classical 4 min; evaluation about 5 min; meta-labelling 3 min features + 18 s fits. Governor waits up to 41 min
  per job (4 heavy slots shared by the program). Peak memory per job 970-1010 MB (cap 1200). Storage
  data/research/ws12: 200 MB (cache 48 MB, test-row predictions 150 MB, of which 87 MB are the 99-quantile arrays of
  the empirical baseline; models 1.6 MB).
