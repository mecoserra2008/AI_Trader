## Methods

**Data.** 11 days, frozen at the start: 2025-08-31 (in), 2025-09-05 (in), 2025-11-19 (in), 2025-12-18 (in), 2026-07-12 (ho), 2026-07-13 (ho), 2026-07-22 (ho), 2026-08-17 (ho), 2026-09-23 (ho), 2026-10-25 (ho), 2026-11-10 (ho). 347 symbol-days with at least 10,000 traded seconds, all symbols pooled. 2,860,668 test windows ({'llm_in_sample': 1071720, 'clean_holdout': 1788948}).

**Inputs.** There are 12 channels of 1 s bars, each normalised by trailing-1h statistics so that no future information enters:

- standardised return and log squared return;
- high-low range;
- signed flow, volume, prints, orders and order imbalance;
- where an L2 book exists: a has-book flag, spread and 10 bp depth (both relative to their trailing means) and 10 bp imbalance.

The input window is the last 300 s. The baseline is 60 x the trailing-1h per-second variance. The target is the next-1m last-trade return.

**Models.**

- CNN: a dilated 1D CNN (4 layers, 16 channels, receptive field about 31 s, pooled over the last 10, 60 and 300 positions plus a max) with a linear skip on 36 HAR aggregates. One seed.
- Linear: the same skip alone, i.e. 10, 60 and 300 s means of each channel.
- Both use the same optimiser and loss (QLIKE for H-NN5, BCE for H-NN6).

**Evaluation.**

- Three rotating leave-days-out folds; the validation set is the last training day.
  - fold 0: test ['2025-08-31', '2025-12-18', '2026-07-22', '2026-10-25'], validation 2026-11-10; 508,380 training / 981,036 test windows; CNN vol best epochs [2], linear [7]
  - fold 1: test ['2025-09-05', '2026-07-12', '2026-08-17', '2026-11-10'], validation 2026-10-25; 546,852 training / 989,280 test windows; CNN vol best epochs [2], linear [7]
  - fold 2: test ['2025-11-19', '2026-07-13', '2026-09-23'], validation 2026-11-10; 538,608 training / 890,352 test windows; CNN vol best epochs [6], linear [6]
- **Runtime:** training windows every 30 s (100k drawn at random per epoch, patience 2, one CNN seed), test windows every 10 s. Integrated gradients on 2,000 test windows per fold; channel ablation on test windows every 60 s.
- These are leave-days-out folds, not forward: training days can be later than test days.

## What this means for tool calls

- **Next-minute volatility:** the CNN beats the linear model on the same inputs only by a small margin (-0.8% QLIKE, t -6.78, day-clustered -2.69). It is better on 69% of symbol-days pooled, just under the 70% bar (72% in the holdout, 62% in-sample). The gain sits in symbol-days without an L2 book (74% better, t -8.5). For the 12 L2-book symbols it is a coin flip (51%, t -0.1). Both models beat the naive trailing-variance baseline by about 10x that margin (CNN -0.093, linear -0.083 per window). Most of the value is in the linear activity aggregates.
- **What the network looks at:** la (34%), z (11%), orders (11%), vol (10%) of the attribution. By lag: 0-9s 15%, 10-59s 29%, 60-299s 56%. The attribution is spread over the whole 5 minutes, with the 1-5 minute span carrying most of it: the network reads the recent activity LEVEL (squared returns, orders, volume, range), not a last-second trigger. Signed flow, order imbalance, spread, depth and book imbalance carry almost nothing (ablation of each costs < 0.005 QLIKE).
- **Next-minute direction:** CNN AUC 0.5051 [0.5037, 0.5065]; verdict supports. Direction is not predictable from 5 minutes of flow; do not call a flow-based direction tool.

## Caveats

- There are only 11 days (4 before the cutoff), and the folds are leave-days-out, not forward.
- Prices are last trades, so bid-ask bounce inflates the 1 s and 1 m returns of one-tick symbols in both models alike.
- The book channels exist only for the L2 symbols. BTC's 10 bp depth is a truncated lower bound, used here only relative to its own trailing mean.
- Neither model has a time-of-day input. The intraday profile (R-VOL-26) is a separate, validated adjustment.
- Training windows are subsampled (every 30 s, 100k per epoch), and the CNN is a single seed (runtime cut, made before any test result was seen).
