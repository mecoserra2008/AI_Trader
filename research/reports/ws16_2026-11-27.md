## In plain words

**What we now believe.**
- **The 4h trend edge is real enough to keep testing and too weak to trust.** On closed trades, with a bootstrap block that matches how long trend trades last (p90 32 days), the surviving unit `zoo_ew` (the 21-model zoo, equal weight) earns **+0.317 R/trade, 95% CI [-0.004, +0.701]** (2,170 trades; one-sided P(mean <= 0) = 0.026). This reproduces the red team's re-judgement exactly: **zoo_ew is FRAGILE (G6), not SURVIVES**. Its closed-trade holdout is **+0.099 R on 131 trades** (CI [-0.70, +0.87]): the holdout carries no information.
- **Nothing beats the plain equal-weight zoo.** Across 38 variants (other weightings, 15 single rules, thresholds, stops, exits, entry timing, order types, regime filters, conviction sizing) the best in-sample improvement is +0.10 R (2-sigma stop, not significant). **Hansen SPA p = 0.60, Romano-Wolf minimum p = 0.82** over the trade modifications, CSCV **PBO = 0.42** (picking the in-sample best is close to a coin flip out of sample). By the frozen selection rule, **spec 1 is zoo_ew unchanged**.
- **The edge lives in two places only: long trades and 2023-2024.** In-sample, zoo_ew longs earn +0.64 R/trade (972) and shorts +0.05 (1,067); holdout longs +0.72 (78), shorts **-0.81** (53). By entry year: 2021 +0.13, 2022 +0.00, **2023 +0.97, 2024 +0.59**, 2025 -0.04, 2026 +0.04. The last 23 months are flat. D8 majors +0.54, the other 15 symbols +0.18.
- **It is a tail strategy.** Hit rate 26%, median trade -1.0 R (55% of trades end at the stop), 95th percentile +4.9 R, 99th +13 R; the 5 best trades are 44% of the total; median hold 6.7 days, p90 32 days.
- **The signal's linear forecasting power is weak; the payoff comes from the trade layer's convexity.** The pooled IC of zoo_ew with the next 7-day standardised return is +0.028 (Driscoll-Kraay t 1.0) in-sample, and it changes sign by year (2022 -0.06, 2025 -0.04). tsmom_30d is the strongest single signal (IC +0.056, t 2.0). The 4-sigma stop plus "hold while the signal agrees" turns a tiny drift into a right-skewed payoff.
- **Against a null without multi-day persistence the trend edge is clearly there** (WS15 core, corrected): on 6 sources, zoo_ew's real mean +0.53 R/trade vs a stationary-block-bootstrap null with 1-day blocks (keeps fat tails and intraday vol clustering, destroys multi-day persistence): null mean -0.03, 95th pct +0.13, **MC p = 0.010**. So H-STRESS8's "the trend edge disappears off real history" came from the opposite flaw (30-day blocks keep real trends). The registered GARCH-t null was **rejected by its own stylised-fact check** (integrated fit, alpha + beta = 1.000; simulated 1h kurtosis ~1,300 vs 25 real); the capped version is pending.

**What changed.**
- zoo_ensemble "SURVIVES" is withdrawn: FRAGILE-positive, the best of a weak family. Several zoo re-weightings and tsmom_30d formally "SURVIVE" (CI lower bound just above 0), but none differs from zoo_ew (paired differences within +-0.04 R, all CIs include 0): those labels are noise at the CI edge across 110 trials.
- **R-GEO-1 stop placement does not matter under the 4-sigma cap**: a stop beyond the prior-day extreme changes only 24% of stops and the mean by -0.002 R [-0.027, +0.020]; the prior-week extreme changes 0.5% of stops.
- **Do not avoid the 12:00 UTC close (US-open vol peak)**: 12:00 entries were the best of the six closes (+0.71 R in-sample, +0.76 holdout; stop share 56% vs 51-59%); blocking them changes nothing (+0.001 R).
- **Pegged entries are right and costless in edge**: a limit at the decision close fills within 15 minutes for 92% of entries (5m bars, 1 bp trade-through); the unfilled 8% are *not* the runners (unfilled minus filled -0.18 R in-sample [-0.58, +0.24], -0.84 holdout). Taker entry costs 0.012 R/trade [0.008, 0.017].
- **No regime filter helps**: v0 "expected regimes" (R1/R2/R7/R8) -0.016 R, direction agreement -0.003, no-R6 +0.001, TradFi-weekend block +0.012 (all CIs include 0). The STRESS weekend slice (-0.09 vs +0.49) does not survive as a rule: blocked entries just happen on Monday.
- **Trailing stops hurt in-sample** (chandelier 4 sigma -0.27 R vs base) but were the best exit in the holdout (+0.30): a regime-dependent effect we cannot select on.

**What to use / stop using (proposals; sleeves unchanged).**
1. **Forward-test spec A on paper: `zoo_ew` exactly as below.** It is the pre-registered default and nothing beats it.
2. **Run spec B, `tsmom_30d`, in parallel as the simple benchmark** (selected by the frozen rule: highest in-sample lower CI bound among single signals).
3. **Replace the trend_4h bracket (ema_stack, 1.5 x HAR stop, 3R target) with spec A when the paper book is next changed**: cfg_ema_stack earns +0.171 R [-0.013, +0.388] in the same trade layer, -0.146 R vs zoo_ew (n.s.), holdout +0.002. Its EV gate currently refuses ~half its candidates on a null probability.
4. **Report long and short trend trades separately from day one**; if the short side is <= 0 after 100 closed forward trades, propose long-only (data-informed: not a registered test).
5. **Expectation:** +0.0 to +0.2 R/trade forward (central +0.1), about **5 engine trades/month** (30/month on the unconstrained 23-symbol stream), i.e. **cents to ~1 USD a month at 1.30 USD/R**; one-year drawdown at the engine median -15 USD, 5th percentile -31 USD; losing runs of 12 trades. A forward test **cannot prove the edge**: detecting +0.3 R/trade at 80% power needs ~1,100 closed trades (~3 years on the full stream). It can catch execution problems and a broken edge.

## Dossier 1: the trend signal

**Variable and decision.** The target is the forward vol-standardised log return of the perp over the holding horizon (y7 = sum of the next 42 4h log returns / (sigma_4h sqrt 42)); the signal decides entry side and timing of the trend sleeve.

**Empirical properties (one-bar target z = r_{t+1} / sigma_t, 4h, llm_in_sample, median over 23 symbols).** Stationary: ADF rejects a unit root for 23/23, KPSS does not reject stationarity for 23/23. Fat tails: excess kurtosis 10.1, Student-t nu 3.4 (scale 0.72), Hill alpha 3.4, skew -0.18. Serial dependence after vol scaling: Ljung-Box on levels (42 lags) rejects for 65% of symbols (small but real autocorrelation, the thing trend signals use), on squares for 35%, ARCH-LM for 35% (the sigma scaling removes most clustering). Calibration of the scale: mean z^2 = 1.20, and by sigma tercile 1.40 / 1.22 / 0.98: sigma_next under-forecasts in quiet states, so a "4-sigma" stop is about 3.4 true sigmas in low vol. Sampling: 4h native bars, listing burn-in; holdout 4.9 months.

**Specifications and assumptions.**

| spec | what it computes (4h bars) | assumptions | why plausible here | if true it implies |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| tsmom_k (tanh) | tanh(z_k), z_k = log(c_t/c_{t-k}) / (sigma sqrt k), k = 7/14/30 d | returns are positively autocorrelated at the horizon; the response is monotone in z and saturates; sigma_next is a calibrated scale | MOP 2012 and the in-sample zoo (tsmom_14d/30d best) | conviction is informative: size or threshold on |s| |
| tsign_k | sign(z_k) | only the direction is informative; a binary bet | MOP use the sign; magnitude may be noise once vol-scaled | magnitude is wasted; simpler signal, always in the market at theta < 1 |
| mac / ema stacks | SMA or EMA differences of log price (ema stack adds an alignment gate) | same persistence; smoother return weights; ema stack: the alignment of three horizons gates entry | classical; cfg_ema_stack is the paper sleeve's signal | the rule shape matters less than the horizon (trend_zoo: ~2 effective bets) |
| donch_n | close beyond the prior n-bar high / low, held until the opposite n/2 channel | price records mark regime starts; path-dependent state | turtle rules; crypto breaks prior extremes (GEO: PD/PW extremes extend) | breakout timing adds over linear filters |
| slope_k | OLS slope t-stat with random-walk-null SE | linear trend inside the window | triangular return weights, low turnover | equivalent to a smoothed TSMOM |
| kalman_llt | local-linear-trend filtered slope, gain adapted to sigma (H-KF1, q = 1e-3), tanh(slope / trailing 180 d sd) | latent slope is a random walk with Gaussian noise; signal-to-noise ratio q is constant; measurement noise = sigma^2 | state-space nests the moving-average filters; adaptive gain discounts loud bars | a model-based trend estimate beats ad-hoc filters |
| zoo_ew | equal-weight mean of the 21 zoo variants | variants are noisy measures of one latent trend; no weight estimation | 1/N is hard to beat (DeMiguel et al.); STRESS SURVIVES | diversification across horizons is the edge, not any one rule |
| zoo_iv / zoo_shr | inverse-variance / shrunk minimum-variance weights (monthly, trailing 365 d) | the P&L covariance is stable enough to estimate | MV weights reduce redundancy among 0.67-correlated variants | estimated weights beat 1/N |
| zoo_fam | equal weight per rule family | redundancy is by family, not by variant | tsmom has 5 members, ema 2 | family balance helps |
| zoo_vote | mean of variant signs | only directions matter | robust to one saturated variant | magnitudes are noise |
| zoo_slow* | 1/N over the 16 variants with horizons >= 7 d | fast variants are cost sinks | trend_zoo in-sample (data-informed) | lookback floor of ~1 week |

**Assumption checks.**
- *Persistence (positive IC):* pooled IC with y7, llm_in_sample (Driscoll-Kraay, lag 7): zoo_ew +0.028 (t 1.0), tsmom_30d +0.056 (t 2.0), slope_30d +0.049 (t 1.6), cfg_ema_stack +0.029 (0.9), donch_30 +0.026 (0.8), ema_4_16_64 +0.021 (0.6), kalman_llt +0.012 (0.6); holdout all |t| < 1. Only tsmom_30d reaches t 2.
- *Stability:* zoo_ew IC by year +0.044, +0.051, -0.059, +0.039, +0.006, -0.036, +0.051 (2020..2026): the sign flips in 2 of 7 years; no year has |t| > 1.
- *Linearity / sign vs magnitude (H-WS16-1):* jointly, sign(z) and tanh(z) coefficients are both insignificant at 7/14/30 d (tanh t 0.42 / 0.16 / 1.26); alone, sign and tanh are equivalent (30 d: t 2.08 vs 1.99). The binned response of y7 to zoo_ew is not monotone on the short side: s in [-1, -0.6) has mean y7 **+0.034** (strong down-trends bounce), s in [+0.6, +1] +0.10. **The data cannot tell sign from magnitude**; trade-layer tsign variants earn a third to a half of their tanh counterparts per trade only because they are always in the market (more trades, the same edge spread thin).
- *Vol scaling:* see the calibration above (z^2 1.4 in the low tercile).

**Out of sample and which to trust.** Paired trade-layer differences (in-sample) vs zoo_ew: every single rule is worse or equal (tsmom_30d -0.032, donch_30 +0.164 n.s., kalman_llt -0.226); re-weightings within +0.033 (zoo_iv, CI [-0.001, +0.077], p_RW 0.20). H-WS16-2 (1/N not beaten) holds; H-WS16-3 (Kalman no better than the 30-day slope) holds (-0.213). **The conclusions do not change across specs**: every medium/slow trend signal gives +0.2 to +0.5 R/trade in-sample and about 0 to +0.1 in the holdout; the data cannot rank them. Trust zoo_ew for its lack of selection (and lowest engine drawdown among the high-mean specs), tsmom_30d for simplicity.

## Dossier 2: the trend trade

**Variable and decision.** Net R per closed trade (stops, sizing, slot use) and its daily aggregate on the live_small engine (drawdowns, day stop).

**Properties (zoo_ew, in-sample, 2,039 closed trades).** Mean +0.331, sd 4.07, skew +9.8, excess kurtosis 133; hit 26%; quantiles 5/25/50/75/95/99%: -1.00 / -1.00 / -1.00 / +0.07 / +4.93 / +13.3. Trades cluster: 2.1 same-side entries per active day; daily R Ljung-Box p < 1e-30 (one bet across symbols, R-PD-1). Monthly R: 75 months, 47% positive, t 1.72; deflated Sharpe on the monthly series with N = 111 trials: **DSR 0.93** (PSR 0.99). By year 2023 and 2024 carry 594 of the 687 R.

**Trade specifications tested (one change each; paired vs zoo_ew, in-sample).** Threshold 0.2 / 0.5: +0.013 / -0.019. Stops 2 / 3 sigma (data-informed): +0.099 / +0.031 (2 sigma doubles the engine drawdown: max DD -57 R, 1y 5th pct -66 USD); prior-day / prior-week structure -0.002 / -0.003. Exits: hysteresis +0.035, flip-only +0.091, chandelier -0.265, 30-day time -0.024. Timing: no 12:00 entries +0.001, 1h delay +0.011, offset grid (02/06/.. UTC) +0.025 (bar alignment is irrelevant). Order: taker -0.012 (CI excludes 0), peg-skip +0.002, peg-chase +0.000 (2022+ window). Filters: -0.016 / -0.003 / +0.001 / +0.012. Conviction sizing (risk proportional to |s|): +0.015 [-0.008, +0.046]; slope of R on |s_entry| +4.0 R per unit (t 1.1): inconclusive.

**Power.** Per-trade sd ~4 R. The in-sample CI half-width with holding-matched blocks is ~0.35 R on 2,039 trades (effective n ~500). In the holdout (131 trades) the minimum detectable effect at 80% power is ~1.1 R/trade. Forward, +0.1 R needs ~10,000 trades, +0.3 R ~1,100.

**Capacity (H-WS16-14).** Square-root impact for 200 USD at the 5th percentile of in-sample 30-day median daily turnover: max 3.8 bp (XLMUSDT, 1.8 M USD/day in early history), 3.5 FIL, 3.3 UNI; all others below 3 bp. ## Caveats

- **Seen data.** The holdout of the trend family was read by trend_zoo (H-TR1) and STRESS before WS16; WS16 selected only on llm_in_sample, but its "clean" holdout is not clean for the family. Only the forward record counts.
- **One history, one bull market.** 2023-2024 make most of the P&L and the long side all of it; survivorship (universe = today's survivors) inflates long trend returns.
- **Engine figures use the full history** (in-sample dominated); recent years are flat.
- Stops fill at the stop price on 1h bars (STRESS caveats apply: next-open fills often help trend units); peg fills are approximated on 5m bars with a 1 bp trade-through; the 5m window starts 2022.
- The GARCH arm was changed after its stylised-fact check (labelled extra trial); the capped GARCH is still too fat-tailed (kurtosis 132 vs 25).
- Bugs found and fixed before any record: chandelier R denominated by the trailed stop, Kalman perturbation KeyError, capacity NaN, one-year drawdown axis.

## Method

- **Registration and freeze.** H-WS16-1..14 were appended to research/hypotheses.yaml (block WS16) and the design was frozen in the diary ("WS16 design frozen before results", 2026-11-26 ~23:55 UTC) before any data run. data/research/ws16/design.json repeats it. The STRESS and trend_zoo reports had already shown the holdout of zoo_ensemble, trend_4h, tsmom, ema_stack and donchian: every selection here uses llm_in_sample only, and the holdout is read once per hypothesis.
- **Data.** Bybit linear perps, universe L (23 symbols of trend_zoo_study.UNIVERSE), `stress.load_ctx` as of 2026-11-26T23:00Z: native 4h bars for signals, 1h bars for the stop path and the v0 cells, HAR daily sigma. 5m bars (BOS cache 2022-01 .. 2026-11-15 plus the GEO extension to 2026-11-25) for the peg fill model only. Segments split by entry date at 2026-07-01.
- **Signals.** The 21-variant zoo (strategies.trend_zoo, unchanged), TSMOM sign variants sign(z_k), the H-KF1 adaptive LLT Kalman slope (q = 1e-3), and the ensembles. zoo_iv / zoo_shr weights are monthly, from the trailing 365 days (>= 180 required, else 1/N) of each variant's pooled daily P&L s_{t-1} r_t / sigma_{t-1} (mean over symbols), known before the month starts; zoo_shr = 0.5 x (non-negative Ledoit-Wolf minimum variance) + 0.5 x 1/N.
- **Trade layer.** `trend_spec.walk`, tested to reproduce `stress.walk_signal` trade for trade in its base configuration (and under 5-minute latency): enter at the 4h close when flat and |s| >= 0.3, exit at the first close with s x side <= exit threshold, exchange stop 4 x sigma_risk_next (4h) from the fill checked on 1h bars (stop fills at the stop price; stop before anything else in the bar), no re-entry after a stop until the signal is flat or flips. Costs = live: pegged entry 2.0 + 1.3 bp, taker 5.5 + 2.0 bp; R = stress.r_net (the at-risk sizing budget). Variants change one thing each (Spec in trend_spec.py).
- **Structural stops.** Beyond the prior complete UTC day's low (long) / high (short) by 0.5 sigma_4h, used when its distance is within [1, 4) sigma_4h of the fill; else the prior ISO week's extreme on the same terms; else 4 sigma_4h.
- **Peg fill model.** A limit at the decision close fills if a 5m bar inside the next 15 minutes trades 1 bp through it. peg_skip: unfilled -> stay flat, the next 4h close decides again. peg_chase: unfilled -> taker at the 15-minute 5m close.
- **Statistics (red-team amendment).** Closed trades only (positions open at the data end are reported separately). Mean net R per trade with a stationary bootstrap of entry days, mean block = max(5, p90 holding days of the spec's in-sample closed trades) (zoo_ew 32 d), 2000 draws; paired tests use one common block (54 d, the largest over the specs). Paired differences: the same day draws for the variant and the base, ratio of sums per draw. Romano-Wolf stepdown (one-sided, studentised max) within families and over all modifications; Hansen SPA (consistent, g_c recentring) over all variants. Deflated Sharpe on the daily in-sample R series with N = WS16 specs + 23 STRESS units + 44 trend_zoo trials + H-TR1 (111), on the monthly series (the daily entry-day series is strongly autocorrelated) and V[SR] across the WS16 specs. CSCV PBO with 16 blocks over the in-sample daily R matrix. Predictive regressions: pooled OLS on the daily (00:00 UTC) panel with Driscoll-Kraay SE (Newey-West lag 7 over days).
- **Verdicts.** STRESS rules unchanged: FAILS if base mean <= 0 or fees x1.5 mean <= 0; FRAGILE if a +-20% one-at-a-time perturbation on D8 turns a positive D8 mean <= 0 (G1), 5-minute latency mean <= 0 (G2), a crisis window < -20 R on the live_small engine stream (G3), +5 bp per side <= 0 (G4), clean_holdout mean <= 0 with >= 20 trades (G5), base CI includes 0 (G6), total without the 5 best trades <= 0 (G7); plus the new G8 (real mean <= 95th percentile of the SBB(1-day block) null), applied where the null was run. In-sample verdicts skip G5 and use in-sample D8 and pre-cutoff crisis windows.
- **Live engine.** stress.live_engine with config/risk.yaml live_small (1.30 USD per R, 3 open, 2 same side, tiers, 10 USD daily stop); first come first served; trades per month and R per month on the accepted stream; one-year drawdowns from a stationary bootstrap of engine days.
- **Synthetic null (WS15 core).** Sources BTC ETH SOL XRP DOGE LINK, each at its full 1h length. (a) Stationary block bootstrap of whole relative 1h bars (return, gap, wicks) with mean block min(Politis-White on |r|, 24 bars): keeps fat tails and intraday vol clustering, destroys persistence beyond about a day. (b) Zero-mean GARCH(1,1)-t fitted per source by MLE (arch), standardised t innovations, wicks resampled in conditional-sigma units from real bars of the same return sign, zero open gaps. Per replication the statistic is the pooled mean net R/trade over the 6 sources; one-sided MC p = (1 + #null >= real)/(1 + reps). Stylised facts: excess kurtosis 1h and 1d, Hill tail index (top 2.5%), ACF of |r| at 1/24/168 h, ACF of r at 1, leverage corr(r_t, sum |r| next 24 h), 7-day variance ratio. Discriminator: logistic regression, 5-fold CV AUC on non-overlapping 30-day windows (10 features: 6 vol, 4 trend).
