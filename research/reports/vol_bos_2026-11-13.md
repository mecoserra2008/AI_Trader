## What worked

- A robust NEGATIVE pattern: after a realised-vol range break (5-20 day RV above its trailing 60-180 day 90-100% quantile) the trend ensemble earns LESS than its own mean over the next 5-20 days. Walk-forward in-sample range|1d -0.037/day (t -2.43), range|4h -0.075 (t -2.37); the frozen detector in the holdout -0.086 (t -2.97, 24 events, 1d) and -0.102 (t -1.59, 4h). Across the whole range grid 89-96% of configurations are negative in-sample and 89-100% in the holdout (descriptive, fig2). CUSUM alarms point the same way in-sample (cusum|4h t -2.33) but not in the holdout. This is the opposite of the registered direction, so it is a candidate to register, not knowledge.
- The nested purged walk-forward, DSR and PBO did their job: they show that every in-sample 'winner' here is a selection artefact (final strategy DSR 0.22 against SR0 0.68 for 992 trials; PBO 0.84; the WF picks switch family or frequency in 5 of 8 block transitions).
- The frozen machinery is reusable: causal detectors with quantile thresholds (unit-tested: appending bars never changes past flags, a planted x4 vol shift is caught within 5 bars by range, CUSUM and HAR), fractal BoS/CHoCH, and a trade builder that reproduces evaluate.strategy_pnl exactly.

## What did not

- H-VBOS1: vol BoS is not the timing filter trend was missing. No family x freq test is positive with t > 2 (best vov|4h +0.028/day, t 0.81; all Holm p = 1); the frozen vov|4h detector is negative in the holdout (-0.057, t -1.16).
- H-VBOS2: which semivariance side breaks first does not point the next 10 days. WF signed return 1d -0.077 sigma (t -1.04), 4h -0.037 (t -0.59). The dominant side agrees with the sign of the trailing return in 69-80% of events, so it is a momentum proxy, and short-horizon momentum after vol expansion does not continue (cf. R-VSIG-2). The 4h holdout bucket-weighted mean is +0.165 (t 1.20), which literally meets the holdout sign criterion, but the event-weighted mean is about -0.02 (up +0.005, down -0.060) and n is 45: the pooled verdict stays 'contradicts'.
- H-VBOS3: 'trend following with market-structure awareness' as specified (enter on a fractal BoS in the ensemble's direction while a vol BoS is active and the dominant semivariance agrees; ensemble or opposite-BoS exit) does not beat the plain ensemble. The in-sample final pick (4h, har_1.5_3, fractal k 3, semivariance on, own exits) has Sharpe 0.35 vs 0.44 for the unfiltered 4h ensemble on the same days at 43% exposure; holdout -0.46 vs -0.12. Its holdout Sharpe equals the median of 200 time-shifted gates, so the vol-BoS timing contributes nothing; the filter is an exposure cut (R-EXIT-2), and here it even cut the good days.
- The opposite-BoS exit hurt in-sample (ablation: 0.35 -> 0.13 when switched on), the same lesson as R-EXIT-1: structure-based exits are tight trails.
- H-VBOS4: thresholds are not stable. 2 of 4 holdout optima fall inside the in-sample top quintile (range|1d, semi|1d) and 2 do not; surface rank correlations in-sample vs holdout are -0.11 / +0.50 (range 1d / 4h) and +0.23 / -0.08 (semi); the split-half in-sample analogue also gives 2 of 4.
- TradFi T cannot test the selection: history starts 2026-04..08, so the 180-day trailing windows and the HAR fit (180-day minimum) never warm up; the frozen VBOS3 config never trades on T. Only the vov/CUSUM families fire on T (all |t| < 1.5).
- BTC alone is too thin for event-level selection (fewer than 5 ten-day clusters per half-year fold for most detectors); its per-symbol strategy pick had in-sample Sharpe 0.77 vs 0.59 but DSR 0.09 and walk-forward Sharpe 0.15 vs 0.65.

## Caveats

- The holdout is 135 days (2026-07-01 .. 2026-11-12) with few independent vol episodes; holdout event counts for the frozen detectors are 4-113.
- Holdout t-values rest on about 10-14 ten-day clusters, so their magnitudes (down to -8 for some range grid points) are overstated; read the holdout for sign and rough size only.
- Ten-day-bucket clustering with NW over buckets is a judgement call; event-weighted and bucket-weighted means differ materially when n is small (VBOS2 4h holdout).
- The ensemble's EW in-sample Sharpe here (4h 0.44, 1d 0.31 over 2021-01..2026-06) is below the trend-zoo report's 0.96 because each symbol starts after 200 warm-up days (the zoo includes 2020H2); the base itself reproduces data/priors/trend_zoo_daily_4h.parquet (monthly corr 0.997).
- Universe L is today's survivors; risk units, not dollars; 7.5 bps per side; no funding.
- The range-break finding is data-generated in sign (the registered direction was positive) and should be registered as its own hypothesis before it is used.
