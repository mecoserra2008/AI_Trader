# WS02 DATA: data quality and point-in-time audit of the lake (2026-11-27)

Specification program workstream WS02. Code: `src/ai_trader/research/data_quality.py` (tests: `tests/test_data_quality.py`).
Hypotheses H-WS02-1..12 were registered and the design frozen (diary 2026-11-26 23:38 UTC) before any row-level check. All results are recorded in
`research/hypotheses_results.jsonl` (segment `pooled`: integrity claims have no in-sample / holdout meaning). Lake state as of 2026-11-27 ~07:30 UTC.

## 1. Verdicts in plain words

**No look-ahead anywhere in the lake.** I found no row whose `known_ts` precedes its information in any table: bars (12 venue/price/freq streams,
4.75M rows), flow_3s (32.0M rows), funding, open interest, long/short ratio, liquidations, trades_raw (18.2M), instruments, flow_bars or the 61
alt_series series. FRED rows are real ALFRED vintages: 200 of 200 sampled as-of reads return the first release, not the revised value. These checks
are a full census (every row), except the FRED as-of sample, where 0 errors in 200 puts a 95% upper bound of 1.5% on the error rate.

**The REST-fed tables survive the PC suspends; the websocket-fed ones do not.** Bybit bars (last and mark, 1h/4h/1d) and funding have **zero holes**
from 2026-11-07 to 2026-11-26 for every live symbol, because the updater backfills after each wake. flow_3s, liquidations and the collector-written
book data exist for only about **half the wall-clock time**: BTCUSDT perp flow_3s covers 47% of the wall time from its first candle (11-07 17:46) to 11-26 (49.5% of the days that have a partition; 11-22 has none), and only 11-14 and 11-15 are complete
days. Liquidations cannot be recovered (Bybit has no REST history).

**The biggest cause of holes is not in the sleep log.** Windows *Modern Standby* (screen-off connected standby; Kernel-Power events 506/507) cut the
feed in 75 windows totalling 121 h over 11-01..11-26. The Power-Troubleshooter sleep log, which the desk and the diary used so far, only shows
the 25 full suspends (213 h). Masking only the logged suspends leaves most holes unexplained (registered test H-WS02-7 fails). The map now includes
both kinds, plus the 2026-11-11 Windows-Update reboot after which the collector stayed down for 25 h.

**Three collector defects that are not look-ahead but bias flow studies:**
1. *Silent trade-feed deficits.* On 1,253 linear symbol-hours (54 symbols, mostly 11-09/10/12/13/16/26), all 3 s rows exist but the taker volume is
   only 5-10% of Bybit's kline volume. The trade stream trickled while the book stream stayed alive, and the collector fills quiet candles, so row
   counts look complete. These hours are now flagged `kind = trade_deficit` in the gap map.
2. *Late trade attribution.* A trade whose exchange time falls in a candle that a later book message already closed is booked into the current, later
   candle. Against the public trade archive, BTC trade counts match per 3 s candle only 57% of the time even though the totals are identical
   (889,606 trades in both, 00:30-08:00 on 11-24). The cumulative lake-minus-archive count is never positive (minimum -1,837 trades). flow_3s trade
   timing is therefore late, by tens of seconds in bursts. The book fields are not affected.
3. *Clock skew.* 54,356 flow_3s rows (0.17%) were on disk up to 2.9 s before their `known_ts`. All of them have `known_ts` equal to the candle end on
   the exchange clock, so the local clock lags Bybit by up to ~2.9 s (median 0.46 s). This is the conservative direction. But any study that compares
   local-clock events (desk logs, fills) with exchange-clock `event_ts` at second resolution carries this skew.

**Survivorship is confirmed** (H-WS02-10). The 92 bars symbols are all trading today. Three USDT perps that traded on 2026-11-07 are already gone
(4STOCKUSDT, AGIUSDT, HAJIMIUSDT), and Bybit lists 318 closed USDT perps, none of which have bars. Every cross-sectional result is survivor-only
(relevant to WS09).

**What to use and what to stop using:**
- Use: `gaps.parquet` to exclude windows; bars, funding and OI as complete; FRED through `Lake.read_asof`, which is point-in-time correct.
- Stop: treating flow_3s row completeness as data completeness (join the `trade_deficit` and `empty_fill` kinds). Stop using flow_3s *trade* fields
  for sub-minute lead-lag or event timing. Use the archive-rebuilt candles (exact exchange time) or minute-or-coarser aggregates. Stop using the
  sleep log alone as the outage mask.

## 2. The gap map (for every workstream)

Files (live copy `data/research/ws02/`, committed copy `research/reports/ws02/`):
- `gaps.parquet`: one row per hole, with columns `dataset, stream, symbol, gap_start, gap_end` (UTC epoch ms, end exclusive), `n_missing, kind, cause,
  fillable_from, filled, duration_s, start_utc, end_utc`.
- `coverage.json`: per flow_3s category / symbol / day `[present, expected, empty_fill, awake_present, awake_expected]` 3 s candles; per-table stats
  (rows, span, PIT counts, duplicates, known_ts lags); machine windows; survivorship and FRED checks.
- `machine_windows.json`: 25 sleeps, 75 Modern Standby windows and 3 reboots (2026-11-01..11-26), each with `margin_end` = wake + 10 min.
- `fills_manifest.json`: the archive fills (§5).

`dataset` values:
- `machine`: symbol `*`. Times the PC was down; this is the master exclusion for anything collected live.
- `collector`: symbol `*`. BTCUSDT perp holes, used as a proxy for "collector down".
- `liquidations`: the same windows, unrecoverable.
- `flow_3s`: per symbol.
- `bars`, `funding`, `ls_ratio`, `flow_bars`: per symbol.

`kind` values:
- `missing_rows`: no row at all.
- `empty_fill`: ≥ 60 s of candles with no trade and no valid book, written by the collector's quiet-candle fill.
- `trade_deficit`: an hour where taker volume is below 90% of kline volume, scaled by the rows present.
- `machine_down`: a machine window.

`cause` values: `pc_suspend`, `modern_standby`, `reboot` (≥ 50% of the hole inside that window, including the margin), `collector_or_ws` (awake
crypto stream), `venue_session` (TradFi perp, closed session), `unknown`.

Join rule: drop any row whose `[event_ts, event_ts + bar)` overlaps a gap of the same `dataset` and `symbol`, or a `machine` / `collector` gap for
any live-collected dataset. For hourly studies on flow_3s, also drop `trade_deficit` hours.

## 4. Point-in-time violations found

None of the look-ahead kind. The registered checks that failed, and why they are not look-ahead:
- **H-WS02-3:** rows exist on disk before their `known_ts`, by at most 2.9 s, because `known_ts` is clamped to the candle end on the exchange
  clock. The data is labelled *later* than it physically arrived, which is conservative.
- **H-WS02-1:** off-grid Binance timestamps are an alignment problem, not timing: `known_ts = close + 2 s` still holds.
- **Open question for the owners of deribit_svi:** the 08:00 → 08:01 latency, listed in the card above.

## 5. Fills done (data/research/ws02/fills/, never the lake)

`flow_3s_trades/category=linear/date=<d>/<symbol>.parquet` holds trade-field 3 s candles rebuilt from public.bybit.com daily archives.
- Coverage: BTC, ETH, SOL, XRP, DOGE, HYPE and BNB perps on 2026-11-20, 21, 22, 23, 24 and 25 (42 symbol-days, 39 MB). These are the highest-value
  holes; the full-day 11-22 partition is missing and 11-23 has 4% coverage.
- Columns: `ev`, OHLC, `vwap`, buy/sell volume and notional, `n_buy`, `n_sell`, `max_trade`, `kn`, `symbol`, `pit_quality = backfill_archive`.
  The full UTC-day grid is included, with close carried forward.
- Rule: `known_ts = candle end + 500 ms` (the trades_raw backfill rule).
- Book fields are absent. The ob200 quote-saver archive would add them, but it is ~hundreds of MB per symbol-day; I did not download it.
- Validity (H-WS02-9): the archive is exchange-time exact. On covered windows its totals equal flow_3s, but the 3 s assignment differs because
  flow_3s is late. Use the fills inside `gaps.parquet` holes, and prefer them over flow_3s trade fields for timing work.
- Raw download: 1.82 GB in total, each file deleted right after processing (largest single file about 160 MB; transient raw never exceeded 0.2 GB).
  `fills_manifest.json` lists every file with its source URL.

## 6. Proposed lake repairs and code fixes (not applied: the lake belongs to the collector and updater)

1. **Collector (crates/at-flow).** Close a candle on a watermark, for example once both the trade stream and the book stream have passed its end,
   or once the wall clock has passed its end plus the grace period. Do not close it on the first later book message. This fixes late trade
   attribution. Also emit a per-stream heartbeat, so that a trickling trade feed (`trade_deficit`) is detectable live.
2. **Ops.** Disable Modern Standby / network standby on the desk PC, or have the collector reconnect on resume. Auto-start the collector after
   reboots (11-11: 25 h lost). Run `flow maintain` (compaction), which never ran: one day has up to 1,734 files.
3. **Lake repair (updater owner).** Compact the exact duplicates. Drop or flag BCHUSDT 1d 2020-12-14 (the zero open). Re-fetch the 23 open-bucket
   OI and ls_ratio rows. Snap or drop the 43 off-grid Binance ETH 1h bars. Optionally import the WS02 archive fills as a separate `flow_3s_trades`
   table with `pit_quality = backfill_archive`; this needs a schema change by the owner.
4. **Daily instruments snapshot.** It stopped on 11-07. It is needed for survivorship (WS09).
5. **Stale series** to add to the updater or drop from studies: index 1h, ls_ratio, OI 1h, flow_bars, stablecoins, Deribit funding, deribit_svi.

## 7. Caveats

- The machine windows come from the Windows event log, so their 10-minute reconnect margin is a guess. Holes shorter than one candle inside awake
  time are not mapped.
- Holes are found on the 3 s grid from a symbol's first candle. A stream removed from the config later shows its tail as `collector_or_ws`.
- H-WS02-7's post hoc variant (adding Modern Standby) is an extra, labelled trial. The registered verdict stands.
- H-WS02-8 and H-WS02-9 compare two exchange outputs, kline and archive. Kline volume could itself include prints the websocket never sends;
  nothing suggests it does, since the archive and flow_3s totals agree exactly.
- The `trade_deficit` threshold (90%) was set after seeing H-WS02-8. It is a mask, not a test.
- The bars-holes check depends on the updater's view on 2026-11-26/27. A later re-fetch could change values (no revision is visible: 0 revised keys).

## 8. Method, data, storage, runtime

- **Method.** DuckDB scans of every lake partition (per year, day or series, capped at 0.45-0.7 GB), Polars per symbol, all under the governor. PIT
  checks are pure row predicates. A file's write time comes from the `part-<written_ms>-` name. Duplicates are classified as exact (same key,
  `known_ts` and values), conflicting (same key and `known_ts`, different values) or revision (different `known_ts` and values). Holes are runs off
  the expected grid. Causes come from the Windows System log (Power-Troubleshooter 1; Kernel-Power 506/507; Kernel-General 12/13). Comparisons were
  made against Bybit 1h klines and the public.bybit.com archive.
- **Dossier note.** WS02 models no target variable. The §5 dossier is replaced by the data cards, and power by census. Every "0 violations" claim is
  exhaustive, except FRED as-of (0/200, 95% upper bound 1.5%).
- **Storage.** data/research/ws02: 0 → 40 MB (fills 39 MB, gap map 41 kB, coverage 0.19 MB, instruments listing 0.85 MB). Raw: peak about 0.2 GB,
  now 0. Quota 1 GB.
- **Runtime.** flow_3s scan 89 s (peak 1.55 GB). The table scan needed five attempts: three out-of-memory failures at caps of 0.7-0.9 GB before I
  moved aggregation into DuckDB per partition, and one crash on a pre-1970 timestamp. The successful runs took 652 s and 641 s (FRED as-of). Fills
  367 s. Governor waits about 15 min, all heavy slots busy; then I moved to the light pool.
- **What went wrong.**
  - I killed my own queued and over-cap jobs three times.
  - A Python `fromtimestamp` failure on pre-1970 FRED dates.
  - `"hour"` is a reserved word in DuckDB.
  - The API session limit paused work from ~00:40 to 07:12 UTC. The early gap map (commit d2d5805, 00:14 UTC) had flow_3s only and preceded the
    Modern Standby labelling of the table gaps.
