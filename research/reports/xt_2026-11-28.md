# XT: BlackBull CFDs on cTrader against Bybit perps, tick by tick (stage 1, 2026-11-28)

Agent XT. Research only. No orders were placed, amended or cancelled anywhere. Bybit was read only through public endpoints. cTrader was read only through `src/ai_trader/ctrader/mcp_ro.py` (get_symbols, get_symbol_details, get_symbol_sessions, get_spot_prices, get_trendbars, get_server_time); no other tool on that server was called. SPYUSDT was never touched. The registered hypotheses are H-XT-1..8. **No verdicts yet**: the registered forward sample runs from 2026-11-29 to 2026-12-12.

## 1. Plain words first

**What we can get.**
- **Bybit, everything public.** The new recorder `scripts/xt_bybit_l1.py` streams top of book (orderbook.1) and trades for the 9 Bybit legs over the public websocket. In the 10-minute smoke test it had no reconnects and no gap over 6 s on any symbol. BTC top of book changed about 19 times per second, ETH about 23. The public archives give full 200-level books (quote-saver ob200) and every trade (public.bybit.com) for all 9 symbols, published a day later. 12 days (2026-11-16..27) are compacted under `data/research/xt/bybit_hist/`: one row per top-of-book change and one row per trade.
- **cTrader, through the local app's MCP.**
  - Symbol details (digits, lot size, volume steps) and weekly sessions for all 16 CFDs are in `data/research/xt/ctrader_map.json`.
  - The server clock agrees with this PC to about 1 ms.
  - get_spot_prices answers in 15 ms (p50; p90 25 ms) at any rate we tried, up to the single-connection maximum of 89 requests/s.
  - m1 trendbars for every symbol back at least 14 days. These are **bars, not ticks**: bid OHLC plus a tick count per minute.
- **What cTrader cannot give us this way.**
  1. **No tick history**: the MCP has no tick tool.
  2. **No quote timestamps**: get_spot_prices returns bid and ask only. Our rows carry local send and receive times, and the quote's true time lies between them.
  3. **Live quotes only for subscribed symbols.** A symbol has to be open in a chart or watchlist of the app. Today that holds for XAUUSD, NAS100, BRENT, UKBRENT.F, US500 and SPX500. **BTCUSD, ETHUSD**, XAGUSD, Copper, QQQ, QQQ-24, USTEC.F, WTI, USOIL.F and US500.F answer "No live quote ... unsubscribed".
  4. **No commission or swap terms**: the MCP symbol details do not expose them.
  5. **No ask-side bars.**

**What went wrong.** The planned pilot was sampled cTrader quotes for BTC and ETH against Bybit. Those two CFDs are not subscribed in the app, and I may not touch watchlists. So the cTrader recorder ran its 10 minutes and recorded nothing for them (the subscribed TradFi CFDs are shut on Saturday). It parks such symbols and retries every 60 s, so they are picked up as soon as they are opened. The pilot's first look at the gap therefore uses cTrader m1 bars (minute bid) against Bybit's best bid.

**Recorder rates.**
- **Bybit**: BTC 18.6 book changes/s and 25.6 trades/s; ETH 23.2 and 14.1; the TradFi perps 1-13 book changes/s on a Saturday. Messages arrive about 120 ms after Bybit's matching-engine time, once the measured clock offset is corrected (Bybit's clock is 147 ms ahead of this PC).
- **cTrader**: chosen rate 20 get_spot_prices/s, round robin over the symbols with a live quote. Latency was flat at every rate, and the app's added CPU stayed at or under 3.5% of one core up to 20/s. The measured ladder is in section 3.
  - With 2 live symbols (a crypto weekend), each is polled every 0.1 s. With all 16 live, each is polled every 0.8 s.
  - From the CFDs' own tick counts (bar volume), a simple Poisson model says the poller sees about 70-80% of BTCUSD / ETHUSD quote changes with 2 live symbols. With all 16 live it sees 13-68%, depending on the symbol.
  - **Sampled quotes are a lower bound.** The cBot below records every tick.

**Pilot numbers.** These are a first look at minute bars. They are descriptive only and test nothing.
- **Crypto:** after the USDT/USD correction (Bybit quotes in USDT, which sat 7-10 bp from USD), the BTC and ETH gaps sit at about 0 bp: BTC median -1.0 bp, IQR -1.9..+0.1; ETH -0.3 bp, IQR -1.7..+1.3. Raw they are +9.5 and +10.3 bp, which is the USDT basis.
- **TradFi:** Bybit's perps quote **17-22 bp above** the CFDs, every day (12 days, 11-16..27):
  - gold +18.5 bp, IQR 17.0..19.6;
  - silver +21.8;
  - QQQ +17.5 (and +17.2 against QQQ-24).
- **Oil:** large roll bases.
  - BZUSDT sits about 4% below the BRENT cash CFD and 2.8% below the UKBRENT.F futures CFD. UKBRENT.F rolled on 11-26/27; the gap was +14 bp after the roll.
  - CLUSDT sits about 1.4% below the WTI cash CFD, and that gap is drifting toward zero. Against USOIL.F it was -1.3% until the futures CFD rolled around 11-18/19, then +15 to +24 bp.
- **Noise vs costs:** the gap's minute-to-minute noise is 0.7-2.9 bp. The round-trip hurdle is about 6-9 bp for TradFi and about 12 bp or more for crypto.
- **What this means:** at minute resolution nothing looks like a convergence arbitrage, and the TradFi levels look like a carry question (Bybit funding against the CFD swap), not an arbitrage. Sub-minute crossings are exactly what the registered study measures, and minute bars can't see them.

**The registered plan.** Hypotheses were registered before the analysis. H-XT-8 was added after this first look and before the forward sample, as a labelled extra trial.
- H-XT-1: gap stationarity around a pair-specific basis.
- H-XT-2: lead-lag. Hayashi-Yoshida, plus Hasbrouck / Gonzalo-Granger information shares at 1 s.
- H-XT-3: raw executable crossings beyond all costs, lasting longer than 1.32 s.
- H-XT-4: P&L of a latency-delayed convergence trade.
- H-XT-5: Bybit's weekend price predicts the CFD's reopen.
- H-XT-6: at the reopen, Bybit converges to the CFD.
- H-XT-7: poller coverage.
- H-XT-8: basis-adjusted crossings. Added because the persistent TradFi premium and the oil roll bases would count as raw crossings under H-XT-3 without ever converging.

Sample: 2026-11-29..12-12 forward, all clean_holdout. H-XT-5/6 use every available weekend of minute bars, split at 2026-07-01.

**What true historical ticks would need.** cTrader Open API credentials in `.env`, added by the user:
- Required: CTRADER_CLIENT_ID, CTRADER_CLIENT_SECRET, CTRADER_ACCESS_TOKEN and CTRADER_ENV (live or demo).
- Optional: CTRADER_REFRESH_TOKEN and CTRADER_ACCOUNT_ID.

`target/debug/atx ctrader check` lists which ones are missing without printing values. `atx ctrader ticks` (ProtoOAGetTickDataReq, about 4.5 requests/s) then pulls bid and ask tick history for any past day, and the Bybit side of those days is already compacted here. Until then, the TickRecorder cBot (section 5) records true ticks forward.

## 5. TickRecorder cBot (research/xt/TickRecorder.cs): true ticks, for the user to run

The cBot subscribes to each listed symbol itself, so it also fixes the "unsubscribed" problem. It never trades and never reads account fields. It writes one CSV per symbol and UTC day: `<folder>\<SYMBOL>\<yyyy-MM-dd>.csv` with `tick_ms,event_server_ms,local_ms,bid,ask,spread`. `local_ms` is this PC's clock, the same clock as the Bybit recorder's `recv_ts`.

1. cTrader Desktop: **Algo** tab, then **cBots**, then **New**. Name it `TickRecorder`, paste the file over the template and click **Build** (Ctrl+B).
2. Add an instance on any chart (for example BTCUSD m1). Set the parameters:
   - **Symbols**: defaults to all 16 names in section 2.
   - **Output folder**: for example `C:\Users\Asus\Desktop\AI_Trader\data\raw\XT\ctrader_ticks`.
   - **Flush every (s)**: default 5.
3. Run it **locally**, not as a cloud instance. Accept the file-system access request.
4. Leave it running. Stop it to close the files; a restart appends.

I will compact the CSVs into `data/research/xt/` and delete them. I have not built or run it, and I called no plugin tool. One caveat: `tick_ms` is the last tick of the symbol's tick series, read when the Tick event fires. If the series updates after the event, it can be one tick behind. `local_ms` and `event_server_ms` are exact for the event.

- Copper: cTrader-only, descriptive.

**Sample.**
- Forward 2026-11-29 00:00 to 2026-12-12 24:00 UTC, all clean_holdout. The verdict rests on the declared one-sided test in clean_holdout alone, because no llm_in_sample tick data exist.
- A pair-day counts if both legs quoted in at least 4 h of common live seconds (crypto: 12 h). A verdict needs at least 5 such days per pair.
- H-XT-5/6 (weekends) use cTrader m1 bars plus Bybit 1 m klines over every available weekend through 2026-12-13, split at 2026-07-01, with at least 8 weekends per segment.

**Alignment.**
- 1 s grid of each leg's last quote, valid for at most 5 s, with both legs live and the CFD in session.
- Local recv_ts on both legs (one clock). As a sensitivity: Bybit cts and cBot server time, corrected by the logged offsets.
- gap = 1e4 ln(mid_bybit / mid_ctrader). USDT legs (BTCUSDT, ETHUSDT, XAUTUSDT) are corrected by 1e4 ln(USDCUSDT); raw and corrected gaps are both reported.

**Costs.**
- Bybit taker: 2.75 bp per fill (TradFi) or 5.5 bp (crypto), from config/fees.yaml.
- CFD commission: 0.3 bp per fill (sensitivity 1.0 bp).
- Exit cost: the trailing 60 min median of both spreads.
- Holds: funding pro rata, plus a swap if the hold crosses 17:00 ET.
- Persistence: longer than 1.32 s (sensitivity 21.2 s).

| id | question | test | success rule (one-sided 5%) | prior |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| H-XT-1 | gap stationary within the day around a pair basis | ADF + KPSS + AR(1) half-life < 300 s per pair-day | share of passing pair-days > 50% (exact binomial), Holm over 11 pairs | 0.6 |
| H-XT-2 | Bybit leads for crypto, CFD leads for TradFi and the proxies | Hayashi-Yoshida xcorr (lag grid +-10 s), VECM Hasbrouck IS bounds + GG share at 1 s, hour / day block bootstrap | peak-lag CI and IS-midpoint CI both on the registered side; leads shorter than one poll interval are unresolved with sampled quotes | 0.6 |
| H-XT-3 | raw executable crossings beyond all costs, lasting > 1.32 s | episodes on the 1 s grid (and event times with cBot ticks) | mean (f_d - 0.1%) > 0 by Wilcoxon, median net >= 1 bp; a "none seen" from sampled quotes is not a contradiction | 0.15 |
| H-XT-4 | latency-delayed convergence trade pays | enter 1.32 s after detection at the touch, exit at half gap / 60 min / session end; day-clustered bootstrap | >= 30 episodes, lower 95% bound > 0 pooled and for the best pair | 0.1 |
| H-XT-5 | Bybit's weekend return predicts the CFD's reopen | OLS of r_cfd on r_bybit across weekends | slope > 0.5 and R^2 >= 0.5 in clean_holdout, slope > 0.5 in llm_in_sample | 0.55 |
| H-XT-6 | at the reopen Bybit converges to the CFD | halving share within 30 min; share of the closing from the Bybit leg | both > 0.5 in clean_holdout and llm_in_sample | 0.6 |
| H-XT-7 | the 20 req/s poller sees most quote changes | recorded changes / bar tick count per symbol-minute (cBot ticks where available) | median ratio >= 0.5 with lower bound >= 0.4 | 0.5 |
| H-XT-8 (extra trial) | basis-adjusted crossings (vs the trailing 60 min median gap) beyond costs, lasting > 1.32 s | as H-XT-3 with y = crossing minus the point-in-time level | as H-XT-3 | 0.1 |

**Sampling caveat, fixed in advance.** Polled quotes understate crossings shorter than the per-symbol poll interval (n_live / 20 s), and their times are late by up to one interval. So sampled-quote crossing counts are lower bounds, and lags shorter than an interval are not read.

## 7. Caveats

- **Bars are not ticks.**
  - The cTrader history here is m1 trendbars: bid-based OHLC with a tick count. The minute gap compares bid with bid at the minute end, so it carries the half-spread difference of the two venues and misses everything inside the minute.
  - The time of the last tick in a bar is unknown; with 1-9 ticks/s it is usually under a second before the minute end.
- **Sampled quotes are not ticks** (`ctrader_quotes_SAMPLED/README.txt`).
  - Rows are written only when bid or ask changed between two polls of the symbol.
  - A change that reverts between polls is invisible.
  - The true quote time lies between send_ts and recv_ts.
- **Unsubscribed symbols.** get_spot_prices answers only for symbols open in the app. Until the user opens them, or runs the cBot, the forward sample will lack the cTrader leg of BTC, ETH, silver, copper, QQQ / QQQ-24, WTI / USOIL.F, USTEC.F and US500.F. Pair-days without both legs do not qualify, so verdicts may come back as "insufficient sample".
- **Clocks.**
  - Bybit: about +150 ms ahead of this PC (one sample set). The offset is logged hourly, so cts can be mapped to local time.
  - cTrader get_server_time agrees with the local clock to about 1 ms. That may be the app's synchronised clock, not the broker's. Aligning on local receive time avoids needing either offset for the polled data.
- **Costs not visible via the MCP.** The CFD commission and swaps are assumptions (0.3 bp per fill, with a sensitivity). The USDCUSDT correction uses 1 m kline closes, which have 1 bp tick resolution.
- **Basis vs arbitrage.** A persistent level, such as the 17-22 bp TradFi perp premium or the oil roll bases, is a carry trade (funding against the CFD swap), not an arbitrage. That is why H-XT-8 exists. Read any H-XT-3 support together with H-XT-8 and H-XT-4.
- **Holidays.** The sessions payload carries no holidays. Thanksgiving (11-26) and the 11-27 early close show up as missing bars.
- **Storage of the forward run.**
  - Measured on Saturday: book 9.2 B per row, trades 6.3 B per row (after the compaction changes).
  - Weekday rates are likely 1.5-2x the weekend rates. Projection for 15 days: about 0.8-1.6 GB for the Bybit book, plus about 0.3-0.6 GB of trades if the BTC / ETH trades are kept. The recommended start line skips them; the public archive has them with exact exchange times. cTrader adds well under 50 MB.
  - Together with the 12-day history (0.55 GB compacted, measured) this is at the edge of the 2 GB quota. If space is short, the history is reproducible from the public archives and can be deleted first.

## 8. Method and files

**Code.**
- `src/ai_trader/research/xt.py` (library):
  - the pair map and SPY refusal;
  - `session_open` for get_symbol_sessions payloads (Windows time-zone names mapped to IANA);
  - `ChunkSink`: rows go to part files (every 20k rows or 60 s), then to one parquet per UTC hour, sorted by symbol and time with zstd 9. Parts of a crashed run are compacted at the next start;
  - a stdlib RFC 6455 websocket client (the main environment has no websocket package);
  - orderbook.1 top-of-book state (keep-alive re-sends flagged `r`) and the trade parser;
  - the gap, Hayashi-Yoshida cross-correlation and episode helpers.
- `scripts/xt_bybit_l1.py` (recorder A): one process with one helper thread (the hourly clock sample); Polars capped at 1 thread. Governor peak: 165 MB over the 10-minute smoke test with the original 5-minute buffers, and 64 MB in a 50 s check after the buffers were cut to 60 s (the Polars import alone is 41 MB). The cTrader recorder peaked at 52 MB.
- `scripts/xt_ctrader_quotes.py` (recorder B): one thread; paced round robin; on-change rows; per-minute poll statistics; hourly clock samples. Symbols out of session are polled every 30 s and unsubscribed ones every 60 s; if the app is closed it backs off from 5 s to 5 min.
- `scripts/xt_study.py`: `map`, `probe`, `hist` (ob200 to a top-of-book change series, trades compacted, raw files deleted as soon as each is processed), `bars` (m1 trendbars), `pilot`.

- Tests: `tests/test_xt_recorders.py` (11 tests, run through the governor light pool). **Data.** Raw downloads went to `data/raw/XT/` and were deleted after each symbol-day.
