# Leverage effect and volatility asymmetry (H-LEV1..H-LEV5)

Generated by `ai_trader.research.leverage_study` as of 2026-11-10T22:46:27.701658+00:00. Data: Bybit 1h bars -> daily realised measures (`features.realized`); Bybit funding (as of). Program: research/plans/2026-11-10_volatility_program.md.

## Methods

Leverage effect / volatility asymmetry block (H-LEV1..H-LEV5, program 2026-11-10).

Design (fixed before any result was seen; diary 2026-11-10 "A1 leverage block ... design fixed before results"):

Data
* Daily measures from 1h bars with the shared definition `ai_trader.features.realized.daily_measures` (complete UTC
  days only; rv, rs_neg, rs_pos, ret, close). Bars come from `research.frame.market_frame` (price sanity, listing
  burn-in). Each symbol's daily series is reindexed to the full calendar, so every rolling window (RV_w = mean of
  days t-4..t, RV_m = t-21..t, 30-day drawdown window) needs consecutive complete days; gaps give nulls, never a
  row-based window that silently spans a hole. Target = RV of day t+1 (must exist).
* Origin (forecast / decision time) of row t = end of day t = its known_ts. Segments by origin vs
  research.knowledge_cutoff: llm_in_sample (origin < cutoff), clean_holdout (origin >= cutoff), pooled (all).
  One-day targets never straddle the cutoff.
* Universe L = the 23 trend-zoo crypto perps. Universe T = Bybit TradFi perps with >= 120 complete days.
  H-LEV1 equity/index subset = T ∩ {TSLA INTC GOOGL AAPL MSTR NVDA MU QQQ SOXL SPCX}. External index series are
  used only if already in data/lake/alt_series (they are not: `index_series_available()`).

Models
* Primary SHAR (Patton and Sheppard 2015), levels:  RV_{i,t+1} = a_i + b- RS-_it + b+ RS+_it + b_w RV_w + b_m RV_m.
  Every RV-type variable of a symbol is divided by that symbol's mean RV in the estimation sample (unit-free,
  equal weight per symbol). Symbol fixed effects by within-demeaning; CR1 standard errors clustered by origin
  date. Test: b- - b+ (t from the clustered covariance; p from Student t with G-1 df).
* Log SHAR (robustness): log RV_{t+1} on log(RS-), log(RS+) (each floored at 1% of RV_t, as H-VCL2), log RV_w,
  log RV_m. Other robustness: Driscoll-Kraay SE (date-summed scores, NW lag), target-weekday dummies (TradFi
  weekends are quiet), and for H-LEV2 a 4h SHAR (4h semivariances from 1h bars, HAR lags 6/30/132 blocks,
  target-hour dummies).
* GJR-GARCH(1,1)-t (`arch` 8.0, constant mean, daily close-to-close log returns in percent, robust SE): gamma;
  EGARCH(1,1)-t asymmetry term as robustness (leverage <=> EGARCH gamma < 0). Per asset per segment with >= 250
  daily returns, otherwise n/a; full-sample fits of shorter TradFi series are exploratory and never used in a
  verdict.
* H-LEV3: D_t = 1{ln(close_t / max close_{t-29..t}) < -1.5 * std(ret_{t-29..t}) * sqrt(30)}. Primary = SHAR +
  RS- x D + RS+ x D (as registered); robustness adds D.
* H-LEV4: pooled panel log-HAR (log RV_d, log RV_w, log RV_m) vs log-SHAR with symbol FE, expanding window, refit
  every 30 days at 00:00 of the refit day on rows whose target day is already complete; lognormal mean
  correction exp(s2/2). First fit when the pool has >= 250 rows over >= 60 origin dates; a symbol is forecast
  once it has >= 30 training rows (its FE). Levels (scaled by the training mean, floored at 1% of it) as
  robustness. Paired QLIKE(SHAR) - QLIKE(HAR) averaged by origin date, Newey-West t.
* H-LEV5 (universe L): levels HAR (RV_d, RV_w, RV_m) + RS- + fz + RS- x fz; fz = (latest funding print known at
  the origin - mean) / std over prints with known_ts in the trailing 90 days (>= 90 prints), clipped to +-5.
* Holm within the LEV block, per segment, over each hypothesis' primary test(s); raw and adjusted p reported.
  Verdicts apply the registered success criteria literally (Holm survival is reported next to it).

## Interpretation

- **No leverage effect anywhere in this data.** In crypto (L, 23 perps, 2,252 in-sample dates) a unit of downside semivariance predicts *less* next-day RV than a unit of upside semivariance (b- 0.26 vs b+ 0.29, diff t -0.28). That holds at 4h (t -0.04), in logs (t +1.30) and with calendar dummies. Median GJR gamma is 0.004 in-sample (13/23 positive, none with t > 2, 2 with t < -2), and EGARCH agrees (median -0.002). Holdout points the same way, slightly inverted (diff -0.20, t -1.57). This repeats H-VCL2 / BELIEFS V8 with panel power: **H-LEV2 is supported in all segments.**
- **The TradFi equity perps do not show the textbook asymmetry either.** In-sample has only 18 dates, so it is inconclusive. In the holdout (132 dates, 9 perps) the level SHAR gives b+ 0.51 > b- 0.23 (t -2.11, DK t -2.14). That is the sign *opposite* to the leverage claim, so the registered verdict is "contradicts". It is fragile, though: in logs the difference is +0.02 (t 0.22), and with target-weekday dummies it is t -1.79. Read it as "no detectable leverage effect", not as an established inverse effect. GJR is n/a (no equity perp has 250 days). Exploratory full-sample GJR gammas are positive for 8/9 assets but none is significant (max t 1.54). No equity index series exist in the lake, so the long-history check could not be run.
- **Drawdown state does not switch on an asymmetry (H-LEV3).** Crypto RS- x D is +0.10 (t 0.59) in-sample and +0.03 (t 0.24) in the holdout. TradFi RS- x D is +0.61 (t 1.28; 33 drawdown dates) in-sample and -0.17 (t -2.20) in the holdout: the sign flipped, so the verdict is "contradicts".
- **Semivariances don't help the panel forecast out of sample (H-LEV4).** On the primary log form, SHAR is slightly *worse* than HAR in both universes: L dq +0.0025 (t 1.95) in-sample and +0.0022 (t 1.00) in the holdout; T +0.0056 (t 2.22) in the holdout. On the level form, SHAR is marginally better for crypto (dq -0.0017, t -2.27 in-sample; -0.0008, t -2.00 holdout). That is about 0.4% of QLIKE, economically negligible. T fails its registered condition (SHAR better with t < -2), so the holdout verdict is "contradicts".
- **Crowded funding doesn't amplify down days (H-LEV5).** The RS- x funding-z interaction is *negative*: -0.050 (t -2.30) in-sample, -0.103 (t -1.23) in the holdout. Funding z on its own does raise next-day RV, by +0.069 per z unit (t 2.36) in-sample and +0.153 (t 2.71) in the holdout, in units of the symbol's mean RV. That main effect was not the registered test, so it is exploratory: it needs its own pre-registered hypothesis before it becomes more than a C-grade rule.
- **Holm:** no positive-direction test survives in any segment. The smallest adjusted p is 0.28 (H-LEV4 L, pooled).

## Caveats

- TradFi perps start Mar-Jun 2026, so their evidence is almost all clean_holdout (132 dates). PAXG and XAUT carry most of the T in-sample rows for H-LEV3 and H-LEV4. Every T result is confidence C until the holdout roughly doubles.
- Leverage is a textbook result Claude has read. In-sample support would have been weak evidence. Here the in-sample result is the absence of the effect, which training-data recall would not predict.
- Level SHAR coefficients are sensitive to a few extreme days, for example TSLA on 2026-11-05 (-12%). That is why the log and calendar variants are reported next to it. The levels and logs disagree on sign for the T holdout and for H-LEV4.
- Equity perps trade 24/7 on Bybit, but the underlying is closed at weekends: weekend RV is tiny. The calendar-dummy variant handles this. The primary spec, as registered, does not.
- Date-clustered SEs ignore serial correlation within a symbol. Driscoll-Kraay SEs are reported and are close in every case.
- Some exploratory EGARCH fits on < 250 days are degenerate (NVDA t -9.6, SOXL t -153). They are excluded from all verdicts.
- SPCXUSDT (117 complete days) is below the 120-day universe-T threshold and is excluded.
- Universe L is today's survivors (survivorship bias). Daily RV comes from 24 hourly returns, which is noisy; QLIKE is used for that reason.
- The verdict mapping for H-LEV2 was corrected before recording to the literal registered criterion (diary 2026-11-10, kind "mistake").
