# BTC order-book generator (H-OBG1, H-OBG2), 2026-11-13

Agent F5. Queue-reactive order-flow generator `src/ai_trader/sim/ob_gen.py`, executed by the Rust matching engine (`atx match-sim`, crates/at-match), evaluated by `src/ai_trader/research/obgen_study.py`. Research only; generated data never reach the LLM layer.

## Design (fixed before evaluation)

- **split**: day-level and fixed before any evaluation (data/research/obgen/split.json): calibration days = the ob200 event-level days og.CAL_DAYS (2026-04-15, 2026-09-13, 2026-10-28; none is a micro_days day) for the queue-reactive tables; held-out days = every verified micro_days day with BTC trades_1s / book_1s / orders / laws (31 days); per held-out day the generator is calibrated on its FIT half (00-12 UTC: queue and depth scales, arrival law, empirical order-size quantiles, sign model; theta (reprice threshold), lam (flow-bridge impact) and deep_corr (deep-level size) by 5 rounds of indirect inference on 06-08 UTC against the share of seconds with a mid change, the impact slope and the median depth) and evaluated on its TEST half (12-24 UTC); segment = llm_in_sample if the day < 2026-07-01 else clean_holdout; pooled = all
- **conditioning**: H-OBG1 paths = the held-out day's real TEST-half 1-minute bars (book mid at each minute end, realized variance of 1 s mid returns inside the minute, taker buy / sell notional and order counts): everything below one minute (book, queues, order timing and signs, 1 s returns) is generated
- **engine**: H-OBG1 days: generator events -> atx match-sim (crates/at-match, F4) -> trades.jsonl / book.jsonl -> sim.book (bin 1 s) and micro_days.bars_1s / merge_orders; twins (H-OBG2): the generator's own price-time book, whose prints and 100 ms book states equal the engine's exactly (tests/test_ob_gen.py, pilots), to fit the runtime budget
- **facts**:
  - spread: share of 1 s book states with spread > 1.5 ticks
  - depth: two components, both must be inside: median log10 depth within 25 bp (USD, both sides, the 200-level view, as the public feed) and median touch share = (bid1_size + ask1_size) mid / depth_25
  - depletion: queue depletion rate at the touch: mean over consecutive 1 s states with an unchanged best price of max(q_t - q_t+1, 0) / q_t, both sides pooled
  - size: two components: median log10 taker-order notional and log10(p99 / p50) (orders = same-ms same-side print runs)
  - interarrival: two components: share of taker-order inter-arrival times <= 10 ms and log10 median inter-arrival (ms)
  - impact: OLS slope of the 1 s mid log return (bp) on 1 s signed taker notional (USD million)
  - absr_acf: mean autocorrelation of |1 s mid log return| over lags 1-60 s
  - flow_acf: mean autocorrelation of 1 s signed taker notional over lags 1-60 s
- **envelope**: per segment and fact: [5%, 95%] empirical quantiles of the real held-out days' TEST-half values (the day-to-day envelope); the generated fact = median over the same days of the generated TEST-half values; a fact is inside when every component is inside; H-OBG1 supports iff >= 6 of 8 facts are inside, else contradicts (segments with < 5 days: inconclusive). Reported, not in the criterion: per-day share of generated values inside the envelope, Spearman of real vs generated per-day values
- **obg2**: BTC next-minute vol (ml.micro channels, decision every 30 s train / 10 s test, target r1m^2, QLIKE loss, forecast F = 60 b_s exp(f + c) with c fitted on the validation day), models ml.micro make_linear and make_cnn trained with research.ml_micro_study.train (same optimiser and early stopping). Leave-days-out: the 31 held-out days sorted, day i -> fold i mod 3; train on the other folds, validation = last training day in date order (real), test = the fold's days (real only). Variants: R100 = all real training days; M50 = R100 + the generated twins of the same days (50% real / 50% generated windows); R25 = a fixed 25% of the training days (seed 2026, at least 2 days); M25 = R25 + the twins of those days. Twin of day d = a 24 h generated day driven by a 30-minute block bootstrap of day d's own 1-minute bars (calibrated on d's FIT half), so no test-day information reaches training. Per test day QLIKE difference B - A = r^2 (1/F_B - 1/F_A) + log(F_B / F_A) over windows; ratio = 1 + mean difference / mean Patton QLIKE of A (windows with r^2 > 0); 95% CI by a bootstrap over test days (B = 2000). Part 1: M50 vs R100 ratio CI upper < 1.01; part 2: M25 vs R25 ratio CI upper < 1. Primary model = CNN (lin reported). supports iff both parts; contradicts iff part 1 CI lower > 1.01 or part 2 point ratio >= 1; else inconclusive
- **recording**: one row per segment (llm_in_sample, clean_holdout, pooled) per hypothesis; skip segments already recorded

## What the generator does not reproduce, and caveats

- Deep levels (>= 30 ticks from the touch) are re-drawn from a stationary profile; their queue dynamics (estimated, reported) are not simulated.
- On H-OBG1 days the 1-minute path (mid, realized variance, taker counts and notional per side) is real: only sub-minute structure is generated.
- Per-day indirect inference on the FIT half targets the share of seconds with a mid change, the impact slope and the median depth; the TEST half is never used for calibration, but these three facts are calibrated quantities, not emergent ones.
- Public ob200 shows ~200 levels (~4 bp of BTC): depth facts are on that truncated view, for real and generated books alike.
- Bybit internals (risk engine, liquidations, hidden priority rules, latency) are outside at-match; market orders use a 1% protection band (assumption).
