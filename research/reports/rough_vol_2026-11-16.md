# Rough volatility and Volterra kernels (block RFSV), 2026-11-16

_Research agent. Hypotheses H-RFSV1, H-RFSV2, H-VOLT1, registered in `research/hypotheses.yaml` before any data was read. Code: `src/ai_trader/research/rough_vol_study.py`. Artefacts: `data/research/rough_vol/` (rough.json, fc_*.parquet, forecast_eval.json, volterra.json; ~60 MB)._

**Data.** Universe L (BTC, ETH, SOL and the 20 alts of `trend_zoo_study.UNIVERSE`). RV is the sum of squared 5m log returns (Bybit public klines already cached by the BOS study, 2022-01 to 2026-11-15). Hourly RV is deseasonalised by an hour-of-week profile that is updated weekly from earlier data only. The direction test uses lake 1h/4h bars via `market_frame`. Segments are split by origin time at 2026-07-01. The holdout holds about 137 days: 3,300 hourly origins and 137 daily origins per token.

## (a) Roughness (H-RFSV1)

H is the slope of log m(2, D) on log D divided by 2, with D = 1..20 days. CIs come from a moving-block bootstrap (30-day blocks, 300 resamples).

| | daily median H (range) | tokens with CI upper < 0.35 | hourly median H |
|---|---|---|---|
| llm_in_sample | **0.129** (0.099-0.161) | 23 / 23 | 0.063 |
| clean_holdout | **0.139** (0.068-0.193) | 23 / 23 | 0.062 |

- Examples, in-sample [CI] then holdout [CI]: BTC 0.099 [0.076, 0.121] then 0.072 [0.00, 0.14]; ETH 0.113 [0.092, 0.136] then 0.082 [0.03, 0.17]; SOL 0.147 [0.115, 0.177] then 0.139 [0.03, 0.28].
- Scaling: zeta_q is close to linear in q (median R² 0.975 in-sample, 0.996 holdout) with slight concavity (zeta_q/q falls from 0.15 at q = 0.5 to 0.11 at q = 3), so there is a mild multifractal tilt. H estimated from lags 5 and up is similar (0.119 / 0.106).
- Regime stability, pooled over tokens with token-cluster bootstrap CIs:
  - By vol state, in-sample: low 0.127 [0.117, 0.138], normal 0.118 [0.108, 0.128], high 0.154 [0.143, 0.162]. Range 0.036.
  - By vol state, holdout: low 0.167, normal 0.084, high 0.107. Range 0.083.
  - Both ranges are under the registered 0.1.
  - By v0 cell the spread is wider: 0.09 (R1) to 0.19 (R9) in-sample, 0.06 (R6) to 0.18 (R3) in the holdout. Liquidation and momentum cells are the "smoothest".
- The hourly H of about 0.06 is biased low. RV from twelve 5m returns carries measurement noise, which adds a constant to m(2, D) and flattens the slope. It should not be read as "rougher intraday".

Crypto log-vol is about as rough as GJR's equity and FX numbers (H ≈ 0.1). Shocks to log-vol are strongly anti-persistent at short lags, while the level has very long memory.

## (b) RFSV forecast vs HAR / log-HAR / GARCH (H-RFSV2)

Setup: purged quarterly expanding walk-forward from 2023Q1 (training targets end at least 1 day before the test quarter). Every model gets a QLIKE-optimal level constant fitted on its training fold. H is re-estimated on each training fold: about 0.06 on the hourly grid and about 0.14 daily. Loss differences are averaged across tokens per origin, with Newey-West t. | | 1h | 4h | 24h |

| in-sample | -2.0%, t -1.73 | -3.7%, t -0.99 | +2.5%, t +2.04 |

- After Holm, no horizon passes in either segment, so the verdict is **inconclusive / inconclusive**. Per token at 1h, RFSV wins with t < -2 for 5 of 23 tokens in-sample and 3 of 23 in the holdout, and loses for none.
- Secondary result (log-MSE, not part of the verdict). The rough kernel beats log-HAR for the *log* RV point forecast at 1h in both segments: -3.4%, t -7.6; and -1.5%, t -3.1. RFSV-reg beats it at 1h and 4h in both segments: -4.1% / -2.5%, and -5.9% / -3.2%, all |t| > 3.
- The gain sits in the median of log-vol, not in the variance level that QLIKE scores. Risk sizing uses the level, so this does not change risk.
- Against level HAR and GARCH(1,1)-t, RFSV wins everywhere: QLIKE -12% to -43%, |t| 2.5 to 14. That is mostly the log transform, since log-HAR shows the same gains.

**Proposal (conditional, not wired).** The user's condition was "if RFSV beats HAR out of sample in both segments". It holds against level HAR but not against the incumbent log-HAR on QLIKE. So I do **not** recommend replacing log-HAR in sizing. The cheap, defensible step is a forward shadow:
- `vol.rough` is a fit-in-Python tool. Per token and grid it stores {H, kernel length 500, level constant c, deseasonalisation profile date} as JSON in `data/models/rough_vol/<symbol>.json`.
- The kernel is a fixed FIR filter (500 taps) on deseasonalised log RV. A Rust port in at-filters is trivial: one dot product per bar, and the weights depend only on H.
- It is scored forward against log-HAR on the median band of hourly log-RV (MSE log, pinball at 0.5), the metric where it wins.
- Do this only if the user wants better intraday vol *bands*. Stops keep `sigma_risk_next`. I did not change the live networks and wrote nothing to `data/models/`.

## (c) Volterra kernel for direction (H-VOLT1)

The signal is s_t = Σ_k k^(-β) z_{t-k+1} (K = 168), with β chosen on the training fold. The baseline is EWMA momentum with its half-life chosen the same way. The trading arm takes the sign of s, enters by limit at the close only if the next bar trades through, pays maker 2 bp, and otherwise crosses at taker 7.5 bp.

| | pooled IC Volterra / EWMA | diff (t) | net Sharpe Volterra / EWMA |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1h in-sample | 0.0055 / 0.0048 | +0.0007 (0.5) | -0.56 / -0.44 |
| 1h holdout | 0.0106 / 0.0078 | +0.0028 (1.0) | -0.07 / +0.41 |
| 4h in-sample | 0.0140 / 0.0108 | +0.0032 (1.1) | -0.43 / -0.61 |
| 4h holdout | 0.0196 / 0.0373 | -0.0178 (-1.4) | -0.10 / -0.30 |

- The chosen β wanders across quarters (0.1 to 1.3), which is itself a sign there is no stable kernel shape to find.
- The fractional kernel carries the same thin, cost-dominated trend IC as EWMA. That fits KNOWLEDGE item 1 (vol is forecastable, direction is not) and the trend-zoo results.

## Not done / caveats

- The ML vol line (H-NN8 XGB / MLP) was not reproduced. Its forecasts are not cached on this grid.
- No Student-t density was scored. Only point losses were.
- The daily grid has no weekday terms for any model. That is fair, but all of them miss the Saturday and Monday effects.
- GARCH failed its sanity check in about 25% of refits, so it is scored on fewer cells (pairwise comparison).
- 5m klines come from Bybit public REST (BOS cache), not from the lake. The lake has no 5m bars for universe L.
