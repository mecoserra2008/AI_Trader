Exploratory. Nothing here is a tested signal. Window: 2026-11-12 23:52 to 2026-11-16 00:40 UTC. Public, keyless data only.
Regime context (Bybit 1h bars, log): BTC -452 bp on 11-13 and -628 bp on 11-14 (with OI -4.6% then +1.8%), then +164 bp
on 11-15. Alts fell 10-16% on 11-14 (SUI -1277, UNI -1625, ENA -1561 bp) and rebounded 4-8% on 11-15.
At 00:00 funding flipped slightly negative on SOL, XRP, SUI and DOGE (-0.1 to -0.26 bp).

## A. Cross-venue tracker (`scripts/xvenue_tape.py`, running)

- Venues: Bybit perp, Binance perp and spot (aggTrades, fromId paging), OKX swap (ctVal from /public/instruments,
  history-trades paging), Coinbase (maker side flipped to taker), Hyperliquid (recentTrades returns about 10 trades, so it
  is SAMPLED and excluded from flow alerts), Deribit BTC/ETH, and Upbit KRW (via KRW-USDT).
- Symbols: BTC ETH SOL XRP DOGE SUI ZEC HYPE + KAVA SOPH RARE. One line per symbol per minute goes to
  `logs/xvenue_tape.log` and JSON to `data/journal/xvenue/<date>.jsonl`. The tracker's PID is in `logs/xvenue_tape.pid`.
- Cycle is 25 s, not the 10 s target: about 0.4 s RTT per request with 2 threads, and HL is polled every 3rd cycle.
  Binance, Coinbase and Deribit page with ids, so they have no gaps. Bybit (1000-trade window) shows 1-2 gaps per hour on
  BTC and KAVA. OKX gaps are frequent on ETH (44) and BTC (20) because history paging is capped at 4 pages, so OKX
  BTC/ETH deltas are lower bounds.
- Premiums use 1-minute VWAPs over the same window (the mid-quote premium is in the JSON but noisy because quotes are not
  taken simultaneously). Basis is each perp's mark vs its own spot index.
- Observed 23:46 to 00:40 (37 ALERT lines, all descriptive).
- **Coinbase premium is a USDT premium, not a US bid.** Raw cbP vs Binance spot is -4 to -7 bp on all majors. Adjusted
    for Coinbase USDT-USD it is about 0 (BTC -0.4, ETH -2.1, SOL -0.05 bp). Korea premium is +4 to +7 bp on majors.
- **ETH 00:17-00:36: Binance perp sold while OKX bought.** Binance perp large-taker (>p95) 5-min delta ran -5 to -11 M
    USD for 20 minutes (5.5x its 1h median) while OKX was +0.6 to +2.8 M. ETH drifted 1825 -> 1820 (-27 bp). The selling
    absorbed with little price impact. Divergence ALERTs fired at 00:20 (bn) and 00:36 (db +269k vs others -8.6 M).
- **BTC 23:58: OKX and Coinbase bought while Bybit and Binance sold.** OKX large +1.9 M and Coinbase +632k vs Bybit
    -1.4 M and Binance -1.4 M per 5 min. No follow-through either way in the next 30 min.
- **KAVA is dislocated.** Upbit KRW-KAVA is +36 to +38% and Coinbase +27 to +28% over Binance spot. Bybit funding was
    -1.43% at the 00:00 settlement and is now at the -2% cap. Mark vs index is -186 bp; OI is $1.8 M.
    This is the signature of a closed deposit/withdrawal route after the 11-14 Kava Lend exploit (news_agent 23:30
    snapshot): the premium cannot be arbitraged. That is unverified because exchange wallet-status pages were not read.
    The scalper's KAVA tape is not a normal market.
- SOPH: the Upbit premium collapsed from +5.3% (23:58) to -0.2% (00:40). Funding_by -0.16%.
- Lead-lag (10-s returns, lags -3..+3): every liquid venue is at lag 0 with corr 0.82-0.98. Upbit lags by 1 bucket and
    no venue leads consistently, so no LEAD alert fired. At 10-s resolution REST polling cannot resolve leadership.
- Most ALERTs are small-venue noise: Coinbase or Upbit 5-min large deltas under $250k "diverging", and basis z-scores
    of 3-6 on OKX and HL right after the 00:00 funding settlement. Read the dollar size before the label.

## Hypothesis candidates (not registered; register in research/hypotheses.yaml before any test)
- **HC-OC-1 (deposit-lock premium).** Trigger: for a Bybit USDT perp, 1e4*ln(UpbitKRW/USDTKRW / Binance spot), both 1-min VWAP, is > +1000 bp for 60
    consecutive minutes. Measure: the next three Bybit funding prints are each <= -0.5%, and the perp's 24 h log return is > 0, compared with
    all-hour base rates. Testable historically with public Upbit and Binance 1-min candles plus Bybit funding.
- **HC-OC-2 (stablecoin contraction).** Trigger: the DefiLlama total USD stablecoin 3-day log change is < -0.5% (known_ts = date + 1 d). Measure: BTC 3-day forward log return < 0, more often than base rate. DefiLlama history is revisable, so treat it as forward-only from our archive, or as `llm_in_sample` before 2026-07-01.
- **HC-OC-3 (reserve drain).** Trigger: Binance PoR BTC wallet balance 3-day change < -1% (DefiLlama daily; drop one-point reversals of more than 20%). Measure: BTC 3-day forward return. The sign is open: this window says outflows coincide with selling.
- **HC-OC-4 (venue divergence, forward-only on this journal).** Trigger: at a minute close, Binance-perp large-taker 5-min delta is < -3x its 1h median |delta| and above $3 M, while
    the sum of the OKX and Coinbase large deltas is > 0. Measure: Bybit perp 15-min forward log return < 0 vs base rate. Same rule with signs flipped.
- **HC-OC-5 (USDT-adjusted Coinbase premium).** Trigger: BTC Coinbase USD 1-min VWAP / (Binance spot VWAP * Coinbase USDT-USD) > +5 bp for 30 consecutive minutes. Measure: BTC 1 h forward return > 0. Testable on public 1-min candles.
