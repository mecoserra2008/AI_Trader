## Verdicts in plain words

**What we now believe**

1. **The desk's field P(jump) is not the probability of a visible jump.** The field prints "jump probability 1.5-2% next hour". Its jump is a latent mixture branch (a move of at least 3 diffusion sd at 1h). For that definition it is calibrated (H-WS05-7 supports/supports):
   - mean p 1.68% vs the posterior jump weight 1.66% in-sample (z -0.9) and 1.64% in the holdout (z -0.5);
   - implied 4-sigma flag rate 1.07% vs 1.11% / 0.90% realised.

   For an observable event (at least one 5m Lee-Mykland jump in the next hour) it fails (H-WS05-6 contradicts/contradicts):
   - the base rate is 5.8% in-sample and 6.3% in the holdout;
   - calibration-in-the-large z is 37.6 / 9.1;
   - the log score is below a constant rate by 0.031 nats (t -17.1) / 0.037 (t -4.5);
   - after a logit recalibration it ties the constant (+0.0006, t 0.8). In the holdout the field's p carries no ranking information for observable jumps.
   - Its 4h version (1-(1-p)^4 = 6.6%) faces a 19-20% observed rate.
2. **Jumps self-excite and cluster on US release minutes. A Hawkes intensity is the right jump forecaster.**
   - **Self-excitation:** at the cutoff fit, LR 3738 vs the same baseline without excitation. The branching ratio is 0.30 (30% of BTC 5m jumps are triggered by an earlier jump) and the kernel half-life 10.5 min.
   - **Release minutes (H-WS05-9 supports):** CPI / NFP / FOMC multiply the 5m jump intensity by 76 in the release bin and by 4.4 over the next 25 min (LR 338).
   - **J1 forecast:** Hawkes+vol (M4) beats the constant rate by +0.0067 nats per hour (t 10.1) in-sample and +0.0083 (t 1.9) in the holdout. It beats the field by +0.037 (t 17.4) / +0.045 (t 3.8). H-WS05-5 and H-WS05-8 both support/support.
   - **J4 forecast:** the holdout gain is sign-only (+0.0055, t 0.7), and the MCS keeps the constant. At 4h, use a constant plus the release-minute term.
   - **Goodness of fit:** the registered single-kernel Hawkes fails time-rescaling (KS p 4e-8; H-WS05-4 inconclusive). A two-kernel version (half-lives 4.9 min and 51 min) passes (KS p 0.046).
3. **Jumps are everywhere and ill-defined at these frequencies.**
   - At 5m, LM fires on 69% of BTC days, BNS on 21% and ASJ on 6% (nominal 1%). Jump variation is 8% of QV (H-WS05-1 supports/supports).
   - The tests disagree on which days had jumps: day kappa 0.03-0.13 (H-WS05-2 contradicts/contradicts). On the flow pool, 1m vs 5m LM kappa is 0.36, BNS 0.06 and ASJ 0.03.
   - Any rule gated on "a jump" must name its test and frequency.
   - Jump size scales one-for-one with spot vol (slope 0.96 ± 0.01 / 1.01 ± 0.03). In sigma units the size law is stable: median |z| 5.4, GPD tail xi 0.36 in-sample / 0.20 in the holdout.
4. **Student-t stays the house density.** On vol-standardised 5m BTC returns, Kou loses to the t by 0.0039 nats (t -14.5) in-sample and 0.0056 (t -7.7) in the holdout (H-WS05-3 contradicts/contradicts).
   - Merton is worse than Kou, and all three beat the Gaussian (SV pure diffusion) by about 0.15 nats.
   - The fitted Kou "jump" branch has p = 0.23 with mean size 1.2 sd: it acts as a scale mixture, not a rare-jump component (t nu = 3.4). This matches H-JD1 at 1h.
5. **Liquidation cascades excite further jumps, and the excess outlives the 60-minute R-FLOW-2 window by about an hour.**
   - After 760 R-FLOW-2 episodes (23 symbols), the same-symbol 1m jump rate is 14x baseline in the first 5 min and 6x at 30-60 min (H-WS05-10 supports).
   - The marked Hawkes liquidation kernel is highly significant given self-excitation (LR 561). Its half-life is 31 min [25, 37].
   - The excess hazard is +54% at 60 min, +28% at 90 min and +14% at 120 min (model). Against each episode's own pre-cascade jump rate (post hoc H-WS05-11b), it is +26% [1%, 56%] at 60-120 min and gone at 2-4 h.
   - H-WS05-11 and 11b both contradict "the excess is gone by 60 min".
   - Cost check: in Nov 2026 the 60-min window already covers 21-26% of valid minutes for BTC / ETH / SOL, and 120 min would cover 34-39%.
6. **Crypto jumps are co-jumps (H-WS05-12 supports).** 62% [50%, 73%] of BTC 1m jump minutes have an ETH jump in the same minute, and 62% have jumps in at least 3 alts. The shifted-day placebo is ~0%. BTC excites the alt basket (LR 7.7, p 0.003).
7. **Stop gaps through jumps are small at our size. The 2 USD worst case holds; the liquid tier's 10 bp slip budget is too tight.** Simulated on the 3s path (23 names, 201 hourly origins, Nov 8-25):
   - At 4 x sigma_1h, the median stop slip is 1.7 bp (jump triggers 2.6 bp vs 1.6 bp otherwise, x1.6). p99 is 41 bp, mean 0.019 R, p99 0.14 R. H-WS05-13 is inconclusive: the gap is x1.6, not the registered x3.
   - Jump-trigger slips reach at most 52 bp; the largest slip, 406 bp, came from a non-jump gapped trigger.
   - P(a stopped trade loses more than 2 USD) is 0.09% at 4 sigma and 0.27% at 1 sigma (1.1% of jump triggers at 1 sigma).
   - Same-side pair co-stops above 6 USD: 0 of 3151 at 4 sigma (max 3.01 USD) and 0.08% at 1 sigma (H-WS05-17 supports).
   - 12-19% of liquid-tier 4-sigma stops exceed the 10 bp budget (p95 18 bp); 2.5-5% of mid-tier stops exceed 25 bp (p95 21 bp).
8. **Joint tails are fatter than Gaussian, DCC beats EWMA and rolling correlation, and a 2-3 position crypto book is about 1.3 bets in every regime.**
   - **Lower tail:** at 1%, BTC-alt co-exceedance is 0.53 / 0.56, vs 0.32 / 0.35 under a Gaussian copula (x1.64 in both segments). The lower tail is fatter than the upper (0.39 / 0.46).
   - **Copula:** a t-copula (nu 6-8) beats the Gaussian copula by 0.31 nats per row (t 40) / 0.35 (t 15.5) (H-WS05-14 supports/supports). It still understates the 1% joint crash a little (0.47 vs 0.53).
   - **Correlation models:** DCC(1,1) (a 0.013-0.015, b 0.89-0.97; correlation half-life about 2 days at 1h) beats EWMA(0.97) and 30-day rolling at 1h and 4h in both segments, with t 2.5-7.4 (H-WS05-15 supports/supports).
   - **Effective bets:** median ENB is 1.27 for pairs and 1.33 for BTC-ETH-X triples.
   - **Co-stopping:** given one leg stops, all legs stop within 4 h 40% of the time (pairs) and 27% (triples). This does not depend on regime: stress (R6/R8/R9) vs calm (R1/R4/R5) ratio 1.03 [0.98, 1.08]. H-WS05-16 contradicts, because its regime half fails.

**What to use**
- **Jump probability:** the Hawkes intensity with release-minute marks (M5, or the two-kernel M4b where goodness of fit matters) for 1h jump risk; a constant plus the release-minute term for 4h.
- **Densities:** Student-t (nu ~3.4 on 5m z).
- **Joint tails:** a t-copula (nu ~6) or empirical co-exceedance for any portfolio tail number.
- **Covariance:** DCC for 1h / 4h covariance.
- **Live limits:** treat 2 same-side crypto positions as ~1.3 bets.

**What to stop using**
- The field P(jump) as a jump gate or as "the chance of a visible jump": the J_field gates in execution_study / mm_weekend_study, and the card sentence "jump probability x%". It is a model-internal mixture weight.
- The Gaussian copula for joint stop risk.
- The 10 bp stop-slip budget for the liquid tier.

**What changed**
- R-FLOW-2's 60 min is shorter than the hazard: half-life 31 min, excess +26-54% in the following hour. Lengthening a calendar blackout is expensive, though (34-39% of minutes at 120 min).
- The measured joint tail sits well inside the live limits (3 open, 2 same side, 400 USD gross, 2 USD per trade, 10 USD per day).

## Dossier 1: jump occurrence (detection) — decision link: blackouts, jump gates

**Variable.** A jump indicator per bar. Lee-Mykland (LM) at 5m and 1m, Barndorff-Nielsen-Shephard (BNS) and Ait-Sahalia-Jacod (ASJ) at the day level, all on periodicity-filtered returns. It feeds the jump-probability target, every gate that conditions on "a jump", and stop-gap classification.

**Properties.** BTC 5m, 2020-04..2026-09, 656,984 valid bars in-sample and 25,555 in the holdout. Detected jump rate per bar: 0.65% at 5m and 0.45% at 1m (ETH Binance), against a nominal ~4e-5. Unit tests confirm LM, BNS and ASJ hold their size (< 5% of days) under a seasonal Gaussian diffusion.

| set (segment) | days | LM days | BNS days | ASJ days | mean jump share of QV |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| BTC 5m lake (in) | 2282 | 68.8% | 20.9% | 6.2% | 8.4% |
| BTC 5m lake (hold) | 88 | 71.6% | 17.0% | 5.7% | 7.9% |
| ETH 1m Binance (in) | 1277 | 92.3% | 36.6% | 30.1% | 6.7% |
| ETH 5m Binance (in) | 1277 | 70.2% | 18.8% | 6.3% | 7.9% |
| flow pool 1m (hold, 23 names) | 299 sym-days | 82.6% | 63.2% | 33.4% | ~10% |
| flow pool 5m (hold) | 299 | 60.2% | 15.1% | 1.7% | |

**Specifications compared.**
- LM (local, bar-level; assumes locally constant vol over one day);
- BNS (day-level, assumes Brownian SV plus finite-activity jumps; power falls with few, small jumps);
- ASJ (day-level, scale-free; very low power at n = 288: the critical S is ~1.0, the pure-jump limit).

**How much the specs disagree.** Day-level kappa:
- BTC 5m: LM-BNS 0.13, LM-ASJ 0.03, BNS-ASJ 0.09 in-sample (0.12 / 0.05 / 0.13 in the holdout).
- Frequency (1m vs 5m, flow pool): LM 0.36, BNS 0.06, ASJ 0.03. ETH Binance in-sample: LM 0.28, BNS 0.12, ASJ 0.03.

The decision changes with the definition. An LM-5m gate is "on" most days; an ASJ gate almost never.

**Trust.** LM at 5m for timing: it is the only test that locates jumps, its size is controlled, and its rate is stable across segments (0.65% / 0.65%). Read an LM jump as "a move larger than 4.3 local bipower sd". With a one-day bipower window this includes intraday vol bursts, so it is not proof of a price discontinuity. Day-level tests are for describing days only.

**Sizes.**
- Median |z| is 5.4 (holdout 5.3), median 69 bp (42 bp holdout), p90 155 bp (88 bp), max 1286 bp (186 bp). Up share 0.50 / 0.60.
- Exceedances over the LM threshold are heavier than exponential: GPD xi 0.36, LR 668 in-sample; 0.20, LR 7.1 (p 0.008) in the holdout.
- log size on log spot vol has slope 0.957 (se 0.009) / 1.014 (0.035). Jump size is proportional to vol, so stops in sigma units face a stable jump-size law.

## Dossier 2: probability of a jump in the next 1h / 4h — decision link: field P(jump), blackouts, quoting gates

**Variable.** J1 / J4 = at least one BTC 5m LM jump in (t, t+1h] / (t, t+4h], from hourly origins. Base rates: J1 5.8% / 6.3%; J4 19.1% / 20.0% (in-sample / holdout).

**Specifications.** All Bernoulli-cloglog per 5m bin with an exact multi-bin forecast:
- M0: expanding base rate.
- M1: seasonal (hour factor).
- M2: + log spot vol.
- M3: seasonal + exponential self-excitation (Hawkes).
- M4: M2 + Hawkes.
- M5: M4 + release-minute kernel.
- FIELD: frozen cutoff field.
- PD: pure-diffusion false-positive rate.

Each assumes a conditionally Bernoulli bin, an exponential kernel, parameters stable over the monthly-refit window, and an origin-frozen vol covariate.

**Fit (cutoff, data < 2026-07-01).**
- Self-excitation: alpha 0.084 per bin, half-life 10.5 min, branching 0.30. LR M4 vs M2 = 3738 (p ~0).
- Log spot vol has a negative coefficient (-0.62 to -0.66). With vol-relative detection, high-vol states produce fewer LM jumps per bar.
- Release coefficients: 4.33 in the release bin and 1.48 for +5..+30 min (LR 338).

**Assumption checks (time-rescaling, 4272 rescaled intervals).**
- M1 and M2: KS p ~0 (no clustering).
- M3: KS p 2e-7. M4: KS p 4e-8 (Ljung-Box p 0.17). M5: KS p 9e-8.
- M4b (two kernels, 4.9 min and 51 min, an extra spec coded before results but not in the frozen list): KS p 0.046, LB p 0.031.

One exponential kernel is too simple; the decay has a fast and a slow component.

## Stability and serial properties of the jump process (descriptive, added after the first commit)

Source: data/research/ws05/stability.json. No new hypotheses.

## Dossier 3: return density with jumps — decision link: Student-t house default, stop tails

**Variable.** BTC 5m z = (r / f) / sigma_bv(previous day).

**Properties.** |z| > 5 on 0.39% of bars in both segments, versus 6e-7 under a Gaussian.

**Specifications** (walk-forward by year, cutoff fit for the holdout):
- Gaussian: the SV pure diffusion, since the vol is already the local estimate.
- Student-t: SV with unobserved intra-day vol.
- Merton: Gaussian core plus normal jumps.
- Kou: Gaussian core plus double-exponential jumps.

**Mean log score (nats per bar).**

| model | in-sample | holdout |
|---|---|---|
| Gaussian | -1.544 | -1.519 |
| Student-t | -1.392 | -1.410 |
| Merton | -1.405 | -1.425 |
| Kou | -1.396 | -1.416 |

**Tests.** DM Kou - t: -0.0039 (t -14.5) in-sample, -0.0056 (t -7.7) in the holdout. Kou - Merton: +0.009 (t 8.5 / 10.6).

**Interpretation.** The Kou fit puts 23% of bars on its "jump" branch with mean size 1.2 sd: it is used as a second scale. The t's single tail parameter (nu 3.4) does it better. The jump-diffusion's extra structure is not identified as rare jumps at 5m either, consistent with H-JD1 at 1h.

**Trust.** Student-t. The decision does not change across the fat-tailed specs (differences under 0.015 nats). It changes a lot against the Gaussian (0.15 nats).

## Dossier 4: liquidation cascades as jump marks — decision link: R-FLOW-2 window, post-cascade stops

**Variable.** The same-symbol LM 1m jump rate after an R-FLOW-2 episode: cluster minutes merged when within 10 min; 760 episodes across 23 symbols, 9-70 per symbol.

**Specifications and how they differ.** Each row is the jump-rate ratio in that lag bin (minutes after the episode).

| baseline (assumption) | (0,5] | (5,15] | (15,30] | (30,60] | (60,120] | (120,240] |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| matched cell (symbol x hour x vol tercile; assumes the cell removes regime) | 14.4 [11.8, 17.4] | 5.6 | 7.0 | 6.3 | 4.7 [3.9, 5.6] | 1.9 [1.5, 2.3] |
| same, jump-only control (no liquidation) | 4.4 | 3.0 | 3.1 | 2.6 | 2.3 | 0.9 |
| own pre-episode rate (-240,-15] (post hoc; assumes a stationary local rate) | 4.6 [3.7, 5.7] | 1.2 | 2.9 | 2.3 | 1.26 [1.01, 1.56] | 0.64 |
| same, jump-only control | 1.05 | 1.04 | 1.03 | 0.74 | 0.64 | 0.22 |
| marked panel Hawkes (self + liquidation + BTC, vol baseline) | x3.0 at 1 min | 2.9 at 5 | 2.5 at 15 | 2.1 at 30 | 1.54 at 60 | 1.14 at 120 |

The marked Hawkes has gamma 0.006 per minute, a half-life of 30.8 min [25.5, 37.2] and LR 561. Its rescaling KS p is 0.02 for BTC, 0.65 for ETH and 0.20 for SOL.

Episodes without a coinciding jump show no excess in the first 15 min, then x2-3 at 15-60 min against their own pre-rate. These are cascades arriving in waves.

**Conclusion.** The size of the excess depends on the baseline, but its shape is consistent across specs:
- liquidations raise the jump hazard well beyond the jump they coincide with;
- the excess decays with a ~31 min half-life;
- the excess is still +25-55% at 60-120 min;
- it is gone by 2-4 h.

**Trust.** The marked Hawkes conditions on vol and self-excitation and passes rescaling for the majors. Read the cell baseline as an upper bound and the own-baseline as a lower bound.

**Power and caveats.**
- The 760 episodes sit in ~13 usable days of one sell-off month.
- The episode bootstrap ignores simultaneity across symbols, so the CIs are too narrow.
- No pre-cutoff liquidation data exist.

## Dossier 6: stop slippage through jumps — decision link: 4-sigma cap (H-STOP1), 2 USD worst case, tier slip budgets

**Variable.** Exit slip (bp, and R) of a stop-market exit, simulated on the 3s path:
- Entries are hourly and on both sides, with the stop at k x sigma_1h, k in {1, 2, 3, 4, 6, 8}.
- The fill is the trigger 3s candle's close, never better than the stop, less half the 3s spread.
- Paths are censored at collector gaps.

**Results at 4 sigma (1135 triggers, 34.5% of them inside jump minutes).**

| subset | median bp | p90 bp | p99 bp | max bp |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| all | 1.7 | 12.6 | 40.7 | 406 |
| jump triggers | 2.6 | 17.7 | 45.6 | 52 |
| non-jump triggers | 1.6 | 10.4 | 34.8 | 406 |
| gapped trigger candle | 4.4 | 26.2 | 141 | 406 |

**Other fill conventions.** Pessimistic (trigger-candle extreme): median 6.7 bp, p99 54 bp. Optimistic: half the spread.

**By stop width.** Slip relative to R falls with the distance:
- mean 0.060 R at 1 sigma, 0.019 R at 4 sigma, 0.009 R at 8 sigma;
- p99 0.49 R at 1 sigma, 0.14 R at 4 sigma.

The 4-sigma cap is not threatened by gap risk. The binding risk sits at tight stops (1 sigma: 1.1% of jump triggers breach 2 USD). The 0.5 sigma minimum distance in placement deserves care.

**Live-cap check.** Loss per stopped trade = N x (distance + 8.8 bp fees + slip), with N = min(tier cap, 1.30 / (distance + 10.8 bp)).

| stop width | P(loss > 2 USD) | max pair co-stop | pair co-stops > 6 USD |
|---|---|---|---|
| 4 sigma | 0.09% | 3.01 USD | 0 of 3151 |
| 1 sigma | 0.27% | 7.68 USD | 0.08% |

**Not modelled.**
- Bybit triggers on MarkPrice, which lags last price.
- Impact: our notional is 50-200 USD, so the book is not walked.
- Larger sizes would see far larger slips.

**Power.** 201 origins x 23 names over 13 days, with no stress event as large as 2022. Treat the tail numbers as lower bounds.

## Dossier 7: dependence and joint tails (WS06 core) — decision link: same-side limit, cluster caps, effective bets

**Variable.** EWMA-standardised 1h / 4h returns of BTC, ETH, SOL, XRP, DOGE, ADA, LINK and AVAX: 41,179 joint hours in-sample and 3,576 in the holdout (from 2021-10, the first hour with all 8 names).

**Tails.** At 1%, lower co-exceedance is 0.53 / 0.56. Gaussian-copula implied: 0.32 / 0.35. t-copula implied: 0.47 / 0.48. Upper tail: 0.39 / 0.46.

ETH has the highest lower co-exceedance with BTC (0.62). In the holdout XRP rose to 0.64.

**Copulas.** The t-copula beats the Gaussian copula by 0.31 / 0.35 nats per row (t 40 / 15.5), with nu 8 -> 6 across the yearly refits. Asymmetric copulas (Clayton) and vines were not tried; the asymmetry above says a skewed or asymmetric t would help.

**Correlation dynamics.**
- DCC (a ~0.014, b 0.89-0.97) beats EWMA(0.97) by 0.35 / 0.42 nats per row at 1h (t 7.4 / 3.8) and 0.29 / 0.54 at 4h (t 4.8 / 2.5).
- DCC beats 30-day rolling by 0.26 / 0.27 (t 6.2 / 4.3) at 1h and 0.22 / 0.36 (t 3.7 / 2.5) at 4h.
- EWMA vs rolling: not distinguishable (t -1.3 / -1.1).
- The desk's 30-day rolling correlation (cluster threshold 0.7) is not the best covariance model. Whether membership at 0.7 would change was not tested; with ENB ~1.3 almost every liquid name is BTC-beta either way.

**Effective bets and co-stopping.** Effective bets, measured as ENB = (sum eig)^2 / sum eig^2 of the 30-day correlation at entry (2021-10..2026-11):

| book | median ENB | ENB p10 | P(all legs stop within 4h, given one stops) |
|---|---|---|---|
| pairs | 1.27 | 1.15 | 0.40 |
| BTC-ETH-X triples | 1.33 | 1.21 | 0.27 |

Stops are 2 x sigma_4h with a 72 h cap. Stress vs calm cells: 0.41 vs 0.40, ratio 1.03 [0.98, 1.08].

The null result is informative, not underpowered: a ratio of 1.2 lies well outside the CI. Co-stopping is structural (one bet), not a regime effect.

**Live limits.** With 2 same-side names, the book carries ~1.3 bets and a 40% chance of a double stop once one stops. The worst measured double stop is ~3 USD at 4 sigma, so the 10 USD daily stop absorbs three such events. The limits match the measured joint tail. A third same-side slot would add only ~0.06 bets.

## Which specifications to trust (summary)

| target | trust | why | not distinguishable |
|---|---|---|---|
| jump timing | LM 5m (name it) | size-controlled, locates jumps, stable rate | test choice changes "jump days" |
| P(jump, 1h) | Hawkes M5 / M4b | DM in both segments, M4b passes rescaling | M3 / M4 / M5 in holdout |
| P(jump, 4h) | constant + release term | MCS keeps M0 in the holdout | all Hawkes vs constant |
| 5m density | Student-t | beats Kou / Merton in both segments | fat-tailed specs within 0.015 nats |
| post-cascade hazard | marked Hawkes (half-life 31 min) | conditions on vol + self-excitation, passes rescaling for majors | exact excess level (baseline-dependent) |
| joint tail | t-copula, nu ~6; empirical for 1% tails | beats Gaussian by 0.3 nats per row | asymmetric copulas untried |
| covariance | DCC(1,1) | beats EWMA and rolling at 1h / 4h in both segments | EWMA vs rolling |

## Caveats

- Every flow_3s and liquidation result comes from ~13 usable days of one month. The collector covers ~50% of minutes, and one-day bipower windows were relaxed to 30% coverage (labelled deviation, diary). No pre-cutoff 1m multi-asset data exist in the lake, so the co-jump and cascade results have no in-sample replication.
- The pre-registered matched baseline for cascades leaves slow regime effects in: the jump-only control is also elevated. The post hoc own-baseline trial is labelled. The episode bootstrap ignores cross-symbol simultaneity.
- LM with a 1-day bipower window flags intraday vol bursts as jumps (0.65% of 5m bars). "Jump" here means "a move larger than ~4.3 local sd", not a proven discontinuity.
- The field's own-definition holdout check first returned NaN (a missing next return on the last row). It was fixed by dropping those rows, with no design change.
- M4b was coded before results but is not in the frozen model list. H-WS05-16 was recorded as segment "pooled" (registered without a split).
- Stop simulation ignores the mark-price trigger and impact. The sigma used is the EWMA sigma_1h, not necessarily the sigma the risk engine uses for the cap.
- The holdout J4 Hawkes gain is sign-only. The registered rule's "diff > 0" in the holdout is weak evidence (WS01's point on sign agreement).

## Method

**Data (no downloads).**
- BTCUSDT 5m Bybit lake, 2020-04..2026-09-27.
- ETHUSDT 1m Binance lake, 2023-01..2026-08.
- flow_3s Bybit linear 2026-11-07..26 for universe L, rolled to 1m (a minute is valid with >= 17 of its 20 3s candles) and 5m.
- Liquidations 2026-11-08..26.
- CPI / NFP / FOMC times from data/research/news/events/events.parquet, used only when schedule_known_ts <= the release.
- The frozen cutoff field file on the lake 1h state.
- Bybit 1h / 4h bars of the focus set.

**Jump tests.**
- Returns are divided by a past-days-only hour-of-day factor (90 days, shrunk with 3 pseudo-days).
- LM: bipower spot vol over the previous day, with Gumbel critical values for alpha 1% per day (4.31 at 5m, 4.61 at 1m).
- BNS: Huang-Tauchen ratio-max with tri-power quarticity.
- ASJ: S(4,2) with truncated power variations, M(4,2) = 160/3.

**Hawkes.**
- A Bernoulli-cloglog discrete-time model with analytic gradients and L-BFGS-B.
- Monthly expanding refits from 2021-04. Forecasts are exact under each model.
- Discrete time-rescaling per Haslinger-Pipa-Brown (2010).
- DM tests: HLN-corrected with Newey-West variance (lag 24 for J1, 48 for J4).
- MCS: Hansen-Lunde-Nason with Tmax, 24 h blocks and 1000 draws.

**Densities.** Yearly walk-forward MLE on 100k-row subsamples.

**Liquidation clusters.** `features.flow_anomaly.liquidation_clusters` (the R-FLOW-2 definition).

**Dependence.** Copulas are walked forward by year on train ECDFs. DCC is refit yearly by 2-step QML. All correlation models share the same EWMA(0.94) vols.

**Compute and storage.**
- Every job went through scripts/ops/governor.py (heavy pool). Peaks: extract 874 MB, the pipeline below 1.2 GB, depend below 0.9 GB.
- Run times: extract 233 s, jumps 7 s, liq 68 s, stops 18 s, hawkes 685 s, score 11 s, density 88 s, depend 207 s.
- Storage in data/research/ws05: 0 before, ~31 MB after (the 40 MB transient 3s extract is deleted).
