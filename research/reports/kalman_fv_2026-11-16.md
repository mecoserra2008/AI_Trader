## (a) Single asset (H-KFD1)

**What the filter says.** The MLE (QML) gain of the vol-scaled local-level model is essentially 1:
- 1h: median K = 1.000 (range 0.965-1.000)
- 5m: median 0.992 (0.873-1.000)
- 1m: 1.000

The estimated observation noise R is tiny relative to Q. For BTC 1h, R/S is about 2e-4. At 5m it is 0.006-0.028. That is, the best "fair value" of log price is the previous price: crypto log prices are close to a random walk at every one of these frequencies. The standardised deviation z is then almost exactly the vol-standardised one-bar return, and the fade is a bet on next-bar reversal of large bars.

Results for the primary variant (k = 2.5, time stop 12; per-day mean net R, NW t):

| freq | segment | trades | net R / trade | gross R | t | hit | DSR |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1h | in-sample | 17,346 | **-0.206** | -0.138 | -11.5 | 0.46 | 0.00 |
| 1h | holdout | 1,817 | **-0.254** | -0.174 | -5.0 | 0.44 | 0.00 |
| 5m | in-sample | 130,017 | **-0.316** | -0.117 | -49.7 | 0.39 | 0.00 |
| 5m | holdout | 16,578 | **-0.341** | -0.119 | -22.9 | 0.38 | 0.00 |
| 1m | holdout (6 days) | 1,244 | **-0.346** | n/a | n/a | 0.34 | 0.00 |

- The fade loses **before costs** at every frequency: gross -0.12 to -0.17 R. That is H-T1 again (deviations continue). Costs then add another 0.07 to 0.2 R.
- Every one of the 10 grid variants is negative in both segments: -0.15 to -0.25 R at 1h, -0.30 to -0.36 R at 5m.
- PBO = 0.0 here only means "the in-sample ranking persists among losers". Larger k is worse.
- The time stops of 12 and 48 bars give identical trades: with K ≈ 1, z re-crosses 0 or the stop fires within a few bars. So the grid has 5 distinct variants, not 10.
- By own-token v0 cell (1h, primary): negative in all 9 cells in both segments. In-sample the range runs from R9 (liquidation) at -0.09 R to R1 at -0.25 R. In the holdout it runs from R3 at -0.14 R to R4 (compression) at -0.38 R. No cell rescues it.

## (c) Order-flow guardrail (H-KFD3)

The guard skips the fade while taker delta (and L1 OFI where it exists) over the last N minutes has the sign of the deviation. Primary variant, guarded minus unguarded mean net R, day-block bootstrap 95% CI:

| source | segment | guarded n | unguarded n | diff [CI] | validation n, diff |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 15m BTC/ETH (N = 60 min, delta) | in-sample (49 days) | 18 | 227 | -0.04 [-0.39, +0.32] | 1, n/a |
| 15m BTC/ETH | holdout (127 days) | 67 | 559 | +0.07 [-0.12, +0.29] | 7, +0.51 [-0.18, 1.10] |
| 1m, 37 perps (N = 15 min, delta + OFI) | holdout (6 days) | 179 | 1,244 | -0.04 [-0.21, +0.07] | 102, -0.03 |
| 1m, delta only | holdout | 375 | 1,244 | -0.01 [-0.13, +0.06] | |

- The guard keeps only 8-14% of the 15m signals and 14% (delta + OFI) or 30% (delta only) of the 1m signals. A large deviation almost always *is* aggressive flow in its own direction, which is why fading it loses.
- Guarded trades are still negative everywhere: -0.25 to -0.39 R.
- The registered sample threshold (30 guarded trades) fails in-sample, so the verdict is inconclusive. Where n ≥ 30 the CIs straddle 0.
- The "validation" variant (flow turning toward fair value) fires too rarely to judge: 7 trades over 127 days at 15m.

## Not done / caveats

- The local-linear-trend variant was not run. With K ≈ 1 for the level, a slope state adds trend-following, which belongs in the trend zoo, not a fade.
- The QML is Gaussian, not Student-t. With K at the boundary the likelihood choice cannot change the conclusion.
- The fill model is trade-through for entries and target exits. Stops are resting and filled at the stop price or a gap-through open, which is optimistic for 1m.
- The 1m and order-flow samples are 6 days (all clean_holdout). H-KFD3 should be rerun once flow_3s holds 60+ days.
- Survivorship: universe L is today's survivors.
