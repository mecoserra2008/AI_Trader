## In plain words

**Should the desk keep the v0 cells, replace them, or drop regime gating?**

- **Keep v0 as a label.** It is what the desk uses to describe the market ("always state the regime"). It costs nothing, does not need fitting, and means the same thing at every refit.
- **Do not replace it with a fitted regime model.** Several fitted specs help next-day vol forecasts slightly, but none of them beats the v0-conditioned forecast. The fitted specs also do worse than v0 as a strategy selector in the holdout.
- **Drop regime gating as a claimed edge.** Per-cell selection of strategies, rebuilt walk-forward, does not beat simply dropping the strategies that lose overall. No regime spec improves the trend sleeve. The fixed avoid lists (R-MAP-3..6) mostly block strategies that lose in every cell anyway.

**What we now believe:**

1. **Regimes mostly re-describe volatility.** HAR already models volatility well. Conditioning the per-symbol log-HAR on a regime state lowers next-day QLIKE by 0.6-2%.
   - Only ghmm3 passes the registered Holm test: -0.0026 (t -3.1, Holm p 0.023) in-sample and -0.0076 (t -2.3) in the holdout.
   - The v0 vol state adds nothing to HAR: +0.0002 (t 0.2).
   - The Model Confidence Set contains every HAR-based forecast in both segments, so the data cannot rank the regime specs against each other.
2. **The regime models' own variance forecasts are worse than HAR.** All 7 are worse in-sample, by +0.008 to +0.12 QLIKE (t up to 6.9). EWMA is worst of all (+0.14).
3. **No regime spec carries next-day direction.** Adding state-specific mean returns lowers the log score in-sample for all 13 specs (t -1.9 to -4.4), and the holdout gains are small and insignificant (t ≤ 1.6). R-PX-6 now holds under every spec, not just v0.
4. **The trend sleeve does not benefit from regime gating.** The best gated zoo_ensemble beats ungated by +0.07 Sharpe in-sample (Romano-Wolf p 0.41; deflated Sharpe 0.64).
   - The HMM-family gates are on more than 99.6% of days, because trend made money in every fitted state during training.
   - The v0-cell gate shows +0.63 [0.06, 1.20] in the 132-day holdout but -0.09 in-sample, so it is not credible.
5. **Cell-based strategy selection does not beat unconditional selection out of sample.** The walk-forward v0-cell book minus the unconditional book is -0.24 Sharpe [-1.01, +0.56] in-sample and +0.73 [-0.22, +1.71] in the holdout. Every fitted-spec book is worse than the v0 book in the holdout; ghmm3 is -1.56 [-2.70, -0.12].
6. **The R-MAP-3..6 cell effects are mostly coarse proxies for trend strength and vol level.** The effect is measured relative to the same strategy's other cells, on 2022+ out-of-sample days.
   - Raw, the cell effects are -1.7 to -3.0 x100 daily-sigma (t -1.6 to -2.3).
   - Controlling for the continuous vol percentile, dir_score and |dir_score| shrinks them to -0.02 to -2.5 (|t| ≤ 1.5).
   - Controlling for ghmm3 states barely changes them.
   - All five rules come out inconclusive by the registered rule (H-WS07-8).
7. **R-VOL-8 is true against EWMA and empty against HAR.** High-minus-low vol state in log(RV 7d / EWMA forecast) is -1.04 (t -22.5) for v0, -0.92 for ghmm3 and -0.83 for bocpd, with the same sign in the holdout. Against a 7-day log-HAR forecast it is -0.07 (t -1.4) in-sample and -0.09 (t -0.4) in the holdout. The effect is EWMA's missing mean reversion, not a property of the v0 labeller.
8. **"The regime" depends on the model.**
   - v0 cells agree with every fitted spec at an adjusted Rand index (ARI) of 0.02-0.12.
   - The return HMMs (ghmm2 and msar2) agree with each other at 0.88, but agree with MS-VAR on log RV at only 0.24.
   - Filtered labels match smoothed labels at an ARI of only 0.56-0.75. In-sample regime pictures overstate what can be known in real time.
   - Fitted specs are stable across refits (median refit ARI 0.67-0.97). Warm-started refits flatter this number.

**What changes (suggestions; research/rules.yaml not edited):**

- **R-MAP-2:** downgrade it, or retire it in favour of "drop strategies with a negative pooled training mean" (unconditional).
- **R-MAP-3..6:** restate as strategy-level avoid lists (they block strategies that fail overall, R-AVOID-1), not as regime evidence. Lower the confidence of the cell-specific claim to C.
- **R-MAP-7** (the only cell gate touching a live sleeve, shock_regime): inconclusive here (-3.0, t -1.6 in-sample; -6.8, t -1.4 holdout; approximated at day level). Keep it provisional until H-S3.
- **R-VOL-8:** add that the effect is relative to EWMA. With log-HAR sigma, no vol-state correction is needed.
- **R-PX-6:** extend the direction null to every regime spec tested here.
- **New:** do not gate zoo_ensemble / trend_4h on any regime state.
- **New:** do not use native Markov-switching variance forecasts for sigma.
- **Optional:** a ghmm3 intercept shift on log-HAR is a legitimate but tiny variance improvement, worth adopting only if WS04's best vol model has no regime or level term.

## Dossier 1: the target, the regime state for day d

**What it feeds.**
- The live gate's start-of-day v0 cell (R-MAP-3..7; execution/gate.py).
- The state card ("always state the regime").
- The vol inputs (R-VOL-8).
- The trend sleeve (zoo_ensemble / trend_4h).

**Empirical properties of the inputs** (23 symbols, llm_in_sample, daily):
- **Returns r.** ADF rejects a unit root for 23/23 symbols and KPSS rejects stationarity for 0/23. Median kurtosis 8.7, skew +0.11. Ljung-Box(10) rejects for 35% of symbols on r and 87% on r².
- **log RV.** ADF rejects for 23/23 and KPSS also rejects for 19/23. ACF is 0.60 at lag 1 and 0.23 at lag 22: long memory.
- **Why this matters.** Long memory and Markov switching are hard to tell apart (Diebold-Inoue 2001). A short-memory chain fitted to long-memory vol finds "regimes" whose durations depend on the sample; here thmm3 gives 52 days and ghmm3 15 days for the same calm state. That is the main reason the fitted regimes add little beyond HAR, which already encodes the memory.
- **Sampling.** Daily from 24 complete 1h bars. 43,015 symbol-days. The out-of-sample comparison uses 26,279 in-sample and 3,381 holdout symbol-days (1,642 / 147 dates).

**Candidate specs and their assumptions:**

| spec | assumes | plausible because | would imply if true |
|---|---|---|---|
| v0 cells | regimes are thresholds on multi-horizon trend z and the EWMA vol percentile, with 3-bar hysteresis | transparent, no fit, the house baseline | the gates carry information beyond continuous trend and vol |
| ghmm2/3 | r_d i.i.d. Gaussian given a first-order Markov state; switching mean and variance; constant transitions | vol clustering as discrete calm/turbulent states | state probabilities improve vol and density forecasts |
| thmm3 | as ghmm with t5 emissions | fat tails within a state (house rule 1.2) | fewer spurious crisis states; longer durations (seen: 52 / 48 / 10 days) |
| msar2 | Hamilton MS-AR(1) | regime-dependent autocorrelation (trend vs reversal) | AR sign differs by state; in practice it collapses to ghmm2 (ARI 0.88) |
| msvar3 | MS-VAR(1) on [r, log RV], Gaussian | RV is a far better vol signal than r² | sharper, shorter states (2-5 days) |
| tvtp3 | msvar3 with transition logits driven by funding z and OI change z | crowding drives state switches | drivers improve forecasts over constant transitions (they did not: QLIKE vs msvar3 +0.0001 in-sample) |
| bocpd | piecewise-constant mean of log RV, geometric run lengths (hazard 1/30), NIG prior | vol level shifts | the level after a change point predicts vol |
| pelt | the same, as an optimal partition with BIC penalty, run online | the offline equivalent | as bocpd |
| kmeans9 / gmm3 | static clusters of [vol percentile, dir_score, funding z, OI z]; no dynamics | "market states" as clusters of positioning and vol | clusters gate strategies better than the v0 grid |

**Assumption checks** (fit data; data/research/ws07/fits/*_diag.json):
- **Convergence.** EM converged in 96-100% of 183 fits per spec.
- **Degeneracy.** Smallest expected state occupancy: median 0.08-0.30. The third state of ghmm3 / thmm3 is about 4% of days (crisis); no fit was degenerate.
- **Calibration of the HAR-t densities.** In-sample: 90% coverage 0.900, lower and upper 1% tails 1.07% / 1.14%, Berkowitz p 0.07. Holdout: coverage 0.894, Berkowitz p 0.51. Regime conditioning leaves calibration unchanged; v0_cell gives coverage 0.899 / 0.891.
- **EWMA-t is badly calibrated.** z variance 1.23, coverage 0.86, Berkowitz p 0.000 in both segments.
- Parameter stability: see refit ARI in Dossier 3.

## How much the conclusions differ across specs, and which to trust

- **Variance.** The decision barely changes with the spec. Every conditioner moves QLIKE by under 1% in-sample, and the MCS cannot separate them. Trust log-HAR. ghmm3 conditioning is the only registered pass, and it is a small, legitimate add-on. Do not trust native MS variance forecasts.
- **Direction.** Every spec says the same: no direction.
- **Strategy gating.** Here the conclusion does depend on the spec, in the wrong way. The sign of the gating gain flips between segments for v0_cell, bocpd and the fitted books, which is what noise looks like.
  - The data cannot distinguish the gates.
  - Holdout MDEs are 0.8-2.3 Sharpe, and in-sample MDEs are 0.14 (ghmm3 sleeve) to 1.2 (books).
  - The one comparison with power, unconditional vs EW, says selection by overall performance is what helps.
- **Which spec to trust as a label:** v0 (no fit, stable by construction, interpretable) for communication. For a statistical vol state, use ghmm3 or msar2 filtered probabilities (stable, registered pass). Neither should gate strategies.

## Power

- **QLIKE.** MDE (80% power, 5% two-sided) for the ghmm3 difference is 0.0023 in-sample (0.6% of QLIKE) and 0.0092 in the holdout (2.4%). For har|v0_cell it is 0.0046 in-sample and 0.025 in the holdout. A holdout null for v0_cell is underpowered.
- **Log score.** MDE 0.0022 nats in-sample and 0.0061 in the holdout.
- **Sharpe differences.** In-sample MDE: sleeve 0.14 (ghmm3) to 0.8 (v0_cell); books about 1.1-1.2. Holdout MDE 0.8-2.3. Every holdout strategy verdict is underpowered.

## Caveats

- The strategy panel ends 2026-11-09 and its holdout (132 days) was already used to grade R-MAP-3..7, so ew_rules' holdout is not clean. The walk-forward books use only training data before each origin, but their holdout is the same 132 days.
- R-MAP-7 is approximated with day-level shock_regime P&L in start-of-day R2. The vol_state-normal and reversal-leg conditions were not applied.
- Deviation logged before results:
  - Warm-start-only refits after each symbol's first fit (2 random block starts at the first fit).
  - MS-GARCH and binary segmentation were not run, because the orchestrator asked to skip expensive specs.
  - R-PX-1 (vol state at shock time) was not tested.
- Daily unit only. Intraday regimes (TVTP report: session and vol states at 1h) are a different question.
- Universe L is survivors. Symbols listed after 2023 enter late. The HMM family needs 365 training days, so HYPE, ENA and SUI contribute only to the last folds.
- WS02's gap map was not available at freeze time. Completeness was enforced per day (24 bars).

## Method

- **Data.**
  - Bybit 1h last-price bars, universe L, via `research.frame.market_frame` as of 2026-11-26. Funding and 4h OI as of known_ts.
  - Day d: r_d = log close-to-close (00:00 UTC), RV_d = sum of 24 squared hourly returns.
  - The v0 cell recomputed here equals `strategy_panel_context` on 42,693 / 42,693 days.
- **State timing.** The state for day d is known at 00:00 UTC.
  - v0 is `regimes.label` on the 1h frame at the 00:00 row, which is the live gate's start-of-day cell.
  - Fitted specs use the filtered P(S_{d-1} | F_{d-1}) for labels and gates, and the predicted P(S_d | F_{d-1}) for forecasts.
  - Smoothed probabilities are descriptive only.
- **Walk-forward.**
  - Expanding window. Origins on Jan 1 / Jul 1 from 2022 to 2026-07-01 (10 folds). Training uses days up to origin minus 2 (1-day embargo), with at least 365 training days per symbol.
  - The causal filter runs with frozen parameters. The holdout is forecast only by fits made before 2026-07-01.
  - The 7-day targets are purged.
- **Specs.** HMM family via the reviewed TVTP module (EM, Hamilton filter, covariance prior). States are sorted by variance. The other specs are as in Dossier 1, with K fixed in advance.
- **Forecasts.**
  - Per-symbol log-HAR on log RV (daily, weekly, monthly), forecast exp(yhat + s²/2). EWMA = 24 × sigma_1h²; WS04 owns the vol tournament.
  - har|spec adds state probabilities as intercept shifts with ridge lambda 20. nat|spec is the model's own forecast.
  - Densities are Student-t with variance equal to the forecast and nu by MLE on training (floor 3). harmu adds state means shrunk with kappa 10 episodes.
- **Tests.**
  - QLIKE and log score; DM with HAC and the HLN correction on date-level means; Holm within families.
  - MCS: Hansen-Lunde-Nason T_max, 10-day moving blocks, 2000 draws, alpha 0.10.
  - Strategy comparisons: paired block bootstrap, Romano-Wolf stepdown, deflated Sharpe.
  - Encompassing: OLS with date-clustered SEs. R-VOL-8: OLS with 14-day-block clustered SEs.
- **Agreement.** ARI, VI in nats, run lengths, and refit ARI.
