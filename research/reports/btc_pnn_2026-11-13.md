# BTC polynomial-neuron network: quantiles, conformal bands and latent dynamics (H-PNN1 to H-PNN3)

_BTC field program 2026-11-13, agent F2. Data as of 2026-11-13; knowledge cutoff 2026-07-01. Code: `src/ai_trader/ml/pnn.py` (network, conformal, latent dynamics, symbolic extraction), `src/ai_trader/research/pnn_study.py` (design in its docstring), `src/ai_trader/research/pnn_report.py`; tests: `tests/test_pnn.py`; artifacts: `data/models/pnn/`. Deterministic research tools; the LLM decision layer is not trained or backtested here._

## Findings

- **1h: a ~500-parameter quantile network beats log-HAR-t, but the polynomial edges add nothing over ReLU.** PNN - log-HAR-t pinball -0.00082 (-7.26) in-sample and -0.00124 (-2.95) in the holdout; MLP - log-HAR-t -0.00083 (-7.82) / -0.00097 (-2.24); PNN - MLP 0.00002 (0.22) / -0.00027 (-1.07). The gain is small (0.4-0.6% of the loss) and comes from the hour-of-week factor and recent |returns|, which log-HAR-t enters only linearly in log variance.
- **1d: nothing beats log-HAR-t.** PNN - log-HAR-t 0.00244 (1.75) in-sample, -0.00819 (-1.84) holdout (133 dates); the MLP is no better (0.00020 (0.17) / -0.00722 (-1.38)). With about 2,000 daily rows the networks stay close to the unconditional quantiles. H-PNN1 contradicts in-sample and is inconclusive in the holdout, so pooled contradicts.
- **Calibration is not the bottleneck.** log-HAR-t bands are already within about 2 pp of nominal on BTC. ACI holds 1h holdout coverage within 0.2 pp at all three levels, but its 1h bands are 2-3% wider than log-HAR-t; at 1d (132 dates) the 90% ACI band covers 0.880. H-PNN2 is inconclusive: the coverage half nearly passes, the width half fails at 1h.
- **The latent space is predictable but useless as feedback.** Linear dynamics with the next-step calendar beat persistence by R^2 0.143 (1h) and 0.886 (1d) in the holdout (t 20.7 / 10.5). Feeding zhat back changes pinball by 0.00002 (0.27) / -0.00050 (-0.69). The predictable part is the calendar and vol persistence, which the head already sees, so part (b) fails and H-PNN3 is inconclusive.
- **What the neurons compute.** At 1h the most important inputs are the hour-of-week factor (lfac), the robust base sigma and the last hour's |return|. The tail increments (o05, o95) are driven by one latent that mixes vol level and hour of week through roughly cubic edges. At 1d one latent is a weekend clock (next_wk_hours -> z3, quadratic, R^2 0.995) that narrows the band on weekends; the others are vol-level mixes. About a third (1h) / a fifth (1d) of encoder edges are cubics with R^2 >= 0.99. The rest saturate in the tails (tanh basis), so the closed forms are approximations over the bulk of the data.
- **Inputs.** Signed flow (delta) has no multi-year BTC history in the lake (flow_bars start 2026-05), so it is absent. F1's `field.state` appeared after this design was fixed. It carries the same information (vol speeds, hour of week, jumps, momentum, funding, OI, regime); a rerun on it would be a new track record, not a change to this one.

## The polynomials inside the neurons (symbolic extraction)

Network: seed-0 PNN of the 2026-07 refit (labels to the end of the previous month). Each edge function phi(x) (x = standardised, clipped input; see `std_mu` / `std_sd` in the JSON to convert) is fitted on the training rows by least-squares polynomials of degree 1-3; the closed form is the lowest degree with R^2 >= 0.99 (else degree 3). Importance = sd of phi over the training rows. A latent unit is z_j = b_j + sum_i phi_ji(x_i); a quantile is built from the head edges of the 4 latents.
