## Verdicts in plain words

**Does a weekend MM in thin alt perps suffer adverse selection from news, beyond order flow? Not measurably.**
- With symbol fixed effects and the flow controls (aligned flow, |OFI|, VPIN, trade size, realised variance), the
  "within 60 min after a precise token news event" coefficient on maker markouts is:
  - +0.28 bp at 60 s (t 0.8) and -0.43 bp at 300 s (t -0.2) in-sample (1.09 M trades, 42 symbol-days);
  - +1.20 bp (t 1.9) and +1.57 bp (t 1.0) in the holdout (2.07 M trades, 43 symbol-days).
  - H-WMN-10 is inconclusive in both segments. The sign is not the adverse one.
  - The minimum detectable effect is about 1 bp in-sample and 1.8 bp in the holdout, so a 1-2 bp news effect cannot be
    excluded. A large one (the registered -1 bp at both horizons, t <= -2) is not there.
- Unlabelled jumps (the endogenous detector) do carry short-horizon adverse selection in the holdout, after the same
  controls: -0.81 bp at 1 s (t -3.1), -0.53 bp at 10 s (t -3.4) and -1.16 bp at 60 s (t -3.2).
  - The in-sample sign agrees only at 1 s (-0.42, t -2.5); at 60 s it is -0.27 (t -0.7), and at 300 s it is positive in both.
  - H-WMN-11 is inconclusive by its registered rule (-1 bp at both 60 s and 300 s, in-sample first).
  - Read it as about 1 bp of extra adverse selection in the first minute after a detected jump, gone by 5 minutes.
- Flow toxicity is the larger and more consistent driver. Trading with the 5-min flow (aligned) costs the maker
  +1.56 bp per unit at 60 s in-sample (t 3.0). Realised variance adds +0.6 to +0.75 bp per log unit in the holdout (t 3.6).
- So the answer to the user's question: **the information hazard a free news feed can see is small next to flow and
  jumps. What matters is the price-detected jump, not the headline.**

**Free news sources label almost none of the information arrival.**
- Of 23,316 in-sample and 7,632 holdout idiosyncratic jump episodes on the 57 core alts, 0.18% / 0.12% have a tagged
  exchange announcement or HN story within [-2 h, +30 min]. The placebo times 7 days earlier score 0.28% / 0.12%:
  the "explained" share is at chance (H-WMN-2 supports, both segments).
- The reverse direction also fails. After 112 in-sample exchange announcements on core names, a jump episode follows
  within 60 min 2.7% of the time, against 4.5% for the placebo windows: lift 0.6, H-WMN-1 contradicts. The holdout has
  0 of 30, inconclusive.
- The endogenous detector is the information-arrival signal for the gate. It is not validated by news, because the
  news we can time almost never coincides with jumps.

**What news does move.** Event minus placebo, log ratios, date-clustered.
- **Listings on another exchange (OKX / KuCoin), 56 events, H-WMN-4 supports in both segments:**
  - abnormal variance in the first hour +0.61 (t 2.6), +0.55 at 1-4 h (t 3.3);
  - volume +0.91 (t 3.5);
  - |CAR| over 48 h +9.3 percentage points (t 2.3);
  - holdout ARV +0.41, positive sign but only 11 events.
- **Bybit delistings, 57 events incl. perps rebuilt from the trade archive, H-WMN-4 supports in both segments:**
  ARV[0,1h) +0.70 (t 2.2), volume +0.83 (t 2.6); holdout +0.05 on 6 events.
- **Bybit spot listing of a token that already has a perp:** ARV +1.12 (t 4.5), volume +1.78 (t 5.5). These are the
  largest effects, but there are only 26 FIT events, below the registered 30, so the category is inconclusive.
- **Hacks at day resolution:** ARV over 24 h +1.36 (t 4.0) on 28 events; also below 30, so inconclusive.
- **Network upgrades / deposit maintenance (365 events) and HN stories (184):** volume rises, variance does not.
  ARV[0,1h) -0.02 and +0.09, inconclusive. HN is contradicted in the holdout (-0.04).
- **Pooled precise token news (H-WMN-3 inconclusive):** ARV[0,1h) +0.163 (t 2.9, registered t >= 3) in-sample and
  +0.187 (t 1.6) in the holdout. The pre-event 2 hours already show +0.19 (t 3.8): announcements arrive on names that
  are already more active, so most of the pooled excess is not caused by the announcement.

**Market-wide news.** US macro releases (CPI / NFP / FOMC) raise BTC variance in the first hour by +1.12 log units
(about x3, t 4.1, 31 releases) in-sample and +1.59 (t 3.9, 9) in the holdout (H-WMN-12 supports both). The EW alt index
shows the same (+1.22, t 4.9; holdout +1.24, t 2.5). HN bursts on bitcoin / crypto do nothing (-0.22, t -1.0). Hacks of
at least 100 M USD pass BH on BTC at day resolution (+0.71) on only 2 events, which is anecdotal.

**Direction: unlabelled jumps revert, news moves are not shown to continue (H-WMN-5 inconclusive).**
- Over the next 47 hours, unlabelled jumps give back 28% of their first-hour move in-sample (slope -0.28, t -2.2, 5,806
  jumps) and 26% in the holdout (t -3.4). This is the overreaction / liquidity signature, and it is consistent across segments.
- News moves (95 events with |SCAR| >= 2): slope -0.01 in-sample and -0.65 in the holdout (17 events). There is no
  evidence of continuation, and the difference from jumps is not significant (t 0.7 / -1.9).

**Weekend shocks are smaller, not larger (H-WMN-6 contradicts, both segments).** The variance response ARV[0,4h) to
jumps and news on Sat/Sun is 0.20 lower than on weekdays (t -3.3) in-sample and 0.13 lower (t -1.7) in the holdout.
Relative to its own (lower) weekend baseline, a weekend shock relaxes faster or is smaller. Thin weekend books do not
amplify it in these data.

**Decay of shock variance.**
- **After unlabelled jumps it is a power law, and stable across segments.** The Omori form beats a single exponential
  out of sample (log-score gain t 3.9 in-sample, t 2.0 in the holdout):
  - m(tau) = B + A (1 + tau / c)^-p with c 0.76 min, p 0.71, A 31, B 1.8 (holdout refit c 0.89, p 0.74, A 30, B 1.85);
  - the half-life of the excess is about 1.3 min, but its tail is long: still about 3x baseline at 20 min and about
    2x at 4 h (the B = 1.8 level, a regime shift that the trailing sigma later absorbs);
  - the exponential alternative has T 103 min.
- **After precise news events there is no decay to fit.** The excess is a small, flat level over 48 h (EXP T 2,543 min,
  A 0.7, B 1.5). POW and EXP tie (t -0.9 / +0.8).
- H-WMN-7 needed both shock types to pass, so it is inconclusive.
- **"News relaxes faster" (Joulin et al.) cannot be decided (H-WMN-8 inconclusive).** The persistence ratio
  news / unlabelled is 1.9 in-sample (90% CI 0.17-4.0, 123 news shocks) and 0.28 in the holdout (CI 0.10-0.97, 23).
  The two segments disagree and the in-sample CI is wide.

**Hawkes with exogenous news marks: a small, not significant gain (H-WMN-9 inconclusive).**
- The mean log-score gain is +0.33 per day in-sample (t 1.5) and +1.8 in the holdout (t 1.8).
- About 87% of jump intensity is baseline, 11-12% comes from BTC jumps (BTC branching 0.08), and 1.2-2% from macro
  releases. Exchange news contributes 0.01% and HN 0.03%.
- Self-excitation of idiosyncratic 5m jumps is almost nil (branching 0.002). That differs from BTC's own 0.30 in WS05:
  idiosyncratic jumps cluster with BTC, not with themselves.

**The gate (frozen, H-WMN-13 inconclusive).**
- On holdout weekend bars, multiplying the base sigma^2 by the gate's M(t) lowers QLIKE by 0.022 (t -1.7, 22 weekends;
  13 of 22 improve). The registered bar is t < -2.
- Pull minutes (3.1% of weekend time) carry 2.6x the normalised realised variance of other minutes (90% CI 2.2-3.1);
  the registered bar of 2x is met.
- All of the improvement comes from the pull part (QLIKE -0.78 on pull bars). The long news "level" kernels add +0.002
  on other bars, i.e. no help.
- The trade-level pull dummy (H-WMN-14) has -0.98 bp markouts at 60 s in the holdout (t -1.6, registered -2 bp, t -2):
  inconclusive.

**What to use and what not to use.**
- Use the jump part of the gate: a pull for roughly 5-10 minutes after each detected idiosyncratic jump (D episode;
  the length depends on w), a market pull for up to ~25 minutes after BTC jumps and after US releases, and the 2-minute
  pre-release pull.
- Do not widen on headlines. Exchange announcements, maintenance and HN stories add volume, not variance, and no
  adverse selection beyond flow. The one exception worth a manual pull is a delisting of the quoted perp
  (kernel A 70, T 5.5 min; the MM should not quote a delisting perp anyway).
- Keep flow toxicity (aligned flow) as the main quoting hazard. News adds nothing measurable to it.

## The frozen gate (for WMQ)

- **Rule:** src/ai_trader/research/wmn_gate.py, with the formula and thresholds as registered.

- events.parquet has 17,464 rows. Precise token events (exchange announcements and HN, merged within 2 h per symbol)
  number 3,261 symbol-rows, but only 769 fall on the 58 core names. The event study used 1,399 events (838 core,
  523 non-core 5m windows, 38 delisted perps from the archive) and 1,072 placebos.
- **Timestamp check (descriptive).** When publishTime is more than 1 h later than dateTimestamp, the variance excess is

- **Coverage gaps.** Spot delisting titles ("Bybit to Delist 5 Token(s)") name no token, and announcement bodies were not

- **Survivorship.** Delisted perps enter only through the trade archive (60-event cap); every other price series is a survivor.

### 2. Endogenous information arrival (detector)

- **Variable:** idiosyncratic 5m jump episodes (D: |z| > 4.31 and turnover >= 3x the median), the gate's main input.
- **Counts:** 23,316 FIT and 7,632 holdout episodes on 57 alts, about 0.75 and 0.9 per symbol-day. That is about 80x the
  nominal 1% per day, as WS05 found for BTC: "jump" means a move larger than about 4.3 local bipower sd.
- **Validation against news fails both ways** (H-WMN-1 / 2 above). The price-only variant Dp gives the same answer
  (lift 1.0 in-sample).
- **Not done:** a size / power check of the volume filter (the volume filter removes only about 6% of price-only detections).

### 3. Event studies

- **Token categories:** see the verdicts; the full table is in data/research/wmn/results/event_tests.json.
- **Families:** BH at q = 0.10 over 5 eligible categories x 4 metrics. Significant: bybit_delist (ARV h0-1, h1-4, AV),
  x_listing (all 4), maintenance AV and |CAR|, hn_story AV.
- **Clustered SEs:** 30-370 date clusters per category; holdout categories have 1-98 events, so holdout checks are sign-only.
- **Spread / depth:** not measured; ob200 was not downloaded and flow_3s covers only November.

### 4. Decay

- **Specs:** EXP and POW on Student-t returns (nu 2.6-3.1). Both assume one common kernel per shock type and that
  shocks do not overlap within 48 h. Overlapping jumps inflate the tail; the B > 1 level partly absorbs that.
- **Profile of mean x = e^2 / sigma_b^2 after unlabelled jumps (holdout):** 10.7 at 5 min, 5.8 at 10-15 min, 4.3 at
  20-30 min, 2.8 at 1-2 h, 2.0 at 4-8 h, 1.9-2.0 at 8-48 h.
- **Trust:** POW for jumps, with stable parameters across segments and DM t 3.9 / 2.0. Nothing for news, where the
  data cannot distinguish the specs and the effect is a level.
- **Power:** news shocks number 938 FIT / 210 holdout, but the score differences are about 1e-5 nats per bar. The data
  cannot separate the shapes of a near-zero excess.

### 5. Hawkes with marks

- **Fit:** cutoff fit on 8.45 M bins with 23,316 episodes. M0 baseline scale 0.88, self 0.0004 (half-life 20 min),
  BTC 0.0157.
- **M1 additions:** exchange 0.0096, HN 0.0091, macro 0.0233 (news half-life 15 min). These are expected jumps per news
  event: 0.05 for exchange and HN, 0.11 for macro.
- **Assumption not tested:** goodness of fit by time rescaling (WS05 found a single exponential kernel fails it for BTC).

### 6. Markouts

- **Sample:** 85 of 96 selected symbol-days fetched. 9 were missing from the archive (TRUMP launch week, MON, GRAM) and
  2 were too large (TRUMPUSDT 2025-01-18 / 20, more than 150 MB compressed). 3.16 M sampled trades, weighted.
- **Raw means (holdout, 60 s):**
  - quiet -0.50 bp, news -0.54, jump windows -0.36;
  - effective half-spread +2.3 to +3.6 bp;
  - the 60-s markout keeps most of the spread on average.
- **Controls:** 42-43 symbol-day clusters only, so the t-statistics rely on few clusters.

## Caveats

- **Clusters.** Few holdout events per category (1-98) and few markout clusters (43). Most holdout checks are sign checks.
- **Timestamps.** Bybit publishTime is late for some announcements (dilutes the Bybit effects). HN timestamps are
  precise, but HN is an echo, not a source. Day-precision hacks cannot drive an intraday gate.
- **Universe.** 58 core names, survivor-only (except the 38 delisted-perp events). Results may not hold for the
  thinnest perps outside the core.
- **Gate interplay.** The gate's base sigma is the trailing 288-bar bipower; the live quoter uses max(240-min, 30-min)
  sigma, which absorbs a shock within about 30 minutes. In WMQ's replay, the jump pull matters mainly in the first
  minutes after a jump, which the quoter's sigma has not yet absorbed.
- **News kernels.** They are flat levels (T > 48 h). They raise widen to 1.1-1.3 for two days after any news and did not
  help QLIKE. WMQ should test the pull alone and pull + widen as separate conditional arms, both already defined by the
  frozen rule.

## Method

**Registration.** H-WMN-1..15 were appended to research/hypotheses.yaml under the registry lock at about 22:07 UTC on
2026-11-27 (the YAML header says ~22:15; the diary entry "WMN design frozen before results" is stamped 22:07:08), before
any event-study, decay, Hawkes, markout or gate statistic. "WMN implementation clarifications before results" (22:27)
lists the details fixed before results. FIT / llm_in_sample means known_ts < 2026-07-01; clean_holdout runs
2026-07-01 .. 2026-11-27; weekends from 2026-11-28 are forward (H-WMN-15).

**Prices.** Bybit public /v5/market/kline, linear, 5-minute bars, 58 core crypto perps (wmn_data.CORE: 6 majors, the 21
MM names of config/mm_illiq20.yaml and research/mm_weekend/universe.json, 31 alts already in the lake), from 2025-01-01
or the listing. Log close-to-close returns of consecutive bars only. Non-core tagged perps: 5m windows of +-50 h around
the event and its placebo. Delisted perps (no klines once closed): 5m bars rebuilt from the public trade archive.

**Detector.** e = r - beta r_BTC, beta = OLS over the previous 7 days (updated daily); hour-of-day factor from the
previous 30 days (shrunk with 3 pseudo-days); spot sigma = bipower over the previous 288 bars of e/f; z = (e/f)/sigma.
D = |z| > 4.31 (Lee-Mykland, 1% per day at n = 288) and turnover >= 3x the median of the previous 288 bars; Dp = price
only. Detections within 60 min of the previous one are one episode, known at the close of its first bar.

**Event study.** tau = 0 is the 5m bar containing the anchor (known_ts; the day start for day-precision events). Baseline
[-50 h, -2 h]: beta, bipower sigma_b, median turnover. ARV = log(mean e^2 / sigma_b^2) over a window; AV = log(mean
turnover / baseline median); CAR = sum of e; SCAR = CAR / (sigma_b sqrt(n)). Placebo = the same symbol 7 days earlier,
dropped when another tagged event of that symbol lies within 48 h of it. Event minus placebo, paired by event, standard
errors clustered by UTC anchor date (CR1). BH at q = 0.10 with one-sided p-values (H-WMN-4, H-WMN-12 secondary set).

**Decay.** For each shock, z_k = e_k / sigma_b for k = 1..576 bars (5 min .. 48 h) after the shock bar. m(tau) = B +
A g(tau), g = exp(-tau/T) (EXP) or (1 + tau/c)^-p (POW); z_k ~ unit-variance Student-t (nu fitted) scaled by sqrt(m).
Pooled MLE with analytic gradients (checked numerically; at most 4000 shocks per fit). Out-of-sample per-shock mean log
score; llm_in_sample = 2-fold time split inside FIT, clean_holdout = fit on all FIT; DM t = mean / date-clustered SE.
Persistence ratio PR = mean(x - 1) over (4 h, 48 h] / mean(x - 1) over (5 min, 1 h], x = z^2, date-cluster bootstrap.

**Hawkes.** Discrete 5m bins on the core alts, cloglog with a linear intensity: symbol baseline (training-window episode
rate x shrunk hour factor), own and BTC past episodes (common half-life profiled over 10 / 20 / 40 min in M0 and reused
in M1), exponentially decayed counts of precise exchange announcements, HN stories (tagged) and macro releases (common
news half-life profiled over 15 min / 1 h / 4 h / 16 h). Linear parameters >= 0 by MLE with an analytic gradient. M1 vs
M0 (no news terms): per-day sums of log-score differences, Newey-West t (lag 7 days).

**Markouts.** Public trade archives of sampled symbol-days (news days, unlabelled-jump days, control days 7 or 14 days
earlier, per segment). Maker-side markout MO(h) = d (p - m(t + h)) / m(t-) x 1e4 with the midpoint proxy of the
registry header; controls aligned flow d x OFI_5m, |OFI_5m|, VPIN_1h, relative log size, log RV_1h; symbol fixed
effects; inverse-probability weights; CR1 by symbol-day.

**Gate.** src/ai_trader/research/wmn_gate.py with kernels fitted on FIT shocks (wmn_run stage_decay -> gate_params.json).
Evaluation at every 5m bar close of the weekend windows (Fri 00:00 - Mon 00:00 UTC): F_base = sum of (sigma_base f)^2
over the next 6 bars (sigma_base = detector machinery on total returns), F_gate = F_base x M(t), RV = sum of r^2 over
those bars; QLIKE = log F + RV / F; weekend means of the difference, t over weekends.

**Compute and storage.** Every job ran through scripts/ops/governor.py (45 WMN jobs; queue waits 1,175 s in total, max 325 s).
Main runtimes: detector 45 s, token event study 19 min (prefetching non-core windows), episodes 2 min, Hawkes 14 min, decay
17 min, markout sample 2 min, gate eval 11 s. data/research/wmn: 0 before, 403 MB at the end (quota 500 MB; detector files
and post-shock arrays compacted to float32 / float16). data/raw/WMN: 0 left (announcement JSON deleted by `build`, trade
archives deleted per file).

**What went wrong.**
1. A lake inventory query ran outside the governor for ~2 min (stopped and re-run in the light pool).
2. The events fetch was restarted three times: a TradFi tagging fix, a KuCoin endless-window bug, and HN typo / prefix
   matching. The stopped HN pass was never saved.
3. The first kline download was cut short by rate-limit replies for about 30 symbols, because another agent was
   downloading at the same time. A backfill with retries completed every history.
4. Memory caps were hit by es_market, es_token (a 406 MB trade-archive file decompressed in memory), the markout
   features and gate_eval. Each was rewritten to stream or chunk and re-run; no design change.
5. Markout day files from a first run were discarded, and the run repeated, after the feature code changed.
6. GDELT answered HTTP 429 to every request and was skipped (not worked around).
7. The registration time in the YAML header is wrong (~22:15 vs ~22:07, diary mistake entry).
8. The 5-hour session limit stopped the agent mid-run. The build had already finished, so the work resumed from it.
