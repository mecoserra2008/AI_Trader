## Dossier A: vol-standardised forward returns (direction), BTC / ETH / ALT, 1h-30d

**1. Variable and decision.** y = log return over (t, t+h] / (ex-ante sigma x sqrt h). It feeds entries: whether any directional
predictor deserves a strategy or a gate (R-AVOID-1, the desk's "regime and players" read).

**2. Properties (llm_in_sample, `props.json`).**
- Tails: the 1h targets have kurtosis 20-30 and a Student-t nu of 2.8 / 2.9 / 3.6 (BTC / ETH / ALT); daily targets have kurtosis
  6.4-7.5 and nu 3.1-4.1. The EWMA standardisation removes the clustering but not the tails.
- Serial dependence: Ljung-Box(10) on levels p 0.10-0.52 (no linear dependence); on squares p 0.001-0.04 at 1h (residual
  clustering) and 0.27-0.53 at 1d for ETH / ALT. ADF rejects a unit root everywhere.
- Sampling: hourly 2020-04 .. 2026-11 (54.6k BTC hours in-sample, about 3.1-3.6k in the holdout); daily 2,244 / 149 days.
  Gaps (PC suspends in Nov 2026) give missing targets; forward sums require every hour present, so gap windows drop out (the
  WS02 gap map was not applied separately).

**Predictor properties** (daily, BTC versions, in-sample): ADF rejects for all except the DVOL level (p 0.29). KPSS rejects
stationarity (p <= 0.05) for funding, the funding percentile, L/S, basis, term basis, DVOL, VRP, Coinbase premia and stablecoin
growth. AR(1) persistence: stablecoin 0.992 (half-life 91 d), DVOL 0.982 (39 d), Fear & Greed 0.966, term basis 0.957, skew
0.944, VRP 0.927, L/S 0.922; funding 0.78, Coinbase raw 0.86, adjusted 0.49, OI change -0.07, NDX return 0.26. Funding is very
skewed (skew 4.5, kurtosis 29). Hourly L/S has rho 0.997. These are exactly the near-integrated, endogenous regressors for which
OLS t-statistics over-reject. Under the house rule (ADF + KPSS) most would be excluded or transformed; we instead use IVX, which
is valid whatever the degree of persistence. Transforming (e.g. differencing funding) would test a different hypothesis.

**3. Specifications and their assumptions.**

| spec | assumes | why plausible here | if true |
|---|---|---|---|
| OLS + NW | stationary x, exogenous innovations, HAC handles overlap | standard Welch-Goyal baseline | slope = predictability |
| OLS + Hodrick 1B | as NW, but uses one-period residuals (better size for overlapping h) | 7d / 30d overlap 7x / 30x | same |
| Stambaugh correction | AR(1) x with innovations correlated with returns | funding / basis / DVOL innovations move with price | small-sample bias removed |
| IVX (KMS) | x anywhere from I(0) to local-to-unit-root | KPSS rejects for most predictors | valid inference under persistence |
| block bootstrap + RW | dependence within 60-day blocks; joint over cells | crypto moves together; predictors overlap | FWER control over 196 cells |
| quantile regression | conditional quantiles linear in x | crowding may move tails, not means | risk input rather than signal |
| expanding / rolling OOS | parameter stability | regimes change (2021 vs 2024) | usable forecast |

**4. Assumption checks.**
- Stambaugh bias is small here: at most 0.013 sd per sd (in the long-horizon cells); e.g. stablecoin ALT 7d slope 0.149 ->
  0.152. That is because T is large relative to persistence at hourly and daily frequency.
- IVX and OLS-NW agree on direction. IVX is stricter for the persistent daily regressors: 44 cells have |NW t| > 1.96, 33 pass
  both IVX forms.
- Hodrick t is smaller than NW t at 7d / 30d, as expected: for example stablecoin ALT 7d 2.21 versus 2.58, and BTC 30d
  funding percentile 1.37 versus 1.78.
- **Stability:** in-sample and holdout slopes agree in sign in 104 of 196 cells (53%), which is what you get from no signal;
  for the significant ones, see the casualties table. The rolling-window GW
  t-statistic is negative for every Coinbase-premium cell that passes CW: the effect cannot be captured with a 90-day window
  estimate, a sign of instability.

**5. Out-of-sample.**
- 69 of 196 cells have a positive OOS R^2 in the in-sample OOS block and 70 in the holdout, against the prevailing mean, which
  is itself a tough benchmark.
- 36 cells have CW p < 0.05 and 6 survive Holm (basis BTC 1h; Coinbase raw BTC 1h / 4h, ETH 4h; adjusted BTC 4h, ALT 4h).
- Only the basis cell keeps a positive holdout OOS R^2 (+0.04%). None passes the conditional GW test at 5%.

**6. Do the specs disagree, and which to trust?**
- **They agree on the conclusion.** No spec finds a tradeable effect. The OLS-NW t makes 44 cells look interesting; IVX,
  Hodrick and above all the joint RW bootstrap cut that to 2, and the holdout to 1.
- **The decision does not change across specs:** no directional predictor earns a strategy.
- **Which to trust:** RW over the joint bootstrap (it accounts for the 196 trials and their correlation) plus IVX (it accounts
  for persistence). The OLS-NW t alone is not trustworthy for these regressors: in our simulation it had 13.7% size at 30d
  with rho 0.99.

**7. Power.**

| horizon | median independent obs (in-sample) | MDE corr single test | MDE corr after RW | break-even corr, BTC taker / maker | largest observed abs slope |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1h | 46,000 | 0.013 | 0.024 | 0.29 / 0.23 | 0.034 |
| 4h | 11,500 | 0.026 | 0.048 | 0.15 / 0.12 | 0.044 |
| 1d | 1,890 | 0.064 | 0.118 | 0.051 / 0.041 | 0.089 |
| 7d | 269 | 0.171 | 0.313 | 0.019 / 0.015 | 0.168 |
| 30d | 62 | 0.356 | 0.652 | 0.009 / 0.007 | 0.252 |

(Break-even for a sign rule: E[sign(x) y] = corr x sqrt(2/pi) under normality, equated to cost / sigma_h. The MDE is the
two-sided 5%, 80%-power value.) At 1h / 4h a break-even effect would have been detected easily, so the null is
informative. At 1d+ a break-even effect sits far below the MDE, so those nulls are underpowered. The holdout (149 days, 20
weeks, 4 months) can confirm only effects with corr above about 0.2 at 1d.

**Economics (H-WS08-14).** BTC basis 1h: +1.08 bp gross per trade in-sample (+1.42 holdout), net -9.9 bp taker / -7.7 bp maker
entry; net in sigma units -0.24 [-0.27, -0.21]. Raw Coinbase premium BTC 1h: +1.36 bp in-sample, -0.17 bp in the holdout. The
deflated Sharpe is about 0 for every cell. Of the 196 sign rules, 82 are positive net in-sample and 33 in both segments, mostly
30d cells with about 60 / 4 independent trades: noise.

## Dossier B: next-week cross-sectional returns of Bybit perps (WS09 core)

**1. Variable and decision.** Next-week log return (and total return including funding) of each liquid perp relative to the
cross-section, vol-scaled by the trailing 30d daily sigma. It feeds alt selection inside the trend sleeve and the universe choice.

**2. Properties.**
- The universe is the 23 long-history universe-L perps: the only crypto perps with history in the lake (30 others start
  2026-11-05). The median number passing the 20 M USD filter is 16 per week (241 in-sample weeks, 20 holdout weeks).
- These are today's survivors: no delisted perp is in the lake. WS09's REST probe of delisted histories left no files. The
  momentum premium is biased up and reversal / low-vol premia have unknown bias.

**3. Specifications.** Tercile sorts (assume monotone, no functional form, net of turnover) versus Fama-MacBeth on ranks
(linear in rank, robust to outliers, joint control for the other characteristics) versus FM on raw beta with Shanken (errors
in variables of the estimated beta).

**4. Checks.**
- Joint and single-characteristic FM differ for beta (-0.04, t -0.77 versus -0.11, t -2.54). Beta's apparent premium overlaps
  with carry and momentum; with 16 names the joint regression has little power.
- The Shanken correction barely moves the raw-beta t (-1.41 -> -1.39) because the BTC weekly factor variance is large
  relative to the premium.

**5-6. Results and agreement.**
- Sorts and FM agree that no characteristic is priced after RW (p 0.53-0.94).
- The trend-sleeve test, which bears directly on the decision, finds the side-signed momentum rank useless in-sample (-0.09 R
  per unit rank, t -0.47) and harmful in the holdout (filter -0.27 R, CI [-0.43, -0.05], 231 trades).

**7. Power.** With 16 names and 241 weeks, an FM t of 2 needs an IC of about 0.13 per week, a large effect. Annualised net
Sharpes of 0.4-0.9 (MOM, carry, low beta) would need about 10-25 years of data to reach t 2. Underpowered: the cross-sectional
null is weak evidence, except for the trend-sleeve filter, whose holdout result points the wrong way.

## Dossier C: same-day crypto returns on macro moves (betas; WS18 core)

**1. Variable and decision.** Weekday UTC-day vol-standardised return on same-day NDX return, real-yield change and dollar
change. It feeds risk-on / risk-off context, stress scenarios and macro-day sizing. These are contemporaneous betas, not
forecasts.

**2. Properties.**
- 1,524 in-sample weekdays (BTC) and 97 in the holdout.
- The NDX beta dominates (t 10-13). The dollar is negative (t -3 to -4). Real yields are insignificant once NDX and the
  dollar are in.
- HY OAS (from 2023-11, 652 days) adds nothing beyond NDX (t -1.2).

**3. Specifications.** Constant (expanding OLS; assumes a stable beta), rolling 90 (assumes a beta that drifts on a ~3-month
scale; costs estimation noise), Kalman random-walk (assumes a slowly drifting beta with Q estimated by MLE).

**4. Checks.** The MLE state variances are tiny for NDX (BTC 8.7e-06, ETH 1.0e-04, ALT 1.5e-05 per day), so the filter
essentially returns a constant beta (in-sample Kalman NDX beta sd 0.035 for BTC).

**5. OOS.**
- Constant ≈ Kalman > rolling-90 on squared error and Gaussian log score: Kalman versus rolling DM t -2.07 / -3.13 / -1.67
  in-sample and -1.21 / -1.59 / -1.58 in the holdout; log score -2.13 / -3.14 / -2.67.
- In the holdout the constant beta beats the Kalman beta (t +1.23 / +1.73 / +1.73, not significant).

**6.** The conclusion is robust across specs: betas are stable enough that the noise of a 90-day window outweighs any drift.
Trust the constant / long-window beta.

**Asymmetry.** b(NDX down) - b(NDX up): BTC +0.30 (t 1.67), ETH +0.33 (1.28), ALT +0.24 (0.99), Holm p 0.29-0.40; holdout
+0.50 / +1.45 / +0.67 (ETH t 2.10). Same sign in 6 of 6, never significant after Holm.

**7. Power.** A beta difference of 0.3 with SE about 0.2 needs about 2.5x the sample for t 2: inconclusive because
underpowered, and leaning towards an asymmetry.

## Caveats and what went wrong

- **The long-horizon IVX is our own variant.** It was not reproduced from KMS (2015). Its simulated size is 10.7-11% at
  k = 7 / 30 (rho 0.99, corr -0.9), so h > 1 cells had to pass both forms. A first version (aggregated instrument) had 73% size
  and was replaced before any result (diary). The one-period KMS statistic has 6.3% size.
- **Hodrick 1B is the reverse-regression form with a White SE, and the Stambaugh bias for h > 1 is scaled approximately.**
  Neither decides any verdict: RW + IVX do.
- **The H-WS08-13 registration was inconsistent** (statement versus success line on GW). The stricter reading is recorded; both
  are reported.
- **The cross-section is 23 survivors** (median 16 liquid names). Survivorship is not corrected, and the delisted history WS09
  was probing never landed (no files). Power is low.
- **Data limits.**
  - Perp index klines end 2026-11-07 (basis holdout 4 months); deribit_svi (skew, term basis) ends 2026-10-09; DefiLlama
    stablecoin supply is revisable (as of 2026-11-06 plus 1 d).
  - FRED graph-CSV series are latest-vintage. Daily market series (DFII10, DTWEXBGS, NASDAQ100) are essentially unrevised, but
    the dollar index can be revised.
  - No ETF-flow data in the lake, so that predictor was not tested.
  - ALT uses BTC DVOL / skew / Coinbase premium / term basis.
- **The Coinbase premium is built from public hourly candle closes** (Coinbase Exchange BTC-USD / ETH-USD / USDT-USD, Binance
  spot). Close-to-close timing differences between venues add noise; a 1-minute VWAP version (as in H-XV2) could be sharper.
  USDT-USD on Coinbase starts 2021-05.
- **The WS02 gap map was not applied explicitly.** Targets need every hour present, so suspend gaps drop out. Predictors are
  as-of joined with staleness caps (2-8 h hourly, 3 h-5 d daily).
- **The trend-sleeve test uses gross R** from the stress-harness trades (pegged entry). Costs are the same across the filter,
  so the comparison holds; the levels are gross.

## Method (frozen before results)

(written before the results; unchanged after)

**Targets.** BTC, ETH (Bybit linear perps) and ALT (equal-weight basket of the other 21 universe-L perps, >= 10 present).
Log return over (origin, origin + h], divided by the ex-ante EWMA(0.94) sigma x sqrt(h) (house rule 1.1). Horizons 1h, 4h
(hourly origins = bar closes) and 1d, 7d, 30d (daily origins 00:00 UTC; sigma from daily returns).

**Predictors** (as of known_ts <= origin; the variable, its source and why it matters for a decision):

| id | definition | source / PIT | decision it would feed |
|---|---|---|---|
| fund | sum of funding settled in the last 24 h (interval-agnostic); ALT = mean over alts | Bybit funding, native | crowding gate, R-AVOID-1 |
| fund_pct | percentile of fund within the trailing 365 daily values (>= 180) | derived | "funding vs its range" in the desk read |
| basis | ln(perp last / index), 24 h mean (daily), last bar (hourly); BTC, ETH; to 2026-11-07 | Bybit index klines | leverage demand |
| tbasis | annualised 30d futures basis implied by Deribit options (08:00 fit); to 2026-10-09 | deribit_svi, native | term leverage demand |
| doi | ln OI change over 24 h (daily) / 4 h (hourly), 4h OI prints | Bybit OI, native | "OI rebuilding" pattern (H-RB1-3) |
| ls | ln(long share / short share) of accounts, 4h; 2021-08 .. 2026-11-07 | Bybit ls_ratio | retail positioning |
| cbp_raw / cbp_adj | ln(Coinbase BTC-USD / Binance spot BTCUSDT), minus ln(Coinbase USDT-USD) for adj; hourly closes, 24 h mean (daily) | public REST candles 2020+ (downloaded, native) | US spot demand (H-XV2) |
| stable | 30d log change of total stablecoin supply, z vs trailing 365 d | DefiLlama, revisable (+1 d) | liquidity (H-FU1) |
| dvol / ddvol | DVOL level; 24 h (daily) / 4 h (hourly) log change | Deribit, native | implied-vol regime |
| vrp | (DVOL/100)^2 - trailing 30 d realised variance (annualised) | derived | risk premium (H-FU2) |
| skew | 25d risk reversal, 30 d (08:00 fit); to 2026-10-09 | deribit_svi | crash demand |
| ry / usd / ndx | 5-obs change in DFII10, 5-obs log change in DTWEXBGS, last NDX daily log return | FRED graph CSV, known obs_date + 1 d 00:00 UTC | risk-on / risk-off gate |
| fng | Fear & Greed level (D-1 value at the D 00:00 origin) | alternative.me, native | sentiment |

Not used: ETF flows (not in the lake); the participant-type mix (PART3 / PART9 already tested 1-60 min on 62 days: nothing beyond
OFI; a 1h version would be a 50-day sample). Daily-only predictors are not used at 1h/4h.

**Per-cell inference** (cell = predictor x target x horizon; 196 cells (171 in the first draft of this line was a miscount)): OLS slope per in-sample sd of x; Newey-West t
(lag max(1.5h, 4(n/100)^(2/9))); Hodrick (1992) 1B t via the reverse regression of the one-period standardised return on the
h-sum of x (Wei-Wright form); Stambaugh (1999) bias of the one-period slope with AR(1) x, carried to h by sum rho^j / sqrt(h)
(approximate); IVX Wald (Kostakis-Magdalinos-Stamatogiannis 2015) one-period statistic with the FM correction, plus a long-horizon
variant (k-sum of returns on the unaggregated instrument, null-imposed NW lag 2k) whose size we simulated (T 2000, rho 0.99,
corr(e,u) -0.9): 6.3% (k=1), 10.7% (k=7), 11.0% (k=30), against 7.3 / 12.7 / 13.7% for the OLS-NW t. Because the variant is
mildly oversized, an h > 1 cell must pass both forms (stricter than registered). Joint moving-block bootstrap over days (60-day
blocks, B = 999) of every cell's in-sample sufficient statistics, so cross-cell dependence is kept; Romano-Wolf step-down on the
bootstrap-studentised slopes over all cells (FWER 5%). Survival (H-WS08-1): RW p < 0.05 AND IVX p < 0.05 (both forms for h > 1)
AND |Hodrick t| > 1.96 (h > 1) in-sample, then the same sign in the clean holdout.

**Segments.** llm_in_sample = origins before 2026-07-01 whose target window closed before it; clean_holdout = origins from
2026-07-01 (read once). Out of sample: expanding purged OLS (only pairs whose target closed before the origin) vs the prevailing
mean, OOS R^2 and Clark-West MSPE-adjusted t (NW lag h), from one year after each cell's first observation; rolling-window
(365 d daily, 90 d hourly) conditional Giacomini-White (instruments 1, lagged loss difference). Holm across cells.

**Quantile regressions** (H-WS08-12): tau 0.1 / 0.5 / 0.9 on 1d (all days) and 7d (Mondays, non-overlapping) for fund, fund_pct,
basis, doi, dvol, vrp, skew; kernel SEs per quantile; tail spread b(0.9) - b(0.1) with a 60-day (9-week) block bootstrap (B 299);
Holm over the 40 cells.

**Economics** (H-WS08-14): sign rule sign(b_IS) x sign(x - median_IS(x)), held h, non-overlapping; net of 11 bp round trip
(taker) and 8.8 bp (pegged maker entry 2.0 + 1.3, taker exit 5.5); in sigma_h units and bp; day-block bootstrap CI; deflated
Sharpe with the cross-cell Sharpe variance and N = number of cells.

**XSECT** (H-WS08-15..19): Bybit USDT crypto perps in the lake (TradFi excluded), weekly Monday 00:00 rebalance, liquidity filter
trailing-30d median daily turnover >= 20 M USD, listing burn-in. Characteristics MOM (t-30d .. t-1d), REV (t-7d .. t), CARRY
(7 d funding sum), BETA (60 d daily beta to BTC). Fama-MacBeth on centred cross-sectional ranks of next-week vol-scaled returns
(joint and single), NW lag 4; Shanken (1992) correction for the raw-beta premium; RW over the 4 joint slopes (8-week blocks).
Tercile long-short books net of 5.5 bp x turnover; CARRY also on total return (price + funding). Trend sleeve: stress-harness
zoo_ensemble and trend_4h base alt trades (closed), trade R regressed on side-signed ranks at the entry day's 00:00,
week-clustered SEs; filter = trades whose side-signed MOM rank is in the top half.

**MACRO** (H-WS08-20..22): weekday UTC-day returns (vol-standardised) on same-day NDX log return, d DFII10, d log DTWEXBGS;
betas constant (expanding OLS), rolling 90 obs, Kalman random-walk betas (diagonal Q and R by MLE on llm_in_sample, then fixed);
one-step conditional fit from 2022-01, squared error and Gaussian log score, DM (HAC, HLN). Down-day asymmetry with HAC (lag 5) on
raw % returns; HY OAS (from 2023-11) as a robustness regressor. DVOL / skew -> realised variance: log-HAR (1, 7, 30 d) direct
h = 1, 7, + log(DVOL^2/365), + rr25, both; expanding refit every 30 days (>= 365 pairs, purged), lognormal correction, QLIKE,
DM (lag h, HLN), Tmax MCS at 90% (block bootstrap).

**Costs.** Taker 5.5 bp per side, maker 2.0 bp, pegged entry 2.0 + 1.3 bp (AGENT_RULES §6).
