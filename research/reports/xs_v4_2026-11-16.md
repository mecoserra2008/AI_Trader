# Shadow xs.v4 over all liquid pairs, and what the networks say today (block XS4), 2026-11-16

_Research agent. H-XS4 was registered as a **forward** hypothesis before any v4 data, training or live line existed. Shadow only: no orders, never read by the decision sessions (R-XS-2 / R-XS-3). xs.v1, v2.1 and xs.v3 and their configs are untouched._

## Build

**Universe.** Bybit USDT linear perps with `symbolType ""`: crypto only, which excludes the innovation zone, stocks, ETFs, commodities and forex. SPYUSDT is excluded. The floor is a 24h turnover of at least 20 M USD, checked point in time from 1h klines (bars t-24..t-1). Live, the top-of-book spread must also be at most 5 bp. Candidates for the history are perps with turnover of at least 10 M USD and spread at most 5 bp on 2026-11-16, plus universe L: 45 symbols (survivorship bias acknowledged). On average 27.8 perps pass the floor per hour (max 40). At the first live line, on Sunday night, 26 passed.

**Data.** The lake has 1h history only for the 23 universe-L perps; the other ~70 symbols start 2026-11-05. So v4 uses Bybit public REST from 2024-07-01: 1h klines, funding history and 4h OI. The cache is `data/research/xs_v4/raw` (24 MB raw). It is the lake's own source, not a new one, and the known_ts conventions are copied from the lake (bar close + 2 s, funding + 2 s, OI + 4 h 2 s). **This is flagged for the user**, since data decisions are user-led.

**Model.** The xs.v3 architecture and protocol, unchanged:
- `ml.xs_net` kan_mix (D = 8, 2 heads), v3's chosen lr 1e-3 / wd 1e-4 (no new search), 3 seeds.
- Purged expanding quarterly walk-forward with warm start and early stopping. Same top-down features (market token -> sector embedding -> token) via `xs_network_study.token_rows` / `assemble`.
- Two changes: 14 sector embeddings (config/sectors.yaml) and the liquidity mask. A token below the floor is absent for that hour from the market token, the relative basket and the targets.
- Walk-forward test quarters: 2025Q4 to 2026Q4, about 2 minutes per quarter. Panel: 19,457 hours from 2024-08-27.
- The frozen final refit uses every hour whose 24h label ends by 2026-11-15 01:00 UTC.

**Files.**
- `src/ai_trader/research/xs_v4_data.py` (REST cache, universe)
- `src/ai_trader/research/xs_v4_study.py` (panel, walk, evaluate, freeze)
- `src/ai_trader/live/xs_v4.py` (hourly shadow line, scoring, its own LATEST)
- `scripts/live_xs_v4.py` (`--once` / `--loop`)
- `config/live_xs_v4.yaml`
- `data/models/xs_v4/` (wf/, frozen/, eval_walk.json)
- Journal: `data/journal/live_xs_v4/`. Own summary: `research/live/xs_v4_LATEST.md` (not LATEST.md).

**Loop.** Started detached at 2026-11-16 ~00:40 UTC. It runs at HH:02:30 UTC. Processes: uv PID 75240, python 109232 -> 105120. First line: xs4-20261116T00, 26 tokens, about 60 s per run.

## H-XS4 forward test (registered)

Scored automatically into `data/journal/live_xs_v4/scores.jsonl`, with running means in xs_v4_LATEST.md:
- IC of v4 and of the low-vol ranking at 4h origins (00, 04, .. UTC) and 24h origins (00 UTC).
- 1h vol pinball against log-HAR-t on every line.

Evaluated once at 60 daily origins (about 2027-01-15). It supports only if, at one or more horizons, IC(v4) > 0 with t > 2 and IC(v4) - IC(low-vol) > 0 with t > 2.
