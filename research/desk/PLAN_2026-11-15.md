## 3. Setups (BTC levels from the user's chart; ETH equivalents from the geometry report)

### A. Breakdown continuation short (the user's main view: lose 63k → 58k)
- Trigger: a 15m close below 63,836, then a 15m close below 63,000.
- Confirmation (at least 2 of 3):
  - spot taker delta negative over 15m (no SPOT absorption alert);
  - Coinbase premium at or below neutral (≤ +8 bp raw);
  - perp OI rising into the break (new shorts) or long liquidations rising.
- Entry: sell limit pegged to the touch on a retest of 63,000–63,836 from below, within 60 min of the trigger. No retest means no trade; don't chase (R-EXE-1, H-BOS5).
- Stop: above 64,494. Back inside the range means the breakdown failed.
- Target: 61,950, just ahead of the 62,068 / 61,899 stop pool. Reward:risk about 2:1 or better at a retest fill near 63,500.
- Time stop: 24 h.
- Why it can work: thesis components (a), (b) and (d) are strengthening; spot isn't bidding; the analogs show the low is often still ahead (R-ANA-3, R-RB-2).
- Why it can fail: post-flush continuation shorts are about breakeven in our test (R-RB-5), and shorts are already crowded (funding p4).

### B. Reclaim breakout long (the user's view: break 66k → 68k+)
- Trigger: a 15m close above 65,904, ideally also above the 24h high at 66,133.
- Confirmation (at least 2 of 3):
  - spot taker delta positive over 15m and 60m;
  - Coinbase premium rising above neutral;
  - short liquidations or OI falling, meaning shorts are covering.
- Entry: buy limit pegged to the touch on a retest of 65,904–65,270 from above, within 60 min.
- Stop: below 65,270.
- Target: 67,376, the user's level; 68,000 (Deribit max pain) is context, not the order.
- Time stop: 24 h.
- Why it can work: crowded shorts above the box have their stops at 65,9–66,2k, and a reclaim forces covering.
- Why it can fail: the bounce so far is perp-led with spot selling, and R9 often whipsaws back.

### C. Spot-absorption reversal long at the lows (the user's view: spot buyers step in at long-term value). Exploratory: untested.
- Trigger: price sweeps 63,836 or the 62,068–61,899 zone.
- Wait out the liquidation cluster: no fade for 60 min (R-FLOW-2).
- Confirmation (all 3):
  - a SPOT absorption alert (spot delta > +150 BTC per 15m while price falls);
  - the Coinbase premium rising;
  - a 15m close back above the swept level.
- Entry: buy limit pegged to the touch after the reclaim close.
- Stop: below the sweep low.
- Target: the next level up (64,494 from a 63,836 sweep; 63,836 from a 62k sweep).
- Time stop: 8 h.
- Note: R-RB-1 says buying the next close after a flush loses. This setup differs (sweep + spot absorption + reclaim) but is not tested, so it gets the smallest risk and is reviewed after every use.

### D. Everything else: no trade.

## 4. Execution
- Entry: pegged limit at the touch (PostOnly), a 15 min window, then cancel. Never chase more than 0.5 ATR from the trigger.
- Exchange stop attached at entry (MarkPrice trigger). Target as a reduce-only order.
- Size: always the risk engine's. The trade is skipped if the minimum lot would break the risk budget.
- Management:
  - No partial take-profits and no tight trailing (R-OPX-4, R-EXIT-2).
  - Exit early only if the thesis is invalidated (the opposite setup triggers) or at the time stop, via `atx live close`.

## 5. Monitoring and process
- `scripts/level_watch.py` runs continuously: level crosses, 15m closes beyond levels, spot absorption, Coinbase premium, 15-min status. Alerts reach the orchestrator within seconds.
- A setup is proposed only when its trigger AND confirmation are logged; the orchestrator checks both against the watch log and the latest desk snapshot before `atx live propose`.
- Desk cycles: after the CME reopen (~23:15 UTC), then every 4 h. The trader logs confluence readings every cycle.
- After each trade:
  - a journal entry covering the setup, the confluence readings at entry, fill quality vs the paper model (slippage, fill time), the outcome, and what to change;
  - a weekly review before any change to this plan. Any change is a new plan version.
